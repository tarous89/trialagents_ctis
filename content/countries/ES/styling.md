# Spain document styling

Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the AEMPS formatting recommendations for the Annex VIIIA model Patient Information Sheet and Informed Consent Form (version 27 February 2025) as recorded in the Spain guides (reviewed 23 September 2026), and the `styling_instructions` of each Spanish participant template (CTIS_ES set). Where they are stricter than the base style, these rules win.

## Rules for all Spanish participant documents

- Spanish only (Part II documents addressed to participants and assessed by the CEIm).
- At most **15 pages** for the information sheet and consent form together (AEMPS recommendation), including every consent form in the document.
- **Arial or Times New Roman, 11 pt or larger; line spacing at least 1.5; normal margins** (do not reduce them).
- The personal-data appendix (Annex VIIIA Appendix 1, "Anexo VIIIA. Apéndice 1") is part of the document, **at most two pages**, with the same minimum font and spacing.
- Headings, short paragraphs and, where it helps, a simple patient-facing visit table, preferably in an annex. Never paste the protocol schedule; do not repeat routine details or the same discomfort under several sections.
- Identification block (`Info table`): study title, study code, EU trial number, sponsor, principal investigator and site. Principal investigator and site fields may stay blank for each site to complete before use.
- Running footer with document name, population, version, date, protocol number (número de protocolo) and EU trial number (número de ensayo de la UE), and page "Página x de y". Version and date are in the document and in CTIS metadata, not in the CTIS title.
- Consent form: handwritten name, signature and date fields for each signer (AEMPS model), as separate blocks: participant and investigator; parent(s) or representative; witness; adolescent. For parents, a checkbox group recording both parents sign / one parent signs and the other does not object / sole legal guardian.
- Optional choices (future data use, future sample storage, substudies, recontact, genetic findings) as independent yes/no lines, never pre-ticked.
- Blank at CTIS submission: no names, signatures or personal data of participants.
- Children's and adolescents' documents: age-adapted design (see their template rules below) within the AEMPS minimums: Arial (or Times New Roman) of at least 11 pt and at least 1.5 line spacing.

## Template-specific rules

Verbatim from each template's `styling_instructions`.

### Hoja de información y consentimiento informado para adolescentes (`L1_SIS and ICF_age 12-17_ES_es.json`)

Target 2-5 pages for the adolescent information and assent document. Use a clear 12-14 point font, generous spacing, short paragraphs, descriptive question headings and ample white space. Use colour, drawings, photographs, icons or infographics where they improve understanding, while keeping all essential information in text. Prefer one idea per paragraph and avoid dense legal text, protocol tables and unexplained abbreviations. Include a simple study calendar, a small notes area and a short glossary only when useful; define difficult words on the same page where possible. Any visit schedule must be simplified for adolescents and must not reproduce the protocol table. Audiovisual or QR-code resources may supplement but must not replace the written information.

National rule applied: the template names no font family or line spacing; use Arial or Times New Roman and at least 1.5 line spacing (AEMPS).

### Hoja de información y asentimiento para niños (`L1_SIS and ICF_age 6-11_ES_es.json`)

Use a child-friendly, uncluttered format, preferably 2-5 pages. Use Arial or another highly legible sans-serif font, preferably 12-14 point and never below the AEMPS minimum of 11 point; use at least 1.5 line spacing, generous white space and normal margins. Organise the document around short question headings. Use colours, drawings, icons, photographs or simple diagrams where they improve understanding, but ensure that every essential point is also stated in text and that visuals are neutral and non-promotional. An approved comic, video or other audiovisual aid may be linked or provided as a supplement, not as a substitute for the discussion or written information. Include a simple child-friendly activity schedule showing visits, major procedures and effects on school or daily life; do not reproduce protocol tables. Define unavoidable terms in a short glossary and provide free space for the child's questions or notes. Offer read-aloud, pictorial, augmentative or other accessible communication support when needed.

National rule applied (authority wins): the AEMPS fonts are Arial or Times New Roman; use Arial for this child document (the template's "another highly legible sans-serif font" is not used).

### Hoja de información y consentimiento diferido en situación de urgencia (`L1_SIS and ICF_emergency_ES_es.json`)

The patient information and consent document must not exceed 15 pages including all consent pathways; Appendix 1 must not exceed 2 pages. Use Arial or Times New Roman, minimum 11-point font, minimum 1.5 line spacing, and do not reduce the document margins. Use simplified, patient-friendly tables; clearly separate past emergency activities from future activities, preferably in a short two-column table. Place the study activity schedule preferably in an appendix and do not reuse protocol tables. Include only the consent pathway applicable at the time and use the participant-after-recovery form only when a representative consented first.

### Hoja de información y expresión de voluntad (`L1_SIS and ICF_limited capacity_ES_es.json`)

Adapte el diseño a las capacidades visuales, lectoras, cognitivas y comunicativas de la persona. Use Arial o Times New Roman de al menos 11 puntos, interlineado mínimo 1,5 y márgenes sin reducir, conforme a la guía AEMPS; aumente el tamaño de letra cuando sea necesario. Mantenga el documento sustancialmente más corto y sencillo que la hoja del representante. Organice la información en un máximo orientativo de 10 a 12 apartados breves, seguidos de la expresión de voluntad y de una documentación profesional concisa. Utilice párrafos cortos, títulos en forma de preguntas, espacios amplios y una secuencia lineal. Incluya la síntesis inicial solo cuando mejore realmente la accesibilidad; no debe exceder dos páginas ni repetir literalmente los apartados posteriores. Emplee imágenes, pictogramas, tarjetas de comunicación o esquemas sencillos cuando ayuden, sin sustituir la información esencial. Separe claramente las opciones de voluntad afirmativa, rechazo e incertidumbre. La imposibilidad de comunicar una voluntad clara debe figurar únicamente en la documentación del profesional. Reserve espacio suficiente para palabras, signos, marcas, fechas y firmas. No utilice un diseño que haga parecer que la persona participante presta el consentimiento jurídicamente válido del representante.

### Hoja de información y consentimiento para el seguimiento del embarazo y del bebé (`L1_SIS and ICF_pregnancy_ES_es.json`)

Keep this focused supplement substantially shorter than the main adult SIS/ICF. The complete information and consent document must not exceed 15 pages including consent forms; Appendix 1 must not exceed 2 pages. Use Arial or Times New Roman, minimum 11-point font, minimum 1.5 line spacing, and do not reduce margins. Use short patient-facing sections, simple tables, clear checkbox groups and separate signature blocks for maternal follow-up and infant follow-up. Do not reuse protocol tables.

### Hoja de información y consentimiento para el seguimiento del embarazo de la pareja (`L1_SIS and ICF_pregnant partner_ES_es.json`)

Keep the document substantially shorter than the general adult trial information sheet and limited to pregnancy follow-up. The information and consent document must not exceed the AEMPS ceiling of 15 pages including consent forms; Appendix 1 must not exceed 2 pages. Use Arial or Times New Roman, minimum 11-point font, minimum 1.5 line spacing, and do not reduce margins. Use simple tables, short paragraphs, clear checkboxes and distinct signature blocks for the pregnant partner and post-birth child-data consent.

### Información y consentimiento para el uso futuro de datos y muestras (`L1_SIS and ICF_secondary_ES_es.json`)

Keep the document materially shorter than the main trial information sheet and within the AEMPS maximum of 15 pages including consent; Appendix 1 must not exceed 2 pages. Use Arial or Times New Roman, minimum 11-point font, minimum 1.5 line spacing, and do not reduce the margins. Make the optional and separate nature prominent. Use short patient-facing headings, one simple lifecycle table, clearly separated yes/no choices and signature fields. Remove all drafting instructions and unused alternatives before issue.

### Hoja de información al paciente y consentimiento informado ante testigo imparcial (`L1_SIS and ICF_witness_ES_es.json`)

The patient information and consent document must not exceed 15 pages including the witnessed consent forms; Appendix 1 must not exceed 2 pages. Use Arial or Times New Roman, minimum 11-point font, minimum 1.5 line spacing, and do not reduce the document margins. Use simplified, patient-friendly tables; place the study activity schedule preferably in an appendix and do not reuse protocol tables. Present the witnessed consent as a clear signature section, with separate fields for the participant when able, the impartial witness, and the investigator or authorised site staff member, plus a field for any approved alternative consent-recording method.
