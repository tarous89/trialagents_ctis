# Denmark: decisions and gaps

Answer these together. Each item gives the question, the options and a recommendation. Until then, the package works with the recommended defaults. Sources: the Denmark guides (`/ctis/denmark/en/`, reviewed 23 September 2026), the CTIS_DK template set and the naming guide.

## Questions for the user

- **D1 Participant information and consent form: one upload or two?** The Denmark checklist lists the participant information sheet (item 3) and the informed consent form (item 4) as separate items, and VMK's CTR consent forms are stand-alone forms. The supplied templates and the naming-guide example combine them. Options:
  - (a) two uploads per population, `L1_SIS_<population>_DK_da` and `L1_ICF_<population>_DK_da`, drafted from the same template with the same version and date;
  - (b) one combined `L1_SIS and ICF_<population>_DK_da`.

  Recommendation: (a). It keeps the VMK standard consent text recognisable (D2) and matches the checklist.
- **D2 VMK CTR standard consent forms.** VMK publishes standard forms for competent persons, holders of parental authority, 15-17-year-olds with parents, and nearest relatives with a trial guardian. It also publishes a parental-authority power of attorney and a right-not-to-know addendum. VMK encourages their use; if the standard text fields are unchanged and the sponsor confirms that, the form is accepted without comments on those fields. None of these forms was supplied. Options:
  - (a) you supply the current VMK forms. We compare them with the templates' declaration sections, bind any that differ in place of those sections, and confirm in the application that the standard fields are unchanged;
  - (b) use the templates' declarations as they are.

  Recommendation: (a). Use (b) until then, and keep the VMK-style wording unchanged.
- **D3 K1 when the protocol already contains everything.** K1 is mandatory "when the information is not fully contained in the protocol". Options: (a) always submit K1 on template v2.0, with references to protocol sections; (b) omit it when the protocol covers every point. Recommendation: (a). The Danish additions (consent physician, record screening, representatives) rarely sit fully in a global protocol.
- **D4 Attestation by a later delegated consent physician.** The templates allow only the same physician to receive consent. The September 2026 VMK guidance also allows a later qualified, delegated physician after a reflection period, with a separate attestation (templates/README.md, conflict 1). Options:
  - (a) when the protocol or K1 uses that route, add a separate attestation block to the ICF and adjust the information sentence, marked as a deviation;
  - (b) always keep the same-physician wording.

  Recommendation: (a), only where the route is planned. Wording from the VMK form once it is supplied (D2).
- **D5 Children's band and children under 5.** The children's template is named `age 6-11`, but its text says 5-11. VMK sets no band below 15. Options: (a) keep the file name and use 5-11 as the drafting band; (b) rename the file `age 5-11`. For children under 5: (c) oral, adapted information only, documented in the consent procedure; (d) a short picture-based sheet built from the 5-11 template. Recommendation: (a) and (c), plus (d) when the child can understand written information, as the guide requires written information "where the child is capable of understanding it".
- **D6 15-17-year-olds: VMK combined form or separate declarations?** VMK publishes one CTR form for the 15-17-year-old together with the holders of parental authority. The templates keep the young person's declaration (12-17 template, route B) separate from the parents' declaration (parents template). Options: (a) the VMK combined form, once supplied; (b) the two template declarations. Recommendation: (a) when supplied, (b) until then. Either way, both consents are needed for the 15-17 pathway.
- **D7 Who informs children aged 5-14.** The templates allow a person with knowledge of the trial and pedagogical skills to inform the child. The minors guide applies the Danish medical-doctor requirement to the information process. Options: (a) the Danish-authorised physician conducts it, and pedagogically skilled staff may support; (b) a non-physician informs the child alone. Recommendation: (a), the guide's rule.
- **D8 Patienterstatningen statement.** If Patienterstatningen covers the Danish sites, no policy is uploaded, but the application must say so. Options:
  - (a) a short Danish O1 statement, drafted by TrialAgents from the sponsor's confirmation, title `O1_Patienterstatningen coverage statement_DK_da`;
  - (b) the sentence in another document (K1 or the cover letter) and nothing in O1.

  Recommendation: (a). It is easy to find in the CTIS insurance slot, and it records nothing that the sponsor has not confirmed.
- **D9 Trial budget and CTA (P2).** VMK accepts a draft or final budget, and optionally a draft CTA. The naming guide has no P2 example. Options: (a) `P2_Financial arrangements_<trial budget or site>_DK_da`; (b) the budget attached to P1. Recommendation: (a), with a note when the budget is a draft.
- **D10 Fee document.** The fee is normally invoiced after submission and paid within one month. There is no CTCG title. Options: (a) no initial upload; upload `Proof of payment of fee_DK_da` only if the invoice process asks for it, in the CTIS area the authority instructs; (b) always upload. Recommendation: (a).
- **D11 R1 national sentence.** VMK publishes a standard English sentence stating compliance with the GDPR and the Danish Data Protection Act. It was not supplied, and we do not reproduce it from memory. Options: (a) you supply it and it is used verbatim (the title then ends `_DK_en` if the whole statement is English); (b) a Danish sentence with the same content until then. Recommendation: (a). Use (b) only as a placeholder flagged in the Check step.
- **D12 Data-only follow-up of a pregnant partner.** VMK says that this is usually outside its Part II approval and recommends omitting it from Part II. Options: (a) omit it and handle it under the data-protection framework; (b) upload it with the three statements VMK requires (voluntary; GDPR and Danish Data Protection Act; not approved by VMK). Recommendation: (a).
- **D13 Extra samples collected for unspecified future research.** This is outside VMK's Part II approval, and VMK recommends omitting it. Options: (a) omit it; (b) upload it with the statement that it is not approved by VMK. Recommendation: (a).
- **D14 "Dine rettigheder som forsøgsperson i forsøg med medicin".** The templates tell you to enclose this VMK leaflet. It is not an item on the Part II checklist and was not supplied. Options: (a) refer to it and hand it out, with no CTIS upload; (b) upload it with the L1 set. Recommendation: (a), unless VMK asks for it. Supply the current version for the reference.
- **D15 Witness route beyond capable adult patients.** The guide also allows the witness route for a legally designated representative who cannot write. The template covers capable adult patients only. Options: (a) add the witness and alternative-method blocks from the witness template to the representative or healthy-volunteer ICF when that case is foreseeable, marked as a deviation; (b) supply further templates. Recommendation: (a).
- **D16 Risk frequency categories.** The Denmark guides set none. Options: (a) the IB or SmPC categories for each product; (b) a fixed scale chosen by the sponsor. Recommendation: (a), recorded as a source.
- **D17 N1 issuer and signature.** The guide says to use the EC form's own signature requirements. The shared form has "Issued by" fields (name, position, organisation, date) and states that the CTR does not require signing. Options: (a) the head of clinic or institution completes the issuer fields, with no extra wet-ink signature; (b) signed by the issuer. Recommendation: (a), unless the site's institutional process requires (b).
- **D18 Right-not-to-know form for other consent givers.** Only the main, healthy and witness templates contain the separate right-not-to-know declaration. Options: (a) adapt that declaration for parents, legal representatives, 15-17-year-olds and the emergency route, marked as a deviation; (b) use the VMK addendum once supplied (D2). Recommendation: (b), with (a) as the interim.
- **D19 Translations for participants who do not understand Danish.** Options: (a) one translated PIS and ICF per language and population, titled with that language code, with the validation evidence recorded as a source; (b) translate only on demand after authorisation. Recommendation: (a) for every language group the protocol foresees. Changes after authorisation need the modification route.
- **D20 Language of administrative documents.** Danish or English is allowed. Options: (a) Danish for everything the assistant drafts (K1, N1, P1, R1, S1), and the investigator's choice for M1 and M2; (b) English for the administrative forms. Recommendation: (a). Do not upload both language versions of the same document.
- **D21 Documents for minors after emergency inclusion.** The emergency template covers adults: the participant, a guardian, or the nearest relative plus a trial guardian. Options: (a) adapt the parents template and the 12-17 template (route B) to subsequent consent to continued participation, marked as deviations; (b) supply dedicated templates. Recommendation: (a) now, (b) for the long term.

## Missing

Documents or texts the guides require or recommend that have no template in CTIS_DK:

- **VMK CTR standard consent forms**, the **parental-authority power-of-attorney form** and the **right-not-to-know addendum** (D2, D18). The templates' own sections are used until they are supplied.
- **VMK standard English sentence** for the R1 statement on the GDPR and the Danish Data Protection Act (D11).
- **Patienterstatningen coverage statement**, or independent insurance documentation for sites outside the scheme (O1; D8). Supplied or confirmed by the sponsor or insurer.
- **Trial budget** (draft or final) and optional **draft CTA** (P2; D9). Supplied by the sponsor.
- **Proof of fee payment**, only if the invoice process asks for it (D10).
- **K2 recruitment material**, including scene-by-scene storyboards for audio or video. Free-form, drafted from the protocol.
- **Information for children under 5** (D5).
- **Subsequent-consent documents for minors after emergency inclusion** (D21).
- **Witness blocks for a representative unable to write** (D15).
- **"Dine rettigheder som forsøgsperson i forsøg med medicin"** leaflet (D14).
- **Validated translations** for participants who do not understand Danish (D19).

Other gaps (not in the Denmark guides; not filled from memory):

- The Danish legal basis for disclosing patient-record information for screening. K1 must state it, but the guides do not name it. The sponsor provides it.
- No insurance minimums and no Patienterstatningen procedure details beyond coverage of the Danish sites.
- No inconvenience-compensation amounts, tax rules or caps for healthy volunteers. VMK's compensation guidance is cited, but its text is not in the guides.
- No fixed number of hours for subsequent consent after emergency inclusion. The sponsor gives an estimate per participant group.
- No risk frequency categories (D16).
- The written delegation agreement between the PI and a delegated physician is a site-file document. It must be in place, but no upload is described.
- The Danish-language guides (`/ctis/denmark/dk`) were not read. They are outside the `/ctis/denmark/en/` scope.
- The variable sheet is named `_DK_de`, but its content targets Danish.
