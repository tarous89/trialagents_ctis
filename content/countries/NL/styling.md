# Netherlands document styling

Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the Netherlands guides (reviewed 23 September 2026), which record the CCMO IVO model version 1.1 (14 August 2026) rules, and the `styling_instructions` of each Dutch participant template (CTIS_NL set, `NL_Participant-facing_templates_05082026.txt`). Where they are stricter than the base style, these rules win.

## Rules for all Dutch participant documents

- Dutch only; B1 language level; short sentences, medical terms explained.
- Layered structure (IVO): a cover page with the main decision points (what participation involves, why the study is done, main risks, time, payment, insurance and withdrawal), then the research-specific explanation, then the appendices (contact details, more information on the study and risks, data protection, insurance, consent form) and the reference to the general CCMO information for research participants.
- The cover page is at most two pages (`Summary section` where the template uses one); the complete IVO, including consent form and appendices, is at most 4,000 words unless the cover letter gives a reason.
- Accessible sans-serif font listed in the official model (the templates name Arial, Calibri, Open Sans or Atkinson Hyperlegible), at no less than 12 pt or 16 px. This overrides the base style's 11 pt body text and 9 pt small text.
- Headings are questions or statements a participant can understand; contact details are easy to find.
- Tables only where they simplify schedules or choices; a short schedule may clarify repeated visits and procedures.
- Consent choices: separate Yes/No lines for each optional item, visually distinct, never pre-ticked and impossible to skip by mistake; the core consent to join is clearly identifiable.
- Keep the questions, tick boxes and completion fields that the model treats as part of the consent discussion (including the spaces to write down questions).
- Delete all instructions, unused options, irrelevant sections and excess white space before submission.
- Page numbers, appendix references and internal cross-references are correct and consistent.
- Running footer (base style) in Dutch, with page "pagina x van y"; the templates' own footer lines (document name, population, version, date, protocol number, EU trial number, study code) are used.
- Signature section: blank, separate blocks per signer with date fields (participant; both parents or guardian; legal representative with relationship; impartial witness attestation; researcher or authorised interviewer).
- The CTIS filename identifies the population and the Dutch language.

## Template-specific rules

Verbatim from each template's `styling_instructions`.

### Informatie Formulier 12-16 jaar (`L1_SIS and ICF_age 12-17_NL_nl.json`)

Use prominent blue-shaded module headings, bold subsection headings, grey-highlighted drafting placeholders, and simple study-relevant images or pictograms where helpful. Include a bordered question-writing box, tables for procedure images and data categories, and place the consent form in a separate appendix on a new page.

### Informatie over onderzoek voor kinderen tot 12 jaar (`L1_SIS and ICF_age 6-11_NL_nl.json`)

Use a large bold document title, blue-shaded bold module headings, bold emphasis on key phrases, child-friendly medical illustrations, bullet lists, and a bordered writing area for questions.

### Informatie na noodinsluiting en formulier voor uitgestelde toestemming (`L1_SIS and ICF_emergency_NL_nl.json`)

Gebruik Arial, Calibri, Open Sans of Atkinson Hyperlegible met een minimale lettergrootte van 12 pt of 16 px. Gebruik een gelaagde opbouw, duidelijke sectietitels, deelnemersvragen met selectievakjes en voldoende schrijfruimte. Verwijder vóór afronding alle instructietekst, niet-gekozen alternatieven, niet-toepasselijke secties en overmatige witruimte. Houd paginanummering, bijlagen en interne verwijzingen consistent.
