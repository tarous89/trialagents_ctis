# Norway: decisions and gaps

Answer these together. Each item gives the question, the options and a recommendation. Until then, the package uses the recommended defaults. Sources: the Norway guides (`/ctis/norway/en/`, reviewed 23 September 2026), the CTIS_NO template set and the naming guide.

## Questions for the user

- **D1 Written standard and language code.** The guides only say "Norwegian". No guide requires a Nynorsk or Sami version. All supplied templates are Bokmål and use the code `no` (`_NO_no`). Options:
  - (a) Bokmål only, titled `_NO_no`;
  - (b) Bokmål titled `_NO_nb`;
  - (c) also prepare Nynorsk or Sami versions.

  Recommendation: (a). A Nynorsk or Sami version is planned only when a site expects participants who need it. It would be a sponsor-checked version of the approved Norwegian text, like any other participant-language version (D16). Confirm with REK KULMU whether a Nynorsk version must itself be submitted.
- **D2 Legal representative wording.** The legal-representative, emergency and right-to-object templates name the nearest relative ("nærmeste pårørende") as the person who consents. The guide says to identify the legally designated representative under Norwegian law and not to assume that the nearest relative has that authority. Options:
  - (a) keep "nærmeste pårørende" only where the site has confirmed that this person is the legally designated representative. Record the basis in the template's "grunnlag" field and describe the verification in K1;
  - (b) change the wording to a neutral "legally designated representative", marked as a deviation.

  Recommendation: (a). The guides do not name the Norwegian legal basis, so none is added.
- **D3 Privacy information in the participant documents.** The templates leave out GDPR rights and further privacy information ("du vil få mer informasjon"). The guide and QC section 5 require controllers and their contacts, recipients, transfers, participant rights and a privacy contact. Options:
  - (a) add a short privacy subsection to each L1, without the legal basis, marked as a deviation;
  - (b) a separate participant privacy notice handed out with L1 and referenced in it. It is not a Part II checklist item, so its upload slot would need confirming.

  Recommendation: (a). Use (b) as well if the sponsor already has a notice.
- **D4 Title of the right-to-object letter** (template `withdrawal notice`). Options:
  - (a) `L1_SIS and ICF_right to object emergency_<population>_NO_no`, with `_participant` or `_representative` when both get a letter;
  - (b) keep the template name: `L1_SIS and ICF_withdrawal notice_<population>_NO_no`;
  - (c) file it under L2 as other subject information.

  Recommendation: (a). The document is a right-to-object notice, not a withdrawal form, and the checklist puts emergency documents in the L1 category.
- **D5 Deletion wording in the right-to-object letter.** The REK text promises deletion after a late objection, unless the data are already analysed. The guide says not to promise automatic destruction, and to distinguish safety reporting and legally required retention. Options:
  - (a) keep the REK text and add one sentence on the data that must be kept for safety reporting or by law, only where the protocol or sponsor confirms such retention, marked as a deviation;
  - (b) use the text as supplied.

  Recommendation: (a).
- **D6 REK number in the right-to-object letter.** Options:
  - (a) EU CT number only, with the "REK-nummer" row removed;
  - (b) both, if REK KULMU gives a reference.

  Recommendation: (a), unless REK KULMU issues a reference.
- **D7 Documents for minors after emergency enrolment.** The emergency template covers adults only. Options:
  - (a) adapt the parents template and the age-band templates with the emergency sections ("what has already happened" and "what happens if you consent"), marked as deviations;
  - (b) supply dedicated templates.

  Recommendation: (a) now, (b) for the long term.
- **D8 Witness for a representative who cannot read or write.** The witness template covers capable adults only. Options:
  - (a) add the witness block, including the role and relationship fields the guide requires, to the representative or guardian document when this case is foreseeable;
  - (b) supply a template.

  Recommendation: (a).
- **D9 Consent at 18.** The guides set own consent at 16 (16-17 template). They do not say whether a new consent is needed at 18 or what the age of legal competence is for CTR Q&A 9.4 (K1 `v113`). Options:
  - (a) no new consent at 18;
  - (b) re-consent at 18 with the main or healthy template.

  Recommendation: confirm with REK KULMU. Plan (b) when participants can turn 18 during the trial, because it is a short step with an existing template.
- **D10 Children under 12.** The file is named `age 6-11`, but the authority's band is "under 12". Options for the CTIS subtype: (a) "children under 12"; (b) the protocol's own age range. For a broad age range: (c) one under-12 leaflet; (d) separate versions, for example for younger and older children, as the guide allows.

  Recommendation: (a), plus (d) when the protocol's under-12 range is broad.
- **D11 Additional trial-specific insurance.** The checklist asks the sponsor to state whether it has additional insurance beyond the LAF certificate. The guides name no document for this. Options:
  - (a) a short English sponsor statement in the O category next to the LAF certificate (`O2_Proof of coverage_<sponsor>`), with the extra policy where one exists;
  - (b) a sentence in the cover letter (Part I).

  Recommendation: (a).
- **D12 Financing and site arrangements (P2).** No template exists, and the naming guide has no P2 example. Options for who writes it: (a) the sponsor supplies it (current setting); (b) TrialAgents drafts the short description from figures the sponsor has confirmed. Options for the title: (c) `P2_Financial arrangements_NO`; (d) fold it into P1, whose CTCG title already says "and financial arrangements".

  Recommendation: (a) or (b) as you prefer, and (c). The checklist treats compensation and financing as two items in two CTIS categories.
- **D13 Project organisation and enrolment by site.** Options:
  - (a) state them in each N1 (`v125`);
  - (b) attach a separate enrolment plan.

  Recommendation: (a). Use (b) only if site numbers are not stated elsewhere.
- **D14 Risk frequency categories.** The Norway guides set none. Options:
  - (a) the IB or SmPC categories for each product;
  - (b) a fixed scale chosen by the sponsor.

  Recommendation: (a), recorded as a source.
- **D15 Assent signature for ages 12-15 (and under 12).** In the templates the child's signature is optional. Options:
  - (a) keep the signature section by default;
  - (b) omit it and document assent in the consent-confirmation block of the guardian document.

  Recommendation: (a) for 12-15, because the guide requires documented assent. Use it for under-12 when age and maturity allow.
- **D16 Participants who do not understand Norwegian.** REK KULMU neither receives nor assesses translations. Options:
  - (a) the sponsor has a certified translation made of the approved Norwegian text when needed, records it as a source and does not upload it;
  - (b) upload the translations.

  Recommendation: (a).
- **D17 National sentence in R1.** The guides require only the GDPR statement, with its issuer and the party it is issued for. Options:
  - (a) no national sentence (`r1_the_sponsor_declares` not applicable);
  - (b) add a sentence on Norwegian data-protection law.

  Recommendation: (a). The guides give no wording for (b).

## Missing

Documents or texts the guides require that have no template in CTIS_NO:

- **K2 recruitment material.** Free-form; drafted from the protocol and K1.
- **Information and consent for minors after emergency enrolment** (D7).
- **Witness blocks for a representative or guardian who cannot read or write** (D8).
- **Separate under-12 versions for a broad age range** (D10), if needed.
- **REK-approved consent of a Norwegian general biobank.** Needed when samples go to such a biobank. Supplied by the biobank.
- **Insurance certificate from the Norwegian Medicines Liability Association** and documentation of any additional trial-specific insurance (O1; D11). Supplied by LAF or the sponsor.
- **Financing and site-arrangements description** (P2; D12). Supplied by the sponsor.
- **CV and declaration-of-interest content**, including evidence of current ICH GCP E6(R3) competence (M1, M2). Supplied by the investigator.
- **Participant privacy notice**, only if D3 (b) is chosen.
- **Outside Part II:** the Norwegian protocol synopsis (Part I) and proof of fee payment (Part I in Norway). Neither needs a template.

Other gaps (not in the Norway guides; not filled from memory):

- **REK KULMU originals.** The original templates were not supplied: the national CTR consent template (3 June 2026), the under-12 and 12-15 templates and the Article 35(3) reservation-right form (24 March 2026), and the future-research templates. The supplied templates could not be compared with them word for word.
- **Legally designated representative.** The guides do not name the Norwegian law that designates the representative for an incapacitated adult (D2), or the age of legal competence (D9).
- **Health-record screening.** The guides do not name the legal basis for health-record screening or the exemption from confidentiality. K1 must describe them, so the sponsor or site provides them.
- **Insurance.** There is no insurance minimum, and there is no wording for the LAF or NPE scheme beyond what the templates carry.
- **Payments.** There are no compensation amounts, caps or tax rules.
- **Risk frequencies.** There is no risk frequency scale (D14).
- **Endpoint documents.** Participant-facing endpoint documents (questionnaires, diaries) are not covered by the Norway guides. The Part II checklist has no R2 or L2 item for them.
- **Norwegian-language guides.** The Norwegian-language guides (`/ctis/norway/no`) were not read. They are outside the `/ctis/norway/en/` scope.
- **Variable sheet name.** The variable sheet is named `_NO_de`, but its content targets Norwegian, and the guide requires English answers.
