# Portugal: decisions and gaps

Answer these together. Each line: question; options; recommendation.

## Questions for the user

- **D1 Form answer language.** In which language are the Part II forms (K1, N1, P1, R1, S1) answered? Options: (a) Portuguese; (b) English. Recommendation: (a). The guides accept either for dossier documents, and the CTIS_PT variable sheet targets Portuguese. `requirements.json` is set to Portuguese.
- **D2 Data-protection statement.** Where does the GDPR statement go, and does it name Portuguese law? Options: (a) the EudraLex statement in the CTIS Form section only, with a sentence on Law No 58/2019 added; (b) the plain EudraLex statement in the Form section only; (c) also upload R1 under Part II. Recommendation: (a). Portugal accepts the Form-section statement and needs no duplicate, and the added sentence matches the participant information, which says the sponsor declared compliance with the GDPR and Law No 58/2019.
- **D3 Endpoint questionnaires and diaries.** Where do participant-facing endpoint documents go? Options: (a) under L as `L2_Other subject information material_<type>_PT_pt`, as the Portugal checklist says; (b) under R as `R2_Patient-facing document_<type>_PT_pt`, the naming-guide pattern. Recommendation: (a). The English sources go with the protocol in Part I either way.
- **D4 N-section structure.** How are the service and research-team authorisation and the pharmaceutical-services declaration with the IMP circuit uploaded? Options: (a) as separate documents per site (`N2_Service and research team authorisation_<site>`, `N3_Pharmaceutical services declaration and IMP circuit_<site>`); (b) combined with N1 into one site declaration. Recommendation: (a), because their signatories differ. The guides name no titles.
- **D5 N1 signatory.** Who signs the site-suitability declaration? Options: (a) the Director of Service, who also signs the service authorisation; (b) another institutional signatory named by the site. Recommendation: (a), which is what the CEIC checklist expects. Either way, the assistant leaves the signature block empty.
- **D6 GCP certificate.** Options: (a) upload `M2_GCP training_<investigator>_<site>` whenever the CV does not clearly document current GCP training; (b) rely on the CV only; (c) always upload a certificate. Recommendation: (a). The guide makes the certificate conditional, and the title follows the Austria and Germany pattern.
- **D7 Written assent at 16.** A participant who turns 16 during the trial must give written assent. Options: (a) build a short stand-alone form from the assent section of the 12–17 template (`L1_SIS and ICF_written assent age 16_<population>_PT_pt`); (b) supply a dedicated template. Recommendation: (a) now, (b) later.
- **D8 Children under 6.** Options: (a) a short information sheet, where meaningful, with no template; (b) oral information only, documented with the representative's consent. Recommendation: decide per study. The guide asks for suitable child information "when meaningful".
- **D9 Short emergency information sheet.** The guide recommends a short approved sheet for when full discussion is temporarily impossible. Options: (a) draft one (no template); (b) omit it. Recommendation: (a) when the emergency setting makes it usable. It never replaces subsequent consent.
- **D10 Witness for a representative or a healthy volunteer.** The witness template covers a capable adult patient only. Options: (a) adapt it, with visible deviations, for a representative who cannot write or for a healthy volunteer; (b) add a witness section to the representative or healthy-volunteer document; (c) supply dedicated templates. Recommendation: (b), which the limited-capacity guide allows ("an impartial-witness section when needed") and which keeps one form per role.
- **D11 Signature rules the guides do not state.** These cover P1, R1, S1, the pharmaceutical-services declaration, the O2 evidence and the coordinator and external-facility documents. Options: (a) no signature beyond the form's own sponsor fields (R1 name, role and date), with institutional documents signed as issued; (b) a sponsor signature on P1, R1 and S1. Recommendation: (a), and confirm against the CEIC checklist v2.0.
- **D12 Pregnant partner wording.** Options: (a) describe the partner as taking part only in the follow-up activity, without receiving the investigational medicine, which follows the guide; (b) keep the template sentence that she is not a trial participant. Recommendation: (a). The guide says she may be a research participant for the follow-up activity.
- **D13 Low-intervention insurance.** Options: (a) a trial-specific policy (O1); (b) ask CEIC to dispense with it on evidence of existing civil-liability cover (O2). Recommendation: (a), unless the sponsor holds such evidence and accepts the risk of CEIC refusal. Low intervention alone does not remove the requirement.
- **D14 P1 when no payment is made.** P1 is conditional in Portugal. Options: (a) omit P1 when participants receive neither reimbursement nor compensation, and state the free supply of medicines and procedures in the PIS and P2; (b) always submit P1 stating "no compensation". Recommendation: (a). The guide sets P1 only when reimbursement or compensation is offered.
- **D15 Optional future use: separate document or module.** Options: (a) a separate future-use PIS and ICF from the supplied template; (b) a clearly separated optional section in the main form. Recommendation: (a). The guide accepts both, Law No 12/2005 asks for separate consent for biological material, and the template set is built for (a).

## Missing

Documents the guides require that have no template in the supplied set:

- **K2 recruitment material and L2 other participant-facing material** (cards, instructions, visit schedules, diaries, questionnaires, guides, brochures, appointment information). Free-form, drafted from the protocol.
- **Written age-16 assent form.** Provisionally built from the 12–17 template (D7).
- **Subsequent-consent material that the guides mention but no template covers:** information for children under 6 (D8), the short emergency information sheet (D9), communication aids and easy-read or accessible formats, and the witness section for a representative (D10).
- **M: GCP certificate** (D6). Supplied by the investigator or training provider.
- **N: service and research-team authorisation, pharmaceutical-services declaration and IMP circuit, external study coordinator evidence, external-facility documentation** (D4). Supplied by the site; the guides name no CEIC templates for them.
- **O1 insurance certificate and O2 evidence for a low-intervention dispensation.** Supplied by the insurer or sponsor.
- **P2 financial arrangements per site.** Contract, agreed model or minimum-information document from the sponsor and site.
- **Fee (Q).** Nothing to upload; the electronic process follows submission.
- **Portuguese protocol synopsis (Part I) and the Portuguese lay summary of results.** Outside Part II.

Other gaps:

- **CEIC Part II checklist v2.0 (1 August 2022).** Its exact items are not in the guides: the national site-detail list, the evidence required for an external coordinator, and who signs which declaration.
- **CEIC financial-arrangements guidance (24 August 2022).** The list of mandatory minimum information for P2 is not reproduced.
- **CEIC adult ICF model (January 2026) and consent-content checklist (23 December 2020).** Not supplied, so the templates' CEIC basis could not be compared with them.
- **Fee amount and exemption criteria.** Not stated in the guides.
- **Signature rules** for P1, R1, S1, the pharmacy declaration and the O2 evidence (D11).
- **CTIS titles** for the N documents and the GCP certificate (D4, D6).
- **Withdrawal-options document** referenced in the main and parents templates (section E, "Opções de Saída do Participante do Ensaio Clínico e Declaração de Retirada de Consentimento"). No template was supplied, and the guides do not mention it.
- **Planned non-Portuguese-speaking groups.** The guides give only the exceptional CEIC waiver, with no procedure for translations or interpreters.
