# Ireland: decisions and gaps

Answer these together. Each line gives the question, the options and a recommendation. Sources: the Ireland guides (reviewed 23 September 2026), the CTIS_IE template set and the naming guide. Until you answer, the package uses the recommendations.

## Questions for the user

- **D1 NREC-CT Site Suitability Form.** Can you supply the current NREC-CT Site Suitability Template (version 2, DOCX)? Options: (a) supply it; we convert it into `countries/IE/templates/part-ii` as a form where the assistant fills the trial fields and the site completes the rest and the authorisation field; (b) leave N1 as a document the site prepares entirely. Recommendation: (a). The generic EU form in CTIS_IE is not accepted on its own, so N1 has no template today.
- **D2 Data-protection route for R1.** Which document goes in R1? Options: (a) NREC Statement of Compliance regarding Data Protection v2: supply the DOCX and its guidance, and we convert it into a form; (b) a sponsor DPIA instead, checked only, with the title `R1_Compliance collection and use personal data_DPIA_IE`. Recommendation: (a), unless the sponsor already has a trial DPIA that covers every point the guide lists. The EU GDPR statement does not replace either.
- **D3 Capable participants aged 16-17.** Which template do they use? Options: (a) the supplied 16-17 template (own consent, slightly simpler language); (b) the main adult template. Recommendation: (a). It is the template written for this group, and it closely follows the adult master.
- **D4 Insurance or indemnity slot.** Where does indemnity evidence go? Options: (a) insurance certificate as `O1_Trial participant insurance certificate_IE`, and State Claims Agency or other indemnity evidence (including the Clinical Trial Indemnity Form) as `O2_Proof of coverage_<sponsor or site>`; (b) everything under O1. Recommendation: (a). It follows the naming guide's two O categories. The Ireland guide treats the evidence as one item and names no slot.
- **D5 Other participant-facing materials.** Which slot do they use? Options: (a) endpoint-related questionnaires and diaries as `R2_Patient-facing document_<type>_IE_en`, everything else as `L2_Other subject information material_<type>_IE_en`; (b) everything under L2. A related question is whether the English endpoint documents filed in Part I (`D4_Patient-facing document_<type>_ENG`) must also be uploaded in Part II. Recommendation: (a). Also upload the final English versions in Part II, because the Ireland checklist (item 4) asks for them and gives no exemption.
- **D6 Other financial arrangements (P2).** Who prepares it? Options: (a) the assistant drafts a short free-form statement from sponsor information (financing, payments to investigators and sites, other agreements), and it is checked; (b) the sponsor's finance or legal team supplies it, and it is checked only (current setting). Recommendation: (a). The guide asks for a description rather than a contract. The kind would change to a drafted document once you confirm.
- **D7 Healthy volunteers aged 16-17.** No template combines own consent at 16-17 with healthy-volunteer content. Options: (a) adapt the 16-17 template with the healthy-volunteer content of the healthy template, with deviations visible; (b) adapt the healthy template to 16-17 language. Recommendation: (a), so the age-appropriate consent wording is kept.
- **D8 Assent bands and children under 6.** The law sets no fixed bands. Options for bands: (a) keep 6-11 and 12-15 as default drafting bands, justified per protocol; (b) set bands per study. Options for under-6s: (a) a short document drafted from the 6-11 template when the protocol enrols children under 6 who can form an opinion; (b) oral information only, documented in K1. Recommendation: bands (a), adjusted when the cohort is narrow; under-6s (a) when such children are enrolled, after you confirm.
- **D9 Witness for a legally designated representative.** The Ireland guide allows the witness route when the representative cannot read or write. The witness template excludes representative language. Options: (a) add a witness block, with the witness fields from the guide, to the legal-rep document for that case; (b) supply a dedicated template. Recommendation: (a).
- **D10 Representative documents after emergency enrolment.** Options: (a) a separate representative document (`L1_SIS and ICF_legal representative emergency_<population>_IE_en`) adapted from the legal-rep template to the deferred-consent context: what has already happened, continued procedures, the right to refuse and data already collected; (b) only the emergency template's conditional representative signature block. Recommendation: (a). The guide lists separate representative information and consent documents.
- **D11 Short immediate information sheet for emergency enrolment.** The guide recommends one "where useful and feasible". Options: (a) draft one when the protocol allows a brief explanation at enrolment; (b) none. Recommendation: (a), only when the protocol foresees such an explanation.
- **D12 Optional genetics and optional substudies.** Options: (a) a clearly distinct optional section with its own Yes/No choice and confirmation in the population's main ICF (the templates' design); (b) a separate L1 document (`L1_SIS and ICF_optional genetics_<population>_IE_en`, `L1_SIS and ICF_<substudy>_<population>_IE_en`) built from the main template. Recommendation: (a) by default. Use (b) when the option has its own procedures, risks or information that change the consent logic. The guide accepts "separate or clearly distinct versions".
- **D13 Separate screening consent for healthy volunteers.** Options: (a) when screening is consented separately, a screening SIS/ICF adapted from the healthy template (`L1_SIS and ICF_screening_<population>_IE_en`); (b) screening covered in the main healthy-volunteer document only. Recommendation: (a) when the protocol has a separate screening consent, as the guide requires; otherwise (b).
- **D14 Fee evidence.** It is listed on the Part II checklist page but sits outside Part II. Options: (a) keep it in the plan as a Forms-section item that is checked only, for commercial sponsors; (b) leave it out of the plan. Recommendation: (a). The sponsor's commercial status is recorded as the study fact `sponsor.commercial`.
- **D15 Health Research Consent Declaration.** When adults lacking capacity, emergency enrolment outside the deferred-consent exemption, or secondary use for such adults apply, how is the HRCDC route planned? Options: (a) record it in the plan as an outside-Part-II prerequisite with an owner and date (`outside_part_ii` IE-HRCDC); (b) leave it to the sponsor without tracking. Recommendation: (a). The guides say it must be obtained or clearly planned before the processing that needs it.
- **D16 Shared pregnant-partner and secondary-use documents.** Options: (a) one shared document across populations when nothing differs, with separate versions per signer pathway (participant, parent or guardian, legally designated representative) for secondary use; (b) one per population. Recommendation: (a).
- **D17 Risk frequency categories.** The Ireland guides require risk "using words and numbers where useful" but name no categories. Options: (a) use the frequency categories of the IB or SmPC for each product, recorded as a source; (b) define one fixed scale for all Irish documents. Recommendation: (a).
- **D18 Irish language (ga).** The guides require English for every document and say nothing about Irish-language versions. Options: (a) English only, with an Irish or other translation only as an approved individual translation when a participant needs it; (b) plan an Irish-language package. Recommendation: (a). No Irish-language version is required, and the guides warn against implying that an Irish-language CTIS guide exists.

## Missing

Documents the guides require that have no template in CTIS_IE:

- **N1 NREC-CT Site Suitability Form (version 2).** Mandatory, one per site. The national DOCX was not supplied, and the EU form is not accepted on its own (D1).
- **R1 NREC Statement of Compliance regarding Data Protection (version 2) and the National Office's Guidance on the Statement of Compliance.** Mandatory, unless a DPIA is provided. Neither was supplied, and the EU GDPR statement does not replace them (D2).
- **Representative information and consent after emergency enrolment.** No dedicated template; to be adapted from the legal-rep template (D10).
- **Short immediate information sheet for emergency enrolment.** Recommended where useful and feasible (D11).
- **Witness version for a legally designated representative who cannot read or write.** No template (D9).
- **Separate screening information and consent for healthy volunteers.** Needed where screening is separate (D13).
- **Healthy-volunteer document for ages 16-17.** No template (D7).
- **Information for children under 6** who can form an opinion. No template (D8).
- **Separate optional-genetics or substudy consent.** Only if D12 (b) is chosen.
- **Free-form participant materials** (no national template by design): K2 recruitment materials and L2/R2 other participant-facing materials.
- **Third-party and sponsor documents** (no template needed): O1 insurance certificate or indemnity evidence (State Claims Agency Clinical Trial Indemnity Form, standard SCA/HSE/IPHA documents), P2 other financial arrangements (D6), the HPRA fee application form and proof of payment (D14), a sponsor DPIA (if D2 (b)), and the HRCDC application or decision (D15).
- **Site process records** (not CTIS uploads): the capacity assessment and reassessment record, and the record of the emergency decision and attempts to obtain consent. These are described in K1.

Other gaps (not in the Ireland guides; not filled from memory):

- **Signature rules.** The exact authorisation fields of the national N1 and R1 forms are unknown until the templates arrive. No signature rule is given for O1, P2 or the fee form.
- **Risk frequency categories.** None given (D17).
- **Cooling-off period.** The QC checklist asks for "the applicable cooling-off or reflection opportunity where practicable" but gives no length.
- **Content of cited official sources.** The guides cite several sources whose own text was not available: the HPRA Guide AUT-G0170-5 (18 August 2026), NREC Part II National Requirements, the NREC Guidance on Age of Consent, NREC Guidance on Legally Designated Representatives, NREC Guidance on Use of Biological Samples and Associated Data, the State Claims Agency indemnity guidance (May 2022), HRCDC FAQs, the HSE National Policy for Consent v1.1 and published NREC-CT meeting minutes. Two of them (the HPRA guide PDF and the NREC age-of-consent page) are linked from the guides but sit outside `/ctis/ireland/en/` and were not read.
- **Low-intervention trials and the insurance exception.** The guides say nothing about them.
- **Separate list of trial sites.** Not an Ireland checklist item. Sites, investigators and participant numbers are checked against CTIS in the package checklist (P3.01).
- **Variable sheet name.** The sheet is named `_IE_de` though its content is English and targets Ireland.
