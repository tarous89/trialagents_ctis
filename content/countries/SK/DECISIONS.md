# Slovakia: decisions and gaps

Please answer these together. Each item gives the question, the options and a recommendation. Until they are answered, the package uses the recommended defaults.

## Questions for the user

- **D1 English versions of patient-facing documents.** The guides say national guidance expects submitted patient-facing documents in English and Slovak. Slovak is the participant-use and controlling version. Options: (a) submit the Slovak documents with their English counterparts (`…_SK_en`), synchronised by version and date; (b) submit Slovak only and keep the English master and the translation trail in the sponsor file. Recommendation: (a) for L1, K2 and L2, since that is what national guidance expects.
- **D2 Language of administrative documents.** The guides allow Slovak or bilingual Slovak/English for K1 and M1. For M2, M3, N1, O1, P1, S1 and the GDPR statement they give no rule beyond "the application dossier in Slovak". Options: (a) Slovak for all of them, with bilingual K1 and M1 only where the sponsor wants it; (b) bilingual Slovak/English wherever the sponsor prefers. Recommendation: (a). It matches the Slovak variable sheet and the dossier rule. Accept a bilingual insurer certificate only if the Ethics Committee confirms it.
- **D3 Slovakia national site suitability form.** The guide requires the Slovakia national form (updated in 2025). The `CTIS_SK` N1 file is the EU form. Options: (a) supply the current national form, to be converted into `countries/SK/templates/part-ii` so the assistant drafts the trial fields and leaves the site fields to the site; (b) collect the content on the shared EU N1 form for now and transfer it to the national form before upload. Recommendation: (a). Use (b) only as interim drafting, never as the Slovak upload.
- **D4 Title and location of the site list.** The guide places the list under Suitability of the investigator. The naming guide gives no example. Options: (a) `M3_List of sites and principal investigators_SK`; (b) a title the sponsor's system uses under M. Recommendation: (a).
- **D5 Participant compensation form (P1).** The guide makes P1 conditional: it is needed when compensation is not already stated clearly in the information sheet. The hub lists financial information in the normal package. Options: (a) always submit P1; (b) submit P1 only when the information sheet does not cover payments and expenses fully. Recommendation: (a). It costs little and answers the recurring ethics finding on travel costs. The amounts must match the information sheet.
- **D6 GDPR statement location and title.** Options: (a) only the EudraLex statement in the CTIS Form section, titled "GDPR compliance statement"; (b) also upload it in Part II as `R1_Compliance collection and use personal data_SK`. Recommendation: (a). The guide says it is not a duplicate Part II narrative, and the Member State overview lists no separate Slovak document.
- **D7 Endpoint-related patient-facing documents.** Options: (a) English source in Part I and the Slovak translation in Part II as `R2_Patient-facing document_<type>_SK_sk`, following the Q&A route; (b) Slovak versions as `L2_Other subject information material_<type>_SK_sk`. Recommendation: (a) for endpoint-related documents, and L2 only for other items the CTR or Q&A requires. Check the current CTIS upload structure before filing.
- **D8 CTIS location of the national approvals.** The guide asks for the GMO authorisation, the authorisation for investigational radioactive products or precursors, and site transplant-centre authorisations "in the application route required for that trial", without naming a placeholder. Options: (a) the proposed titles `N<next number>_GMO authorisation_SK`, `N<next number>_Radiation authorisation_<procedure>_SK` and `N<next number>_Transplant centre authorisation_<site>`; (b) confirm the route with ŠÚKL or the Ethics Committee for each trial. Recommendation: (b), with (a) as the default titles.
- **D9 Child age band below 12.** The guides fix only the adolescent set (12 to 17). For younger children who can understand they give no fixed band. Options: (a) define the child band per study from the protocol's age range and justify it in K1; (b) use one fixed band for every study. Recommendation: (a). The guide requires bands to be justified and allows more than one child version when the age range is wide.
- **D10 Parental signatures.** Options: (a) signature blocks for two parents or legal representatives, with K1 stating when one suffices and on what legal basis; (b) one block, with the legal basis documented. Recommendation: (a). The guide says not to guess from family circumstances, and to document the legal basis whenever one signature is proposed.
- **D11 Short initial notice in emergency enrolment.** Options: (a) plan the short notice when the protocol shows it can be given without delaying urgent care; (b) none. Recommendation: (a) per protocol. The notice never replaces the full participant and representative documents.
- **D12 Secondary use: separate form or section.** The guide allows either. Options: (a) a separate optional information and choice form (`L1_SIS and ICF_secondary use_SK_sk`); (b) a clearly separate optional section in the main form. Recommendation: (a) when there are several optional choices or samples are stored, and (b) for a single simple choice.
- **D13 Information sheet and consent form: one document or two.** Options: (a) one combined document per population; (b) two controlled documents. Recommendation: (a). It matches the CTCG `L1_SIS and ICF` title and keeps the versions together.
- **D14 Pregnancy follow-up of a participant.** Options: (a) a separate form only when follow-up collects data beyond the main trial information; (b) always a separate form. Recommendation: (a), as the guide says.
- **D15 Missing Slovak participant templates.** Options: (a) supply a Slovak template set, to be split into `templates/participant` as `*_SK_sk.json`; (b) draft each document from the Slovakia guides with the base style, or adapt it from its reference-package counterpart (`based_on`). Recommendation: (b) now and (a) when a set is available.
- **D16 Who prepares the site list (M3).** Options: (a) the sponsor supplies it and TrialAgents checks it against CTIS, M1/M2 and N1 (current setting); (b) TrialAgents assembles it from site data the sponsor supplies, without inventing anything. Recommendation: (b) when the site data are available. The document kind then changes to a drafted document.
- **D17 Representative who cannot read.** The witness route applies to a participant or a representative. Options: (a) add the witness attestation and oral-consent blocks to the representative document, with the deviation visible; (b) a dedicated document. Recommendation: (a).

## Missing

Documents the guides require that have no template in the supplied set:

- **The whole Slovak participant template set.** No templates were supplied, and the text file in `CTIS_SK` is the Bulgarian set. 13 planned templates: adult patients (`SK-PT-main`), healthy volunteers (`SK-PT-healthy`), parents or legal representatives of minors (`SK-PT-parents`), adolescents 12-17 (`SK-PT-adolescents-12-17`), children under 12 (`SK-PT-children`), legal representative of an adult unable to consent (`SK-PT-legal-rep`), adapted participant information and assent (`SK-PT-limited-capacity`), emergency participant information once capable (`SK-PT-emergency`), emergency short initial notice (`SK-PT-emergency-notice`), impartial witness and oral consent (`SK-PT-witness`), pregnancy follow-up of a participant (`SK-PT-pregnancy`), pregnant partner (`SK-PT-pregnant-partner`), secondary use (`SK-PT-secondary`). All are marked `"template": "missing"` (D15).
- **The Slovakia national site suitability form** (2025 update) (D3).
- **K2 recruitment material and L2 other patient-facing material.** Free-form, drafted from the protocol.
- **M3 list of sites and principal investigators.** No template (D4, D16).
- **O1 insurance certificate.** Issued by the insurer.
- **National approvals.** The GMO authorisation, the authorisation for investigational radioactive medicinal products or precursors, and the transplant-centre authorisation are issued by the authorities or held by the site (D8).

Other gaps:

- **National Ethics Committee contact details**, including the required telephone number. Every information sheet needs them, but the guides do not give them. They come from the current Slovak participant-information guidance, which was not supplied.
- **Fee.** The Slovakia guides state no Part II fee, payment route or proof-of-payment rule.
- **Minimum insurance cover.** The guides do not state it.
- **Current national Ethics Committee forms and instructions.** These are the site suitability form instructions (2025) and any republished declaration-of-interest form. The guides say to verify them before submission.
- **Legal-representative rules under Slovak law.** These cover who is the parent or legal representative and when two must sign. The guides refer to them without stating them, so the K1 answer and the signature plan need the sponsor's or site's legal basis (D10).
- **Audit script and missing templates.** `tools/content/audit.py` accepts `"template": "missing"` in `population_sets`, but it checks every id in `population_rules.*.always`, `age_bands[].template` and `shareable_templates` against `participant_templates`. With no templates, the Slovak template ids are recorded under `always_missing`, `age_bands_missing`, `optional_missing` and `shareable_templates_missing`, and `shareable_templates` is left empty. The script was not edited.
- **Slovak-language guides** (`/ctis/slovakia/sk`) were not read, because they are outside the `/ctis/slovakia/en/` scope. The EU hub linked from the Slovakia hub was not re-read.
- **Variable sheet name.** The variable sheet's file name carries `_de` although its content is Slovak. This has no effect, because the forms are shared.
