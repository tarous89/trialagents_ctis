# Slovakia document styling

Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the Slovakia guides (reviewed 23 September 2026): patient information and consent requirements, patient-facing document QC checklist and the population guides. No Slovak participant templates were supplied, so there are no template `styling_instructions`; the national rules below apply to every participant document. Where they are stricter than the base style, they win.

## Rules for all Slovak participant documents

- Written in Slovak, the participant-use version. Familiar words, short sentences, direct explanations; abbreviations expanded at first use.
- The Participant Information Sheet may be combined with the Informed Consent Form or presented as two controlled documents (DECISIONS.md D13). Either way the information comes before the consent section, and the consent section starts on a new page with the letterhead and info table (base style).
- Running footer and filename carry version number, date, language, population and country, plus the protocol number and EU trial number (base style). Page "Strana x z y".
- Trial identity at the start: full and short title, protocol identifier, sponsor, EU trial number where available (`Info table`).
- Visit schedule and procedures as a table, with research-only procedures clearly distinguished from standard care.
- Voluntariness and withdrawal, the main risks and what to do in an emergency in `Info/warning/note box` elements where they help.
- Contacts block: a reachable trial medical contact, a contact for participant rights or complaints, and the national Ethics Committee contact route including its telephone details. These details must be easy to find (ŠÚKL 2025 deficiency).
- Consent section: one line per statement (information received and understood; time and opportunity to ask questions; voluntariness; agreement to the main trial procedures; source-record access; privacy, injury, reimbursement and withdrawal information received).
- Optional choices (future data or sample use, genetic research, sharing with external researchers, transfer outside the EEA, re-contact, recordings, substudies): a separate yes/no line for each, never preselected (`Checkbox group`). Formatting must not hide qualifications or make optional text look mandatory.
- Signature blocks: participant and person conducting the consent discussion, each with name, signature and personally written date. Legal-representative, parent, witness and interpreter blocks only in the pathway where they apply, clearly separated so they cannot be confused; the oral-consent record (participant able to consent but unable to write) has its own block with the witness signature.
- Statement that the participant receives a copy of the signed and dated form.
- Final PDF: special Slovak characters (á, ä, č, ď, é, í, ĺ, ľ, ň, ó, ô, ŕ, š, ť, ú, ý, ž) render correctly; selectable text; correct page order and line breaks; bookmarks if used; no comments, tracked changes or hidden content.

## Population-specific rules

- **Children and adolescents:** information written for the age group's level of understanding; for young children a short verbal or visual explanation. The adolescent document closely reflects the adult content. The assent block is separate from the parent or legal-representative permission and does not look like legal consent.
- **Adults unable to consent:** participant information in a form matched to the participant's ability; the assent or acknowledgement block must not suggest that the participant gives the representative's legal consent.
- **Impartial witness:** witness attestation with the six statements of the Slovakia witness guide; witness, oral-consent, participant and interviewer blocks distinct; interpreter block only if needed.
- **Pregnant partner:** addressed to the partner; medical-record access stated clearly; infant data block separate where a parent or legal-representative permission is needed.
- **Emergency enrolment:** the later full information separates what has already happened from what will happen next.
