# Croatia document styling

Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the Croatia guides (reviewed 23 September 2026): patient information and consent requirements, patient-facing document QC checklist (sections 7 to 9) and the population guides. No Croatian participant templates were supplied, so there are no template `styling_instructions`. The national rules below apply to every participant document. Where they are stricter than the base style, they win.

## Rules for all Croatian participant documents

- Written in Croatian: professionally translated and proofread, and adapted to Croatia rather than translated literally. Medical and regulatory terms are explained in plain Croatian. Sentences and paragraphs are short enough for the intended population.
- **Title:** the document title includes the Croatian term **Informirani pristanak**, used consistently. Vague labels such as "information", "form" or "document" do not replace it. Assent documents for minors are titled **Izjava o pristanku za sudjelovanje u kliničkom ispitivanju**.
- **One unified document:** all participant information comes before the signature section, in the same document. Never split it into an information sheet and a signature-only form. This replaces the base-style rule that the consent form starts as a separate part with its own letterhead and info table. The signature section may start on a new page, but it continues the same document and pagination, carries the document identity in the Croatian header and footer, and must not look detached from the information.
- **First page:** a header with the basic trial and sponsor details (`Info table`: title, protocol number, EU trial number where available, sponsor, site and principal investigator).
- **Header and footer in Croatian:** the running footer has the document name, population, version, date, protocol number and EU trial number, plus the page as "Stranica x od y". Population, site, country, language and age band are clear in the file name and, where useful, in the header.
- **Formal address:** speak to the participant directly with the formal pronoun Vi. Its forms start with a capital letter (Vi, Vas, Vam, Vaš). Avoid excessive repetition.
- Visits and weeks use Croatian ordinal numbers.
- **Layout:** the document is complete but not unnecessarily long. Avoid large blank spaces, inconsistent margins and small text. Font, contrast, spacing, headings, tables and page breaks support reading and printing.
- Visit schedule and procedures go in a table (base style). Risks are grouped and prioritised, and serious or likely effects are not hidden in dense text.
- **Optional choices:** each optional question has both a **DA** and a **NE** box (`Checkbox group`), never preselected. It is visibly separate from the main decision to take part.
- **Contacts block:** the investigator first, then the trial lead, for trial questions. Then medical questions and emergencies, data protection and complaints. The Central Ethics Committee's role, its approval once obtained, and its address for written questions about participant rights. The route to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).
- **Signature blocks:** labels name the participant, the legal representative, the parent or guardian, the physician or dentist who conducts the interview ("liječnik ili doktor dentalne medicine koji provodi razgovor"), and the impartial witness. Each block has name, signature and date. Add time fields where the protocol or the emergency process needs evidence of the exact sequence. Representative, parent, assent and witness blocks appear only in the pathway where they apply, separated so they cannot be confused. Signature pages include the document identity.
- Croatian diacritics (č, ć, dž, đ, š, ž), capitalization, grammar and formal address are consistent and render correctly in the final PDF.
- No tracked changes, comments, placeholders, foreign contacts or inconsistent page numbers. Only the current version is in the package.

## Population-specific rules

- **Ages 4 to 7:** short assent statement with simple illustrations, simple explanations and **font no smaller than 20**.
- **Ages 8 to 11:** separate assent statement with simple illustrations, simple explanations and **font no smaller than 16**.
- **Ages 12 to 14 and 15 to 17:** separate versions in an ordinary document format with simpler language. The 15 to 17 version may come close to the adult document in detail.
- Child documents focus on what the child will experience and decide. They are never the parent document in smaller font. Required procedures are kept apart from optional research, and DA and NE choices are age-appropriate where the child's view is sought.
- **Parent or representative document:** one parent signature field per Central Ethics Committee guidance, or the correct guardian or legal-representative field. A separate signature field for a minor who can form an opinion and evaluate the information.
- **Adults lacking capacity:** participant information adapted in words, length, layout, images or supported-communication methods to the person's actual ability. Add a signature or assent field when the participant can make or record a meaningful choice. It must not look like the representative's legal consent.
- **Impartial witness:** a witness section with an attestation: the information was explained accurately, the participant or representative appeared to understand it, the decision was voluntary, the alternative means recorded consent, and the witness observed the process. The participant or representative, witness and interviewer blocks stay distinct. A translator block appears only if the procedure requires it.
- **Emergency enrollment:** the later full information separates what has already happened from what will happen next. Any short initial information must not imply that full consent was obtained.
- **Pregnancy follow-up:** the pregnant-participant and pregnant-partner documents are separate and addressed to the woman concerned. The infant follow-up period is stated.
