# Croatia: decisions and gaps

Please answer these together. Each item gives the question, the options and a recommendation. Until they are answered, the package uses the recommended defaults.

## Questions for the user

- **D1 Language of forms and administrative documents.** The cover letter and all participant-related documents must be in Croatian. Other documents may be in Croatian or English. Options: (a) Croatian for every form TrialAgents answers (K1, N1, P1, the GDPR statement, S1), with English accepted only for supplied third-party documents (insurance, GCP certificate, agreements, mentor documents); (b) English for the forms that are not participant-related. Recommendation: (a). It matches the supplied Croatian variable sheet and keeps one language across what reviewers read.
- **D2 PI CV format.** Central Ethics Committee guidance requests the HALMED CV format, in Croatian, signed and dated, and no more than six months old. The `CTIS_HR` M1 file is the EU CV form. Options: (a) supply the HALMED format, to be converted into `countries/HR/templates/part-ii` so TrialAgents prepares only the trial fields; (b) collect the CV on the EU form and move it to the HALMED format before upload. Recommendation: (a). Use (b) only for interim drafting, never as the Croatian upload.
- **D3 Title of the GCP training certificate.** The guide requires a signed GCP certificate with the CV but gives no CTIS title. Options: (a) `M2_GCP training_<investigator>_<site>` (Austria and Germany precedent); (b) attach it to the CV upload as a split file of `M1_CV_<investigator>_<site>`. Recommendation: (a).
- **D4 Title of the mentor documentation.** Options: (a) `M3_Mentor documentation_<investigator>_<site>`; (b) a title the sponsor's system uses under Suitability of the investigator. Recommendation: (a).
- **D5 Title of the hospitalisation agreement.** The guide says to include it with the site-suitability evidence. Options: (a) `N<next number>_Hospitalisation agreement_<site>` as a separate N upload; (b) a split file of `N1_Site suitability form_<site>`. Recommendation: (a).
- **D6 Insurance evidence at initial submission.** Options: (a) submit only the executed policy, certificate and terms; (b) if the executed cover is not ready, ask the Ministry of Health and the Central Ethics Committee in writing which interim evidence they accept. Recommendation: (a) where possible, otherwise (b). Never state that a draft alone is enough without that written confirmation.
- **D7 Description of trial financing.** The guide asks for one description of trial financing, payments to investigators and sites, participant reimbursement and other arrangements. The EU compensation template (P1) covers only the participant part. Options: (a) P1 plus a short sponsor description, `P3_Funding of the trial_HR`, covering financing, payments to investigators and sites, and the statement that the sponsor bears all protocol-related costs; (b) P1 only, with those points placed in its free-text fields. Recommendation: (a). The P1 fields do not ask for financing or site payments.
- **D8 Site agreements in CTIS.** The signed sponsor–site agreement is mandatory before each site starts. The guide says that where it is not an application document in the procedure, the sponsor keeps evidence of execution and provides it on request. Options: (a) upload it per site as `P2_Financial arrangements_<site>`; (b) track it only as a site-start condition and provide it if requested. Recommendation: (a) unless the current Central Ethics Committee application forms show it is not an application document for the procedure.
- **D9 GDPR statement location.** Options: (a) only the EudraLex statement in the CTIS Form section, titled "GDPR compliance statement"; (b) also a Part II `R1_Compliance collection and use personal data_HR`. Recommendation: (a). Use (b) only if the national information is not fully covered or the authority asks for it.
- **D10 CTIS category for questionnaires, scales, diaries, instructions and cards.** The guide says to use "the closest participant-facing CTIS category". Options: (a) `L2_Other subject information material_<type>_HR_hr`, with endpoint-related patient-facing documents as `R2_Patient-facing document_<type>_HR_hr` (naming guide); (b) L2 for everything. Recommendation: (a). Add the English original (`_HR_en`) for any instrument that is not scientifically validated.
- **D11 Children under 4.** The Croatian assent bands start at 4 years. Options: (a) parent or representative consent only, with oral assent recorded in the approved manner if the child can express a view, as K1 describes; (b) also a picture-based information sheet. Recommendation: (a). Ask the Central Ethics Committee if the protocol enrols children under 4 who can express a view.
- **D12 Parent signature fields.** Central Ethics Committee guidance provides one parent signature field, or the guardian's or legal representative's field if the child has no parent. Options: (a) one parent field, as the guidance says; (b) two parent fields. Recommendation: (a). Add a second field only if the sponsor's or site's legal advice for a specific custody situation requires it, and record why.
- **D13 Pregnancy and infant data from healthcare professionals.** Croatian national specifics forbid contacting the woman's gynaecologist, obstetrician, the child's paediatrician or other healthcare professionals. Options: (a) remove such clauses and collect everything from the woman; (b) if the safety objective cannot be met that way, seek written clarification from the Central Ethics Committee before submission. Recommendation: (a), and (b) only where the protocol truly needs source access.
- **D14 Adults lacking capacity in psychiatric or psychopharmaceutical trials.** Central Ethics Committee guidance bars people under guardianship who cannot decide independently from psychopharmaceutical trials. It also expects trials in people with mental disorders to include adults with legal capacity. Older rules mention a positive opinion from the committee protecting people with mental disorders. Options: (a) leave this population out of the plan until written advice from the Ministry of Health and the Central Ethics Committee is received; (b) prepare the documents in parallel while the advice is pending. Recommendation: (a).
- **D15 Short initial information in emergency enrolment.** The guide recommends it. Options: (a) plan `HR-PT-emergency-initial` when it can be given without delaying treatment; (b) none. Recommendation: (a), per protocol. It must not suggest that full consent was obtained.
- **D16 Witness information or confidentiality form.** It is optional in the guide. Options: (a) add it only when the witness will receive personal or health information beyond what is needed to observe consent; (b) always add it. Recommendation: (a).
- **D17 Genetics consent: own document or part of the secondary-use consent.** The guide calls for a separate Croatian informed consent for genetic testing and genome or exome analysis. Options: (a) a separate genetics document (`HR-PT-genetics`) besides the substudy, biobank and future-use consent; (b) one optional consent covering both, with separate DA/NE choices. Recommendation: (a). If genetic analysis is mandatory and integral to the trial, it goes in the main consent instead.
- **D18 Missing Croatian participant templates.** Options: (a) supply a Croatian template set, to be split into `templates/participant` as `*_HR_hr.json`; (b) draft each document from the Croatia guides with the base and Croatian styles, or adapt it from its reference-package counterpart (`based_on`). Recommendation: (b) now, and (a) when a set is available.
- **D19 Exceptional electronic consent.** Options: (a) paper consent in person only; (b) describe an exceptional electronic-consent process in K1, followed by in-person paper consent as soon as possible. Recommendation: (a). Use (b) only where the protocol has a real exceptional need, approved in advance.
- **D20 Shared documents across populations.** These consents do not depend on the population: pregnant partner, contact person, home delivery and witness information. Options: (a) one shared document per trial when nothing in it differs; (b) one per population. Recommendation: (a). Agree it with the user per study, as the planning guide says.

## Missing

Documents the guides require that have no template in the supplied set:

- **The whole Croatian participant template set.** No templates were supplied (D18). There are 24 planned templates:
  - Adults and volunteers: adult patients (`HR-PT-main`), healthy volunteers (`HR-PT-healthy`).
  - Minors: parent, guardian or legal representative (`HR-PT-parents`); assent for ages 4-7, 8-11, 12-14 and 15-17 (`HR-PT-assent-4-7`, `HR-PT-assent-8-11`, `HR-PT-assent-12-14`, `HR-PT-assent-15-17`).
  - Adults lacking capacity: legal representative (`HR-PT-legal-rep`), adapted participant information (`HR-PT-limited-capacity`).
  - Emergency enrolment: participant (`HR-PT-emergency`), representative (`HR-PT-emergency-representative`), initial information (`HR-PT-emergency-initial`).
  - Impartial witness: witness section (`HR-PT-witness`), witness information (`HR-PT-witness-info`).
  - Pregnancy follow-up: pregnant participant (`HR-PT-pregnancy`), pregnant partner (`HR-PT-pregnant-partner`), representative (`HR-PT-pregnancy-representative`), assent (`HR-PT-pregnancy-assent`).
  - Optional research: substudies, biobank and future use (`HR-PT-secondary`), genetics (`HR-PT-genetics`).
  - Third parties: caregiver or study partner (`HR-PT-caregiver`), emergency contact person (`HR-PT-contact-person`), home delivery (`HR-PT-home-delivery`), other third party (`HR-PT-third-party`).
- **The HALMED CV format** for M1 (D2).
- **K2 recruitment material and L2 questionnaires, scales, diaries, instructions and cards.** Free-form, drafted from the protocol and the validated instruments.
- **GCP training certificate, mentor documentation, hospitalisation agreement.** Supplied by the investigator, mentor, training provider, site or hospital.
- **O1 insurance policy, certificate and terms.** Issued by the insurer.
- **Funding description (HR-P-FUNDING) and sponsor–site agreements.** Supplied by the sponsor (D7, D8).

Other gaps:

- **Central Ethics Committee address** for written questions about participant rights. Every informed consent needs it, but the guides do not give it.
- **AZOP contact details** for the data-protection route. These are needed in every informed consent and are not given in the guides.
- **Fee.** The Croatia guides state no Part II fee, payment route or proof-of-payment rule.
- **Minimum insurance cover.** The guides do not state it.
- **Authorised GCP training provider.** The guide requires a certificate "from the authorised provider" but does not name the provider or how authorisation is shown.
- **Central Ethics Committee source documents.** These were not supplied and only their content as recorded in the guides was used: detailed instructions to applicants, Version 3; national specifics, Version 1, February 2022; application forms.
- **Current procedural route for mental-disorder trials.** This means the committee that protects people with mental disorders (D14).
- **Croatian-language guides** (`/ctis/croatia/hr`) were not read, because they are outside the `/ctis/croatia/en/` scope. The EU hub linked from the Croatia hub was not re-read.
- **Variable sheet name.** The variable sheet's file name carries `_de` although its content is Croatian. This has no effect, because the forms are shared.
- **Audit script.** `tools/content/audit.py HR` passes with `participant_templates: []`, the planned ids in `participant_templates_missing` (a list of `{id, name, role}`) and those ids in `population_rules`. No script change was needed.
