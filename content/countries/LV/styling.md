# Latvia document styling

Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the Latvia guides (reviewed 23 September 2026), namely the Part II document checklist, the patient information and consent requirements, the patient-facing document QC checklist and the population guides. No Latvian participant templates were supplied, so there are no template `styling_instructions`. The national rules below apply to every participant document. Where they are stricter than the base style, they win.

## Rules for all Latvian participant documents

- Written in Latvian, concise and understandable to the intended age and population. Use short sentences, familiar Latvian terms and clear headings, and define abbreviations at first use. Remove technical detail that does not help the decision.
- The document identity is easy to find at the start (`Info table`): descriptive title, version and date, sponsor protocol number, EU CT number, study-centre name and address, and data-controller identity.
- Contacts block, easy to find: trial questions, urgent medical issues (including after-hours), participant rights, privacy, ZVA, the assigned ethics committee and the Data State Inspectorate. Privacy contacts are kept separate from medical-emergency contacts.
- The running footer and the filename carry the document title, population, version, date, sponsor protocol number and EU CT number (base style). The page format is "x. lapa no y". The filename identifies the document type, for example adult, legal representative, 0 to 13 years or 14 to 17 years.
- Visit schedule and procedures go in a table, with research-only procedures clearly distinguished from usual care.
- Consent section: specific statements, with the mandatory agreement to participate kept separate from optional activities. Leave enough space around consent choices and signatures.
- Optional choices (future research, optional biopsies, recordings, genetic analysis beyond the trial purpose, re-contact): one unticked choice per activity, or another active decision method (`Checkbox group`). Each choice can be answered independently.
- Signature blocks: the participant or authorised decision-maker, and the person conducting the consent interview, each with name, signature and date. Impartial-witness and interpreter blocks appear only in the witness pathway. From age 14 the minor has their own block. Each signer's block is clearly separate.
- State that the participant or representative receives a copy of the signed consent documentation.
- Page numbers, headers, footers, version control and filenames are consistent.
- The CTIS copy is blank: no participant signatures, completed personal data, tracked changes, comments or drafting notes.
- Before upload, a native Latvian proofread follows the last content change.

## Population-specific rules

- **Minors:** the information is age-appropriate. The adolescent (14 to 17) document is direct and understandable, and is not a copy of the parent document with minor wording changes. The minor's own consent block is separate from the legal representative's. A younger child's assent block does not imply legal consent.
- **Adults unable to consent:** participant information is in a form and language suited to the participant's ability (simplified sheet, visual aid or another approved format). The legal-representative document has separate fields for the identity and authority of the participant and of the representative.
- **Impartial witness:** the witness statement block records the participant and document version, the reason an alternative means was used (without unnecessary health detail), the alternative means, confirmation of accurate explanation and apparent understanding, confirmation that consent was freely given, and the witness's name, role or relationship, signature and date. A separate interpreter block is added if an interpreter is involved.
- **Emergency enrolment:** the subsequent information separates what already occurred under the emergency pathway from what will happen next.
- **Pregnancy follow-up:** the pregnant-participant, pregnant-partner and infant pathways are separate documents or clearly separated sections. A legal-representative signature block is included for infant data where required.
