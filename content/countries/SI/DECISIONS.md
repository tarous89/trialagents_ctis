# Slovenia: decisions and gaps

Please answer these together. Each item gives the question, the options and a recommendation. Until they are answered, the package uses the recommended defaults.

## Questions for the user

- **D1 Language of the administrative documents.** The guides allow Slovenian or English for K1, M1, M2, N1, O1, P1, P2, R1 and S1. Options: (a) Slovenian for all of them; (b) English where the sponsor prefers, with the participant documents, cover letter and synopsis still in Slovenian. Recommendation: (a) for the forms the assistant drafts (K1, N1, P1, R1, S1), because the supplied Slovenian variable sheet targets Slovenian. Accept CVs, DoIs and the insurer's evidence in either language.
- **D2 Italian or Hungarian versions.** The 11 Slovenia guide pages, the variable sheet and the `CTIS_SI` set state no Italian or Hungarian requirement for any document. This includes sites in the bilingual areas, which the guides do not mention. Options: (a) Slovenian only, as the guides require; (b) also give Italian or Hungarian participant documents at sites in the bilingual areas, as companion versions (`…_SI_it`, `…_SI_hu`) alongside the Slovenian documents; (c) ask KME RS for each such site. Recommendation: (a) by default, and (c) when a site is in a bilingual area. Add (b) only on KME RS's or the site's instruction. A translation never replaces the required Slovenian version.
- **D3 Missing Slovenian participant templates.** Options: (a) supply a Slovenian template set, to be split into `templates/participant` as `*_SI_sl.json`; (b) draft each document from the Slovenia guides with the base style, or adapt it from its reference-package counterpart (`based_on`). Recommendation: (b) now, and (a) when a set is available.
- **D4 PIS and ICF: one document or two.** The guide allows either. Options: (a) one integrated document per population; (b) two coordinated documents with matching identifiers. Recommendation: (a). It matches the CTCG `L1_SIS and ICF` title and keeps the versions together.
- **D5 Endpoint-related participant documents (diaries, questionnaires).** The guide says to upload the Slovenian version "in the appropriate Part II category according to current CTIS guidance". The English version stays with the common scientific assessment. Options: (a) `R2_Patient-facing document_<type>_SI_sl` for endpoint-related instruments and `L2_Other subject information material_<type>_SI_sl` for other materials; (b) L2 for everything. Recommendation: (a). Check the current CTIS upload structure before filing.
- **D6 Trial financing description.** Checklist item 9 asks for trial financing, payments to investigators or sites and participant compensation. The shared P1 form covers only participant compensation. Options: (a) P1 on the shared form, plus a separate `P2_Financial arrangements_SI` drafted from the sponsor's financing facts; (b) one P1 document combining the form with an appended financing section. Recommendation: (a). It keeps the shared form unchanged.
- **D7 Cover letter and synopsis.** Both are national language items outside the Part II placeholders. Options: (a) record them as sponsor documents, which are checked here (current setting); (b) have the assistant draft them in Slovenian from the plan and protocol when Part I is in the plan. Titles proposed: `B1_Cover letter_<EU CT number>_SI_sl` and `D1_Protocol synopsis_SI_<EU CT number>_sl` (plus `_en` in a multinational application). Recommendation: (b) for the synopsis when Part I is in the plan, and (a) for the cover letter. Confirm the titles.
- **D8 Insurance policy wording.** The guide asks for the policy wording or an explanatory guarantee when the certificate omits essential terms. Options: (a) upload it with O1 as `O2_Proof of coverage_<sponsor or site>`; (b) merge it into the O1 upload. Recommendation: (a), and only when it is needed.
- **D9 Fee proof.** The guide says to retain proof of payment to JAZMP. It does not say whether to upload the proof or where. Options: (a) retain it in the sponsor file only; (b) also upload it in the CTIS Forms section as `Proof of payment_JAZMP fee_SI`. Recommendation: follow the current JAZMP payment instructions for the application type, and use (a) unless they ask for an upload.
- **D10 Reproductive-risk acknowledgment.** KME RS expects a signed acknowledgment from women of childbearing potential, and from healthy female partners of male participants where relevant. Options: (a) a separate acknowledgment document (`SI-PT-reproductive-risk`), one version for participants and one for partners; (b) a signed section inside the main ICF, plus a short partner acknowledgment. Recommendation: (b) for participants, because it keeps the risk information and the signature together, and a separate partner document.
- **D11 Pregnant participant follow-up.** Options: (a) a dedicated form only when the follow-up goes beyond the main trial information; (b) always a dedicated form. Recommendation: (a), as the guide says.
- **D12 Emergency concise information sheet.** The guide lists a concise information sheet for the participant as soon as they can receive information, as well as the full subsequent documents. Options: (a) a separate concise sheet plus the subsequent participant and representative documents (current setting); (b) put the concise part at the start of the subsequent participant document. Recommendation: (a) when the protocol has an early contact point, and otherwise (b). No national emergency form or waiver exists.
- **D13 Representative who cannot write.** The witness route applies to a participant or a legally designated representative. Options: (a) add the witness section to the representative document; (b) a dedicated document. Recommendation: (a).
- **D14 Secondary use: separate form or section.** The guide allows either. Options: (a) a separate optional form (`L1_SIS and ICF_secondary use_SI_sl`); (b) a clearly separated optional section in the main form, with its own choice and signature. Recommendation: (a) when there are several optional choices or samples are stored, and (b) for a single simple choice.
- **D15 Child groupings below 15.** No national bands below 15 exist. Options: (a) define the groupings per study from the protocol's age range and burden, and justify them in K1; (b) use one fixed child band for every study. Recommendation: (a), as the guide says.
- **D16 Parental signatures.** The guides do not say whether one or both parents must consent, only that legal authority must be confirmed for the particular child. Options: (a) signature blocks for two parents or legal representatives, with K1 stating when one suffices and on what legal basis; (b) one block, with the legal basis documented. Recommendation: (a), with the legal basis confirmed by the sponsor or site.

## Missing

Documents the guides require that have no template in the supplied set:

- **The whole Slovenian participant template set.** The 17 planned templates are: adults (`SI-PT-main`), healthy volunteers (`SI-PT-healthy`), screening consent (`SI-PT-screening`), parents or representatives of minors (`SI-PT-parents`), children under 15 (`SI-PT-children`), adolescents 15-17 (`SI-PT-adolescents-15-17`), legal representative of an adult unable to consent (`SI-PT-legal-rep`), adapted participant information (`SI-PT-limited-capacity`), emergency concise information (`SI-PT-emergency-notice`), emergency subsequent participant consent (`SI-PT-emergency`), emergency representative consent (`SI-PT-emergency-legal-rep`), impartial witness (`SI-PT-witness`), reproductive-risk acknowledgment (`SI-PT-reproductive-risk`), pregnant participant follow-up (`SI-PT-pregnancy`), pregnant partner (`SI-PT-pregnant-partner`), infant follow-up (`SI-PT-infant`) and secondary use (`SI-PT-secondary`). All are marked `"template": "missing"` (D3).
- **K2 recruitment material and L2 other participant materials.** Free-form, drafted from the protocol.
- **P2 financing description.** No template (D6).
- **O1 insurance proof.** Issued by the insurer or guarantor.
- **Slovenian cover letter and protocol synopsis.** No template (D7).
- **Fee proof.** Issued on payment to JAZMP (D9).

Other gaps:

- **Contact details.** The guides do not give the current KME RS contact details or the other national contact points (JAZMP, patient-rights representatives, Human Rights Ombudsman, Information Commissioner). They must come from the official KME RS page at drafting time.
- **Minimum insurance cover.** The guides state none.
- **Fee amount.** The JAZMP tariff is not in the guides.
- **Slovenian data-protection statute.** The guides refer to "Slovenian law" and the Information Commissioner without naming the statute, so R1 states compliance with Slovenian law without a citation.
- **Parental authority rules under Slovenian law.** The guides refer to these without stating them (D16).
- **KME RS source documents.** The 2020 instructions, the model consent form and the current KME RS page were not supplied. Their content is taken only as the guides record it.
- **Pages not read.** The Slovenian-language guides (`/ctis/slovenia/sl`) and the EU hub linked from the Slovenia hub are outside the `/ctis/slovenia/en/` scope and were not read.
- **Variable sheet name.** The file name carries `_de` although the content is Slovenian. This has no effect, because the forms are shared.
