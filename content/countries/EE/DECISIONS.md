# Estonia: decisions and gaps

Please answer these together. Each item gives the question, the options and a recommendation. Until they are answered, the package uses the recommended defaults.

## Questions for the user

- **D1 Answer language of the forms.** The guides say the application dossier is generally in English. Only participant-facing information must be in Estonian, and the Principal Investigator CV must be in English. The supplied variable sheet asks for Estonian answers. Options: (a) Estonian for K1, N1, P1, R1 and S1, and English for M1 and M2; (b) English for all administrative forms, with only participant-facing documents in Estonian; (c) Estonian for K1, because it describes the consent procedure for the Estonian participant information, and English for N1, P1, R1 and S1. Recommendation: (a). It matches the variable sheet and the guides do not exclude Estonian. Use (b) or (c) if the sponsor's regulatory team works in English.
- **D2 Russian and other participant languages.** The guides do not mention Russian. Ravimiamet requires every material given to participants in every language in which it is intended to be used. Options: (a) Estonian only, plus a full `_EE_ru` (or `_EE_en`) version of each participant document whenever the sites plan to use that language (study fact `population.non_estonian_speakers`); (b) plan Russian versions by default for every Estonian package. Recommendation: (a). Ask the sites at planning whether Russian-speaking participants will receive Russian materials, and do not assume it.
- **D3 Placement and scope of Estonian patient-facing materials.** From 27 April 2026, Ravimiamet places Estonian-language patient-facing materials in Part II section R for multinational trials. Options: (a) apply the rule to the items whose English version sits in Part I section D (questionnaires, diaries, cards), titled `R2_Patient-facing document_<type>_EE_et`, and keep the information and consent forms in L1; (b) also move the L1 sets to section R. Recommendation: (a). The L1 forms were never in section D, and the hub calls this an operational upload rule alongside the Part II subject-information category. For a mononational trial, follow the EU Q&A and check the CTIS structure before filing.
- **D4 GDPR statement location and title.** Options: (a) the EU statement in the CTIS Form section only, titled `R1_Compliance collection and use personal data_EE`; (b) also upload it as a Part II R1 document. Recommendation: (a). The Estonia checklist names the Form section only.
- **D5 Fee and invoice information.** Estonia requires no prepayment. The guides do not say where in CTIS the invoice details go. Options: (a) enter them as structured data, with a file titled `Proof of payment_Invoice recipient details_EE` only if CTIS needs an upload; (b) a cover-letter paragraph only. Recommendation: (a). If an academic fee exemption is claimed, upload the confirmation that no compensation is received from a medicine manufacturer in the same place. Confirm the location with Ravimiamet if unsure.
- **D6 Trial budget information (P2).** The P1 compensation form has no field for the budget information or the Health Insurance Fund statement. Options: (a) a separate `P2_Financial arrangements_EE` that the sponsor supplies and TrialAgents checks (current setting); (b) TrialAgents drafts P2 from sponsor-supplied figures; (c) put the budget information in the cover letter. Recommendation: (b) when the sponsor provides the figures. The document kind then changes to a drafted document.
- **D7 Documents for minors aged 7 to 17.** The minor's own consent is required by law. The guide allows separate child or adolescent documents or one coordinated form. Options: (a) separate age-adapted minor documents with their own consent block, plus the representative form, split into two versions (for example a younger and an older band) when the protocol's age range is wide; (b) one coordinated form for the minor and the representative. Recommendation: (a). It makes each decision and signature clear. Define and justify the drafting bands in K1 (v114).
- **D8 Children under 7.** Options: (a) a short information sheet for young children who can understand simple information; (b) oral involvement only, documented by the site. Recommendation: decide per study. Use (a) when the protocol enrols children close to 7 or the procedures are burdensome.
- **D9 Number of representative signatures for minors.** The guide says not to assume a two-parent rule without a verified legal basis. Options: (a) one representative block plus an optional second block, with K1 stating how authority is verified; (b) always two parent blocks. Recommendation: (a). The site verifies the legal basis for each case.
- **D10 Optional future use: section or separate document.** Ravimiamet recommends separate consents "in the information form". Options: (a) separate consent choices inside the main form for pseudonymised health data and sample use; (b) a separate future-use document (`L1_SIS and ICF_future use_EE_et`). Recommendation: (a) for simple pseudonymised data or sample use, and (b) when the future research is broad, involves genetics, external sharing or transfers outside the EEA, or concerns minors or incapacitated participants.
- **D11 Pregnancy follow-up of a participant.** Options: (a) a separate pregnancy follow-up document only when the protocol collects data beyond what the main form covers; (b) always a separate document. Recommendation: (a). The pregnant partner always gets her own document.
- **D12 Impartial witness pathway.** Estonia has no separate witness form. Options: (a) add a witness attestation and signature block to the standard form for the population, plus the same block in the representative document when a representative cannot write; (b) a dedicated witnessed version of each form. Recommendation: (a).
- **D13 Signature on the declaration of interests (M2).** The guides require the CV to be signed and dated, but state no rule for the declaration. Options: (a) date and sign it like the CV; (b) date only, as the template provides. Recommendation: (a). It keeps the investigator documents consistent. Confirm with the current Ethics Committee instructions.
- **D14 Other subject information material (L2).** The Estonia checklist does not list a separate L2 item. Options: (a) no L2 by default: endpoint-related items go to R2 (D3), and any other participant material required by the CTR or the Q&A gets `L2_Other subject information material_<type>_EE_et`; (b) upload all operational participant materials. Recommendation: (a).
- **D15 Missing Estonian participant templates.** Options: (a) supply the Estonia national SIS/ICF template (Ravimiamet, Estonian and English) and an Estonian template set, to be split into `templates/participant` as `*_EE_et.json`; (b) draft each document from the Estonia guides with the base style, or adapt it from its reference-package counterpart (`based_on`). Recommendation: (a) for the national template, which the guides recommend as the baseline, and (b) until it is supplied.
- **D16 Academic insurance exception.** Options: (a) always plan O1 unless eligibility has been confirmed in writing under the Medicinal Products Act and the Health Insurance Act; (b) skip O1 for academic sponsors. Recommendation: (a), as the guide says.

## Missing

Documents the guides require that have no template in the supplied set:

- **The whole Estonian participant template set.** No templates were supplied. 12 templates are planned: capable adults (`EE-PT-main`), healthy volunteers (`EE-PT-healthy`), legal representative of a minor (`EE-PT-parents`), minors 7-17 (`EE-PT-minor-7-17`), children under 7 (`EE-PT-child-under-7`), legal representative of an incapacitated adult (`EE-PT-legal-rep`), simplified participant information (`EE-PT-limited-capacity`), emergency consent to continue (`EE-PT-emergency`), impartial witness (`EE-PT-witness`), pregnancy follow-up (`EE-PT-pregnancy`), pregnant partner (`EE-PT-pregnant-partner`) and optional future use (`EE-PT-secondary`). All are marked `"template": "missing"` (D15).
- **The Estonia national SIS/ICF template.** The guides recommend it as the baseline, but it was not supplied (D15).
- **K2 recruitment material and R2 Estonian patient-facing materials.** These are free-form, drafted from the protocol and the English Part I versions.
- **O1 insurance certificate** (insurer), **P2 budget information** (sponsor, D6) and the **invoice and decision recipient details** (sponsor, D5).

Other gaps:

- **Fee amount.** The guides state the invoicing route but not the amount.
- **Minimum insurance amount.** The certificate must state the insured amount, but the guides give no minimum.
- **Estonian minimum wage figure.** Healthy-volunteer time compensation is based on the minimum wage in the year the trial starts. The figure is not in the guides and must come from the sponsor's sources.
- **Legal-representative rules under Estonian law.** This covers who represents a minor or an incapacitated adult, and when more than one signature is needed. The guides refer to these rules without stating them (D9).
- **Participant-rights contact.** The QC checklist asks for contacts for participant rights. The guides do not name the body or the contact details.
- **Health Information System access.** The guides say what not to promise. The actual access each site can give comes from the site.
- **Official sources not read.** The Ravimiamet pages, the Medicinal Products Act and EudraLex documents that the guides cite were not opened. Only the TrialAgents guides were read. The Estonian-language guides (`/ctis/estonia/et`) are outside the `/ctis/estonia/en/` scope, and the EU hub linked from the Estonia hub was not re-read.
- **Variable sheet name.** The file name carries `_de` although its content is Estonian. This has no effect, because the forms are shared.
