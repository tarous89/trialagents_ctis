# Poland document styling

Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the Poland guides (https://trialagents.com/ctis/poland/en and its ten linked pages, reviewed 23 September 2026) and the `styling_instructions` of each Polish participant template (CTIS_PL set, `PL_Participant-facing_templates_05082026.txt`). Where they are stricter than the base style, these rules win.

## Rules for all Polish participant documents

- **Language.** Every participant-facing document is in Polish. Review the final Polish text clinically, not only linguistically; drug names, procedures, reproductive precautions, risks and withdrawal instructions use terms a Polish participant understands.
- **Readability.** Written for a lay reader: clear headings (preferably phrased as the participant's question), short paragraphs, medical terms defined at first use, and a logical sequence from study purpose through procedures, risks, rights and contacts. Shorten long legal passages where the legal meaning can be kept in plain language. Keep the participant's actions visible: whom to call, when to stop a medicine, when to report a pregnancy, how to withdraw.
- **Info table and running footer.** Each template's `info_table` opens the information and is repeated on the consent form. Running footer on every page as in the templates' `footer`: document name, population label, version (`wersja`), date, protocol number (`Numer protokołu`) and EU trial number (`Numer badania UE`), with page numbering "Strona x z y". Headers, footers and page numbers consistent across the document.
- **Title and population.** The document title identifies the intended population clearly. Age ranges in Polish labels follow NKB notation: "13–18 lat" means the participant has reached 13 but not yet 18. Never a label such as "dzieci 12–18 lat" across the 13-year threshold.
- **Tables and visuals.** Visit schedules and procedure lists as tables; adverse-effect frequencies in the templates' frequency table (bardzo często, często, niezbyt często, rzadko). Pictures, symbols, diagrams or simplified wording only where they improve comprehension and remain accurate; they never replace required information.
- **Optional choices.** The main participation decision is visibly separate from optional choices (future use, genetic research, recontact). Separate YES/NO lines, never pre-ticked; separate signature or initial boxes only when they make the choice clearer.
- **Signature blocks.** Each block names its function and has labelled blank fields for name, date and signature; separate blocks for each signer (participant, legal representative or parent(s), impartial witness, person conducting the consent interview). No signature line for anyone who is not a consent-giver, the consent-taker or the impartial witness. One impartial-witness block only, visually and legally separate from the consent-giver and interviewer blocks, with a statement limited to what the witness observes. Parent forms show only the chosen joint-decision route (both parents, or one parent with the joint-decision attestation), visually distinct from a sole-representative block. Child information for under-13s has no signature or consent checkbox.
- **Adapted documents.** A participant document for an adult who cannot consent is labelled as information or declaration of willingness (`formularz wyrażenia woli`), not as the legal consent; the layout must not suggest the participant gives the legally operative consent.
- **Clean final version.** No tracked changes, drafting comments, translation notes, template instructions, examples ("Przykład"), unused alternatives, the section "Informacje i instrukcje ogólne" or the ABM and PSBK logos.
- **Fonts and margins.** The Polish templates prescribe no font, size, margins or spacing; use `styles/base.md`.

## Template-specific rules

Verbatim from each template's `styling_instructions`.

### Informacja dla uczestnika i formularz świadomej zgody na udział w badaniu klinicznym (`L1_SIS and ICF_main_PL_pl.json`)

During drafting, blue text contains explanatory instructions, green text contains adaptable examples, and yellow text contains optional questions for checking participant understanding. Adapt the examples to the specific study and remove all unnecessary guidance, examples and unused alternatives before finalisation. Before using the template, remove the section 'Informacje i instrukcje ogólne' and the ABM and PSBK logos. Tables, visit schedules, diagrams, pictures or simple visual aids may be used where helpful to explain procedures, adverse-effect frequencies and sample volumes. The template does not specify fixed font, font size, margins or paragraph spacing.

### Informacja dla przedstawiciela ustawowego i formularz świadomej zgody na udział dziecka w badaniu klinicznym (`L1_SIS and ICF_parents_PL_pl.json`)

During drafting, italic text contains explanatory instructions and adaptable examples that must be removed from the final study document. Address the reader consistently as „Pan/Pani”; do not alternate between „Pan/Pani” and „Państwo”. Refer to the participant as „dziecko” or „dziecko, które Pan/Pani reprezentuje”. Use headings phrased around the child, for example „Co będzie się działo z dzieckiem?”, and avoid wording that makes the parent or guardian appear to be the participant. Use concise tables for schedules, adverse-effect frequencies and signature routes. Clearly separate child identification, representative authority, parental consent declarations, any conditional current-trial sample choice, the formal GDPR notice and investigator confirmation. The two-parent, one-parent joint-decision and sole-representative routes must be visually distinct; retain only the applicable route in the final form. Use diagrams or visual aids where helpful to explain paediatric procedures or sample volumes. Remove general ABM instructions, logos, unused alternatives and examples before use. The source template does not prescribe fixed font, size, margins or paragraph spacing.

### Informacja o badaniu klinicznym dla uczestnika małoletniego (`L1_SIS and ICF_age 6-11_PL_pl.json`)

Stosuj bardzo duże, czytelne nagłówki pytaniowe, krótkie akapity i dużo wolnego miejsca. Każde zdanie powinno przekazywać jedną prostą myśl. Unikaj terminów prawnych, skrótów i słów takich jak „pseudonimizacja”, „administrator danych”, „interwencja”, „zdarzenie niepożądane” lub „randomizacja” bez prostego wyjaśnienia. W razie potrzeby użyj obrazków, symboli, osi czasu, prostego planu wizyt, rysunku sprzętu albo prawdziwej probówki pokazującej ilość pobieranej krwi. Stosuj krótkie listy zamiast rozbudowanych tabel. Instrukcje redakcyjne zapisuj kursywą, a przykładowe brzmienie oznaczaj słowem „Przykład”. Dodawaj proste pytania sprawdzające zrozumienie. Nie stosuj sekcji podpisowej ani checkboxów wyrażających zgodę dziecka.

### Informacja o badaniu i formularz świadomej zgody na udział w badaniu klinicznym dla uczestnika małoletniego (`L1_SIS and ICF_age 13-17_PL_pl.json`)

Stosuj krótkie akapity, jasne nagłówki pytaniowe i bezpośrednie zwroty do nastolatka. Unikaj języka dziecięcego, szkolnych porównań i nadmiernie prawniczego stylu. Używaj tabel tylko wtedy, gdy ułatwiają zrozumienie harmonogramu, procedur albo działań niepożądanych. Instrukcje redakcyjne zapisuj kursywą, a przykładowe brzmienie oznaczaj słowem „Przykład”. Pytania sprawdzające zrozumienie można umieścić w polu informacyjnym. W części podpisowej pozostaw wyłącznie pola uczestnika i osoby uzyskującej zgodę.

### Informacja dla przedstawiciela prawnego i formularz świadomej zgody na udział w badaniu klinicznym (`L1_SIS and ICF_legal rep_PL_pl.json`)

During drafting, blue text contains explanatory instructions, green text contains adaptable examples, and yellow text contains optional questions for checking the representative’s understanding. Adapt examples to the study and remove all drafting guidance, unused alternatives and inapplicable modules before finalisation. Clearly separate participant identification, representative identification, evidence of authority, representative consent and investigator confirmation. Tables, schedules, diagrams, pictures or communication aids may be used to explain study procedures to the representative and, separately, to support the participant’s involvement. Before use, remove the section “Informacje i instrukcje ogólne” and the ABM and PSBK logos. The template does not specify fixed font, font size, margins or paragraph spacing.

### Informacja dla uczestnika i formularz wyrażenia woli udziału w badaniu klinicznym (`L1_SIS and ICF_limited capacity_PL_pl.json`)

Use a spacious, uncluttered layout with short paragraphs, one idea per sentence and clear patient-question headings. Use large readable text and simple tables or visual schedules where helpful. Pictures, symbols, diagrams, demonstrations or communication aids may be added to explain visits, procedures, pain, sample volumes, placebo or randomisation. Avoid dense legal text, long tables and unexplained abbreviations. During drafting, italic and underlined text contains instructions or conditional drafting notes and must be adapted or removed before finalisation. The template does not prescribe a fixed font, font size, margins or paragraph spacing.

### Informacja i formularz odroczonej zgody na dalszy udział w badaniu klinicznym (`L1_SIS and ICF_emergency_PL_pl.json`)

During drafting, italic text contains instructions and adaptable examples that must be removed from the final participant version. Preserve the Polish adult-template layout and national information modules. Use a short, simple two-column table to distinguish activities already performed during the emergency from activities remaining if continued participation is authorised. Include date and time fields where they are relevant to the emergency enrolment and deferred-consent chronology. Keep the participant and representative consent pathways visually separate and retain only the pathway actually used. Tables, diagrams and simplified visit schedules may be used where they improve understanding. The source template does not prescribe fixed font, font size, margins or paragraph spacing.

### Informacja dla uczestnika i formularz świadomej zgody – wersja z udziałem bezstronnego świadka (`L1_SIS and ICF_witness_PL_pl.json`)

During drafting, blue text contains explanatory instructions, green text contains adaptable examples, and yellow text contains optional questions for checking participant understanding. Adapt the examples to the specific study and remove all unnecessary guidance, examples and unused alternatives before finalisation. Before using the template, remove the section “Informacje i instrukcje ogólne” and the ABM and PSBK logos. Tables, visit schedules, diagrams, pictures or simple visual aids may be used where helpful to explain procedures, adverse-effect frequencies and sample volumes. The witness-attestation and investigator-attestation blocks are mandatory in this template and must remain clearly separated. Participant signature fields must state that they apply when the participant is able to sign, and each applicable decision block must provide space to document an approved alternative consent-recording method when the participant cannot sign. The template does not specify fixed font, font size, margins or paragraph spacing.

### Informacja i formularz świadomej zgody na obserwację ciąży i zdrowia dziecka (`L1_SIS and ICF_pregnancy_PL_pl.json`)

Keep this focused supplement substantially shorter than the main adult SIS/ICF. Preserve the Polish template's distinction between drafting instructions and participant-facing text, and remove all instructions, unused alternatives and conditional modules before finalisation. Use short sections, simple tables only where they improve understanding, and clearly separated signature blocks for maternal follow-up and post-birth infant follow-up. Do not reproduce the full protocol schedule, the general adult-trial risk list, the generic insurance/Fund section or the general secondary-use/genetic modules. Add insurance or compensation wording only if this follow-up includes an additional research procedure with its own risk and the wording is applicable. The source template does not impose a fixed font, font size, margins or paragraph spacing.

### Informacja i formularz świadomej zgody na obserwację ciąży partnerki uczestnika badania oraz zdrowia dziecka (`L1_SIS and ICF_pregnant partner_PL_pl.json`)

Keep this focused supplement substantially shorter than the main adult SIS/ICF and aligned in structure and level of detail with the Polish pregnant-participant follow-up template. Preserve the Polish template's distinction between drafting instructions and participant-facing text, and remove all instructions, unused alternatives and conditional modules before finalisation. Use short sections, simple tables only where they improve understanding, and clearly separated signature blocks for maternal pregnancy follow-up and post-birth infant follow-up. Do not reproduce the full protocol schedule, general trial-treatment information, the generic insurance/Fund section or general secondary-use/genetic modules. If additional research procedures are planned, use separate adequate information and consent rather than expanding this limited follow-up form. The source Polish template does not impose a fixed font, font size, margins or paragraph spacing.

### Informacja i formularz świadomej zgody na przyszłe wykorzystanie danych i materiału biologicznego do badań naukowych (`L1_SIS and ICF_secondary_PL_pl.json`)

Keep the document materially shorter than the main SIS/ICF. Preserve the drafting conventions of the Polish source template and remove all instructions, examples and unused options before issue. Use short headings, one lifecycle table, clear YES/NO decisions and separate signature blocks for the optional activity and, where applicable, GDPR consent. The Polish source template does not prescribe fixed typography.
