## 2. Missing participant templates

- **No set at all:** Belgium (French, Dutch and German), Croatia, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Finland (Swedish set only; the Finnish set is there). Each country's population rules list the planned templates as missing.
- **Commonly missing within supplied sets:** information for the youngest children (under 5, 6 or 7 depending on the country), documents for minors after emergency enrolment, witness blocks for a representative who cannot read or write.
- **Cyprus** was not built: there is no Cyprus guide on trialagents.com, and the folder has no participant templates.

## 3. Decisions that recur across countries

1. **Answer language of the shared forms.** Some guides require English (Austria, Greece, Norway; Estonia for the CV), others the national language or either. Each country records its default; confirm per country.
2. **Template file names whose age band differs from the authority.** The authority was followed in the rules and the file kept as supplied (Spain under 12, Netherlands, Ireland, Greece, Denmark, Austria, Czechia, Finland, Sweden, Portugal, Bulgaria, Norway); Poland's file was renamed to 13-17 because its own text already said 13. Decide whether to rename the files to match.
3. **Template text broader than the national rule** (for example the impartial-witness route for people who cannot read, or re-consent at 18 where own consent starts earlier). The national rule is applied in the plan; decide whether to edit the templates.
4. **Interim use of the shared EU form where a national form is required** (Italy P1, Czechia, Romania N1/P1, Spain S1): content is drafted on the EU form but marked not for upload until the national form is supplied.
5. **Facts the guides do not state:** fees, minimum insurance amounts, national contact details for participant documents. Supply them or allow the assistant to ask the user per study.
