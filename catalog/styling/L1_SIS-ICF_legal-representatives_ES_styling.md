The representative information and consent document must not exceed 15 pages including the consent forms; Appendix 1 must not exceed 2 pages. Use Arial or Times New Roman, minimum 11-point font, minimum 1.5 line spacing, and do not reduce the document margins. Use simplified, lay-friendly tables; place the study activity schedule preferably in an appendix and do not reuse protocol tables. Ensure accessible presentation and provide suitable formats or communication support where needed for the participant.
