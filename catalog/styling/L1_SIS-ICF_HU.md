Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the `styling_instructions`, `footer` and `info_table` of each Hungarian participant template (CTIS_HU set, participant templates dated 9 August 2026) and the ETT-KFEB rules recorded in the Hungary guides (reviewed 23 September 2026). Where they are stricter than the base style, these rules win.

## Rules for all Hungarian participant documents

- **Language.** Final, natural Hungarian; no literal translation, no unexplained jargon, abbreviations explained at first use, no references to foreign laws that do not apply in Hungary (ETT-KFEB). Form of address consistent within a document: "Ön" (magázás) where the template requires it (main, healthy, legal representative, parents, emergency and two-witness templates) and consistent formal address in the other adult documents; "tegező" form in the child (6–11) and adolescent (12–17) documents (templates). Terminology identical across ICF, recruitment material, emergency card, pregnancy, genetic and substudy documents.
- **One combined document.** Information and consent form one "Tájékoztatáson alapuló beleegyező nyilatkozat" with **one signature set per document**; never a second signature page for the information part (ETT-KFEB). The legally effective documents keep the KFEB name "Tájékoztatáson alapuló beleegyező nyilatkozat" in their title (templates).
- **Info table** (`Info table`) as listed in each template: A klinikai vizsgálat teljes címe; rövid címe; Protokollszám (verzió és dátum); EUCT szám; Szponzor teljes címmel; Vizsgálatvezető; Vizsgálóhely neve és címe (the Hungarian healthcare provider); A tájékoztatást végző személy neve és beosztása/munkaköre; the participant's name, place of birth and date of birth; where applicable the authorised person's name, place and date of birth and legal status. **Never a participant study code number** in the ICF (ETT-KFEB).
- **Running footer** as in every template: `[document name] - [population label] - 1.0 verzió - [date]` and `Protokollszám: [töltse ki] | EUCT szám: [töltse ki]`; replace the bracket notes with the document's values (version from the plan, not always 1.0) and add the page number in Hungarian ("x. oldal / összesen y"). Version and date must match the Hungarian cover letter and Part II document list.
- **Tables** for visits and procedures where they help (templates); headings and consent choices visually clear (ETT-KFEB QC).
- **Signature section** (`Signature section`): fields for the participant or authorised person (name, signature, date) and for the investigator or person providing the information; all blank in the CTIS copy. Where the oral-consent route can apply: two witness blocks, each with name, signature and address or contact, plus the date of the consent process. Representative blocks only where the legal situation requires them; no general two-parent block (parents template).
- **Optional choices** (genetic testing in its own document; future use; substudy elements) are separate yes/no lines, never pre-ticked, and do not add a second signature set inside one document (secondary-use template).
- **Patient emergency card** is a separate annex to the main ICF, not part of its text (templates; ETT-KFEB). Card layout: the participant's name and date of birth, protocol identification, IMP (and mechanism where needed), indication, contraindications, dangerous interactions, site address, investigator or designated physician and 24-hour telephone number.
- **Privacy** sits inside each participant document. A separate privacy appendix, if used, carries its own participant or healthy-volunteer signature field and is referenced by the main ICF (ETT-KFEB).
- Remove every author instruction, example, unused alternative and conditional module before issue (templates).
