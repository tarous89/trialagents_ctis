- Title box with the document title, then the population or variant as the subtitle (for example "Todos los participantes del estudio").
- Directly under it, the study information table (table style "info"): study title, short title, protocol number with version and date, EU trial number, sponsor with full address, principal investigator, site name, site address.
- One shaded band per section, in the order of the content template. Start with an introduction that invites the reader, then purpose, what taking part means, visits, risks and benefits, voluntariness, data protection, contacts.
- Show the visit schedule as a timeline when there are up to eight visits; add a table of procedures per visit when the detail matters.
- Rights and options as bullet lists; steps the participant takes as numbered lists.
- The consent section ends with signature blocks as a table: participant, person obtaining consent, and, where applicable, legal representative or witness, each with name, date and signature lines.
- Footer line 1: document name, population, version and date. Footer line 2: protocol number and EU trial number.
