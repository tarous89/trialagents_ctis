Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the Finland guides (Tukija template T/447/2026 and guideline T/448/2026 as summarised in https://trialagents.com/ctis/finland/en, reviewed 23 September 2026) and the `styling_instructions` of each Finnish participant template (CTIS_FI_fi set, `FI_13 participant-facing templates_11082026.txt`). Where they are stricter than the base style, these rules win. The same national rules apply to the Swedish package; no Swedish templates were supplied, so Swedish documents keep the layout of the Finnish document they are adapted from.

## Rules for all Finnish and Swedish participant documents

- Language: Finnish and/or Swedish only. Formality and direct address consistent throughout each document and across the family (one form of address).
- Identification: the trial code and a concise, clear Finnish trial title identify each Finnish document; the longer official title may follow as a subtitle (Finnish templates). The Finnish information sheet opens with the heading "TIEDOTE KLIINISESTÄ LÄÄKETUTKIMUKSESTA" before the info table (template `content_instructions`).
- Length: main information sheet about 5 to 8 pages, normally no more than 10; a longer document needs a real complexity justification. Detailed operating instructions go to a separate participant guide.
- Layout: headings, short paragraphs, lists only where useful and enough white space. Clear everyday language; medical or foreign terms defined at first use; consistent units and time periods adapted to Finland.
- Running footer on every page: document type, population, language, version and date, protocol number and EU trial number (template `footer`: "[document name] - [population label] - versio 1.0 - [date]" and "Tutkimussuunnitelman numero | EU-tutkimusnumero"); the same identifiers in filename, change log and CTIS metadata. Page numbering in the document's language (Finnish "Sivu x/y", Swedish "Sida x av y").
- Consent form: separate section starting on a new page; distinct signature and date fields for the participant (or representative) and for the person receiving consent; blank in the CTIS copy. Witness and representative fields only in the documents that use those pathways.
- Optional choices on separate lines, never preselected.
- Easy-language versions, pictures and supported communication where the population needs them (recommended by Tukija); visuals add to the text and never replace required information.
- Final files as PDF or PDF/A; tracked changes, comments, instructions and template prompts removed.
