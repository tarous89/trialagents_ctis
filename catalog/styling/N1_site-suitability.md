  description: 'Please provide a comprehensive written statement on the suitability of the site adapted to the nature and use of the investigational medicinal product. — Instruction: A paragraph in the language of this form version stating and describing to regulators how SITE is suitable to participate in the study. Start by stating the suitability then Include max to 6 bullet points to confirm the suitability based on the specific trial requirements. Keep your bullet points concise and start each with SITE can or SITE is. Do NOT use the exact wording in the task details, instead use study-specific information. Explain in bullet points that the site is suitable to cover the main requirements specific to this study. Focus on on the following aspects adapted to study protocol. a- Care setting & facilities (e.g. inpatient/ outpatient/ specialized unit/ monitoring) b- IMP requirements (e.g. storage/ handling/ preparation/ administration/ accountability) c- Study procedures (e.g., injection/infusion/ sampling/ imaging/ specialized procedures) d- Disease & population expertise (e.g. target condition/population characteristics/ inclusion complexity/ standard-of-care needs) e- Safety & side-effect management (e.g. risks & side effects/ monitoring/ escalation/ emergency) f- Data quality & compliance (e.g. source data / eCRF / archiving). If the answer contains country-level or language specifics, localize your answer to the Member State concerned.'
- id: v01126
  accepts: text
  required: true
  description: 'Please describe in detail the suitability of the facilities — Instruction: A structured section in the language of this form version describing to regulators the suitability of SITE facilities for conduct of the study. Structure the response in up to 9 concise bullet points covering the relevant of the following topics one by one and adapting each point to the protocol requirements rather than answering in generic terms: a) describe the overall care setting and physical infrastructure of the site, including whether the study is supported in an inpatient, outpatient, day-unit, or other relevant setting, and explain why this matches the trial design and participant pathway. 2) describe the available premises, rooms, apparatus, and site equipment relevant to study conduct, and explain how they support the required study visits, assessments, and participant management. 3) describe emergency preparedness, including availability of appropriate emergency equipment, established emergency processes, and access to higher-level care such as ICU where relevant to the study risk profile. 4) explain the site’s arrangements for investigational medicinal product management, including storage, handling, preparation if applicable, administration if applicable, and accountability, and clarify why these arrangements are suitable for the nature and use of the IMP in this protocol. 5) describe laboratory, sample-handling, and testing capabilities relevant to the study, including responsibility for processing samples for clinical chemistry or other protocol-required analyses, and explain how these processes support protocol compliance and sample integrity. 6) confirm that source documents are securely maintained and remain accessible to the investigator during the trial and after study completion in accordance with retention requirements. 7) describe the site’s treatment focus, experience in the trial indication, typical annual patient volume in the target population, and the planned recruitment contribution for this trial, explaining why the site is a good fit for the study population and enrolment strategy. 8) where relevant, explain how the site manages competing studies with similar eligibility criteria and overlapping recruitment periods. 9) confirm that appropriate SOPs and investigator-site quality processes are in place, including informed consent, staff selection and delegation, documentation, data handling, and archiving, and explain how these support reliable and compliant trial conduct. Throughout the response, use study-specific details, clearly link each facility aspect to the actual needs of the protocol, and show not only that the capability exists but why it makes the site suitable for this study. If the answer contains country-level or language specifics, localize your answer to the Member State concerned.'

- id: v01129
  accepts: text
  required: true
  description: 'Please provide a detailed description of Human Resources arrangements and expertise at the site — Instruction: A paragraph in the language of this form version describing to regulators the human resources arrangements and expertise at participating SITE for this study. The output must be detailed yet concise, written in bullet points, and structured. Keep the response valid for the full duration of the trial, including personnel changes. Cover the following points: 1) Staffing concept: minimum physicians, minimum non-physician staff, overall suitability for study complexity. 2) Regulatory readiness: GCP training, regulatory training, protocol-specific training, trial-readiness before task assignment. 3) Required personnel roles. Return distinct personnel roles, not named individuals and not broad grouped departments. Use study-specific wording and explain why each role is a good fit for the protocol. Role examples may include investigator, medically delegated study physician, restricted-delegation team member, non-medical scientific member, study nurse, or study coordinator, where relevant. If the answer contains country-level or language specifics, localize your answer to the Member State concerned.'

Shared EU form used by every country unless the country has its own form. Answer each field in the language of this form version (see the planning checklist row for the country; the form text itself stays as provided). Follow each field's instruction; give the template's "not applicable" answer where a condition is false. Fields for the site or investigator are left for them.
