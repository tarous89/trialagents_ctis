Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the `styling_instructions` of each German participant template (CTIS_DE set, 27 November 2026) and the AKEK guidance summarised in the Germany guides. Where they are stricter than the base style, these rules win.

## Rules for all German participant documents

- Site letterhead (Briefkopf der Prüfstelle) on the information and again on the consent form.
- Running footer with document name, population, version, date, protocol number (Prüfplannummer) and EU trial number (EU-Prüfungsnummer).
- Structure: Kurzdarstellung (short summary), Teil I (procedures and health aspects), Teil II (data protection and samples), then the Einwilligungserklärung (consent form) on a new page.
- The Kurzdarstellung is at most two pages and visually highlighted (`Summary section`).
- Pictograms where they help understanding.
- Study procedures: only study-specific measures, clearly separated from routine care; shown as a table or simple graphic (the templates' "ggf. graphische Darstellung"), with an optional schedule in an annex.
- First use in humans is highlighted.
- Risks with the German frequency categories: sehr häufig (> 10 %), häufig (1–10 %), gelegentlich (0,1–1 %), selten (< 0,1 %).
- Space in the consent form for additions from the consent conversation (Ergänzungen durch die aufklärende Person).
- Name, date, place and signature fields as clearly recognisable form areas with enough space; separate blocks per signer.
- Deviations from the AKEK model text are visibly identifiable (AKEK guideline, 9 April 2026).
