Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the `styling_instructions`, `footer` and `info_table` of each Czech participant template (CTIS_CZ set, participant templates dated 5 August 2026) and the SÚKL requirements recorded in the Czechia guides (KLH-CTIS-01 Version 3, 28 January 2026; KLH-22 Version 5, recommended; guides reviewed 23 September 2026). Where they are stricter than the base style, these rules win.

## Rules for all Czech participant documents

- **Czech only.** No English text and no bilingual layout in the standard Czech submission (SÚKL). A bilingual layout is used only for a certified translation for a foreign national, submitted later as a non-substantial amendment.
- **Length (KLH-22, templates):** adults, parents and legal representatives normally at most 8 pages, 10 only in specific cases; 12–14 years at most 4 pages; 15–17 years may approach adult length (at most 8, exceptionally 10); children 6–11, pregnancy follow-up and the separate future-use document at most 4 pages (template instructions); limited-capacity information shorter than the adult document and in larger type. No annexes (templates).
- **Font and language:** a standard readable font, for example Times New Roman 12 or Arial 11 (templates); larger type where the population has reduced ability to read or understand long documents (KLH-22). Clear chapters and paragraphs; no unexplained abbreviations or specialist terms.
- **Every page** numbered and marked with the document version, issue date and trial identification (SÚKL, KLH-22). Running footer as in every template: `[document name] - [population label] - verze 1.0 - [date]`, `Číslo protokolu: [doplňte] | EU CT číslo: [doplňte]` and `Identifikace klinického hodnocení: [doplňte] | verze dokumentu: [doplňte]`; replace the bracket notes with the document's values (version from the plan, not always 1.0). Page numbering in Czech: "Strana x z y". Versions and dates match the machine-readable document list.
- **Info table** (`Info table`) with: Celý název klinického hodnocení; Zkrácený název klinického hodnocení; Číslo protokolu with version and date; EU CT číslo; Zadavatel with full address; Hlavní zkoušející; Název pracoviště; Adresa pracoviště. The full title and EU CT Number appear in every Czech version (SÚKL).
- **No site letterhead section.** The Czech templates have no `Header` section and the guides require none; do not add or invent a letterhead.
- **No summary box.** The Czech templates have no `Summary section`; none is required by the guides.
- **Visit schedule and procedures** in a clear table (`Standard table`), showing what is additional to routine care.
- **Contacts:** the ethics committee is given by e-mail only (eticka.komise@sukl.gov.cz or the specific committee's e-mail); never an ethics committee telephone number (SÚKL).
- **Optional choices:** no general yes/no tick questions; only genuinely independent optional activities get their own yes/no choice with their own signature and date, never pre-ticked, visually separate from the main consent signature (templates; SÚKL separate-signature rule for future sample use).
- **Signature section:** separate blocks with name, date and signature for each signer (participant or legally designated representative, person conducting the consent interview, independent witness only where the witness pathway applies). The blank CTIS copy carries no signatures. The consent text states that one dated and signed copy (stejnopis) goes to the participant and one stays at the site.
- **Remove before finalising** all instructions for the drafter ("Pokyn pro zpracovatele"), examples, unselected alternatives ("Vyberte a upravte", "Podmíněný text") and non-applicable parts (templates).
