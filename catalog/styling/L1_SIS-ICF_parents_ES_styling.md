The parental information and consent document must not exceed 15 pages including the consent form; Appendix 1 must not exceed 2 pages. Use Arial or Times New Roman, minimum 11-point font, minimum 1.5 line spacing, and do not reduce the document margins. Use simplified, parent-friendly tables; place the study activity schedule preferably in an appendix and do not reuse protocol tables.
