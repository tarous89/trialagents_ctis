Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the Slovenia guides (reviewed 23 September 2026), namely the patient information and consent requirements, the patient-facing document QC checklist and the population guides. No Slovenian participant templates were supplied, so there are no template `styling_instructions`. The national rules below apply to every participant document. Where they are stricter than the base style, they win.

## Rules for all Slovenian participant documents

- Written in Slovenian. Use natural Slovenian terminology and short sentences, and define technical terms. KME RS asks for clear, short, respectful, empathetic information that a lay reader can understand. Headings answer the participant's questions. Keep paragraphs short, and use lists only where they make procedures or risks easier to follow.
- The PIS and ICF may be one integrated document or two coordinated documents (DECISIONS.md D4). Either way, the title, protocol identifier, EU trial number, population, version, date and site contact details are the same in both. Keep the information sheet and the consent choices visually easy to follow. The consent form starts on a new page with the letterhead and the info table (base style).
- Every document has a clear title, population, version number and date. The version and date match in the filename, footer, CTIS record, document list and translated file. The running footer also carries the protocol number and the EU trial number (base style), with the page shown as "Stran x od y".
- Put the trial identity at the start in an `Info table`: full and plain-language title, sponsor, protocol number and EU trial number.
- Show the visit schedule and procedures as a table, with research-only procedures clearly marked apart from standard care.
- Use `Info/warning/note box` elements where they help for voluntariness and withdrawal, the main risks, what to do in an emergency, and pregnancy and contraception warnings.
- Contacts block: give the name, role, address and telephone number of the investigator or qualified contact for questions and urgent medical advice. Add the participant-rights contact routes that KME RS expects, using current official details. These must be easy to find.
- Explain probabilities in plain language and use consistent denominators.
- Consent section: one line per statement (see `drafting_rules`). Each genuinely optional activity gets its own statement with a separate yes/no line, never pre-ticked (`Checkbox group`), and its own signature box where the guide asks for one. A refusal of an optional item must not look like a refusal of the main trial.
- Signature blocks: one block for the participant or legally designated representative and one for the person conducting the interview, each with printed name, role where relevant, signature and date. Witness, adolescent-agreement and representative blocks appear only in the pathway where they apply, and are clearly separated so they cannot be confused. The form states that it is prepared in two copies, one for the participant and one for the trial archive (KME RS).
- Check font size, spacing, contrast, page breaks, tables and how legible the signature page is.
- Final file: Slovenian characters (č, š, ž) render correctly. Text is selectable. No tracked changes, comments, hidden text, unresolved fields or drafting notes. Every cross-reference points to the correct document and section.

## Population-specific rules

- **Healthy volunteers:** no treatment or disease-benefit wording. Show payment separately from expense reimbursement. Do not let payment dominate the layout or the recruitment material.
- **Children under 15:** information suited to the child's maturity and reading ability. Pictures and simple layout are welcome where they help. The assent block is separate from the parent or representative consent and must not look like legal consent.
- **Adolescents 15 to 17:** a document complete enough to support the minor's own agreement. It may mirror the adult PIS in simpler language. The agreement block is separate from the representative's consent block.
- **Adults unable to consent:** participant information suited to the person's understanding, which may use pictures or supported-communication aids. The assent block must not suggest that the participant gives the representative's legal consent.
- **Impartial witness:** a witness statement with the five confirmations from the Slovenia witness guide. Show the witness's printed name, independence statement, signature and date, plus the reason the person cannot write. Add date and time where the sequence matters. Offer accessible formats (large print, audio, screen-reader compatible text) where they allow direct review.
- **Emergency enrolment:** the subsequent information keeps what has already happened separate from what will happen next. Date and time fields are clearly marked.
- **Reproductive-risk acknowledgment:** a signed acknowledgment block for women of childbearing potential, and one for healthy female partners of male participants where relevant (KME RS).
- **Pregnant partner and infant follow-up:** addressed to the partner. Access to medical records is stated clearly. The infant follow-up permission is a separate block.
- **Secondary use:** a separate form, or a clearly separated optional section with its own choice and signature.
