Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the `styling_instructions` of each Greek participant template (CTIS_GR set, `GR_13 participant-facing templates_08082026.txt`) and the Greek National Ethics Committee rules recorded in the Greece guides (reviewed 23 September 2026). Where they are stricter than the base style, these rules win.

## Rules for all Greek participant documents

- **One unified document.** The National Ethics Committee treats the information text and the consent page as one Informed Consent Form (Έντυπο Συναίνεσης μετά από Ενημέρωση), even when a global package separates them. Pagination runs continuously through the whole document, with the total page count: «Σελίδα Χ από Υ». The consent page may start on a new page, but it stays inside the same document and page count.
- **Every page** shows, in the header or footer, the document type (document name), the protocol code or acronym, the version and the date. The templates' footer also names the population and the EU trial number (Αριθμός κλινικής δοκιμής ΕΕ).
- **Type:** readable font of about 12 pt (the templates name Times New Roman 12 or equivalent); this replaces the base style's 11 pt. Larger where the reader needs it (limited capacity, children).
- **Structure:** numbered questions as `H3 module title`, sub-headings as `H4 section title`, following the template order. Short sections, enough white space.
- **Info table** at the top as in each template's `info_table`; site letterhead (`Header`) supplied by the site, never invented. The guides do not require a letterhead.
- **Plain, non-promotional Greek**; abbreviations and medical terms explained; consistent terms.
- **Blood volumes** in ml/cc (ml/κ.εκ.), never in comparisons such as spoonfuls.
- **Risks** by likelihood and seriousness where the evidence supports it. The Greece guides give no fixed frequency categories (see `../DECISIONS.md`).
- **Visit schedules and procedure lists** as tables (base style).
- **Optional choices:** no YES/NO tick boxes and no initials in place of a dated signature. Each genuinely separable optional activity has its own short decision block with the dated signature of the person consenting (or the applicable representative) and of the investigator, and the text makes clear that not signing means no and does not affect the main trial. This replaces the base style's `Checkbox group` for consent choices (templates; DECISIONS.md D4).
- **Signature section:** labelled blank lines with full date (day/month/year) for every signer; time where the template or the pathway needs it (emergency). Separate blocks per signer: participant, both parents (or guardian), legally designated representative, investigator or authorised team member, witness 1 and witness 2, only where the pathway applies (pregnant-partner form: representative and two-witness blocks always). Consent statements stay close to the signature page.
- **Consent visit fields stay blank**; no signature in the CTIS copy.
- **Final PDF** searchable, selectable and correctly rendered.
- **Recruitment and other information material** (K2, L2): same header/footer and pagination rule; factual, no emphasis on payment.
