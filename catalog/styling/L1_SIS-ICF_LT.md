Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the Lithuania guides (reviewed 23 September 2026): patient information and consent requirements, patient-facing document QC checklist and the population guides. They summarise the Lithuanian Bioethics Committee's ICF recommendations, Part II recommendations (17 May 2022, amended 18 October 2022) and FAQ. No Lithuanian participant templates were supplied, so there are no template `styling_instructions`; the national rules below apply to every participant document. Where they are stricter than the base style, they win.

## Rules for all Lithuanian participant documents

- Written in Lithuanian, understandably, avoiding specialist terminology where possible; terms explained; consistent wording, including for risks.
- Short sections, short sentences and headings that make the document easy to scan (base style).
- Trial identity at the start: title, protocol number, sponsor, EU trial number where available (`Info table`).
- Running footer with document name, population, version, date, protocol number and EU trial number; page "Puslapis x iš y". The version number and date match the document register.
- Research-specific procedures visibly distinguished from routine care; visits and procedures as a table (base style).
- Optional choices visibly separate from the main-trial consent, each with its own unticked choice (`Checkbox group`), never pre-selected; a future-use choice is preceded by the information needed to decide.
- A statement that the investigational medicinal product and trial health checks are free of charge, where that applies, placed where the reader sees it with costs and payments.
- Reimbursement and compensation for time shown clearly, with an approximate hourly amount when time is compensated.
- Contacts block: whom to contact about the trial, about participant rights and about research-related injury.
- Consent section on a new page with letterhead and info table (base style). Fields for the participant or consent-giver and for the investigator or authorised research person: name, signature, date and time. Where another person gives consent, a field for the basis of representation.
- A statement that the participant or representative receives a copy, also signed by the investigator or authorised research person.
- Witness fields only in documents for the pathway where a witness may be used.
- Blank in CTIS: no signatures or personal data in the submitted version.
- Filenames and titles distinguish population and use case.

## Population-specific rules

- **Parents of minors:** signature blocks for both parents (the normal Lithuanian pathway), plus a field to document the reason when only one parent signs (exception and its basis). Not an adult ICF with a parent line added.
- **Children aged 12 and above:** written information and an assent form in language suited to age and maturity; the assent block must not look like the legal consent of an adult participant.
- **Children under 12:** oral information is the rule; written child information only where appropriate, short and age-adapted, with pictures where they help.
- **Adults with limited capacity:** participant information adapted to the person's understanding; separate blocks for the legally entitled representative (with the basis of representation) and for the participant's own assent, agreement or objection.
- **Emergency enrolment:** the later document separates what has already happened from what continued participation involves; the right to refuse and the right to object to the use of data already obtained stand out (`Info/warning/note box`).
- **Impartial witness:** blocks for the witness (signature and date), for the person conducting the interview (signature and date) and for recording the participant's consent by the alternative method.
- **Pregnant partner:** addressed to the partner; medical-record access and infant data stated clearly; not mixed with the participant's pathway.
- **Future use:** a separate document, or a separate opt-in box in the consent section with its own yes and no choices.
