Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the Portugal guides (reviewed 23 September 2026: patient information and consent requirements, patient-facing document QC checklist, minor, limited-capacity, witness and healthy-volunteer guides) and the `styling_instructions` of each Portuguese participant template (CTIS_PT set, `PT_12 participant-facing templates_08082026.txt`). Where they are stricter than the base style, these rules win.

## Rules for all Portuguese participant documents

- Language: natural European Portuguese; no participant-facing content left only in English; translated text, numbers, units, dates and contact details checked against the source.
- Identification: the same study title, EU trial number, sponsor, investigator and site details as CTIS, shown in the info table at the start of the information and again on the consent form.
- Running footer with document title, population, version and date, plus protocol number and EU trial number (the templates' footer: `[document name] - [population label] - versão … - [date]` and `Número do protocolo … | Número do ensaio UE …`); page numbering "Página x de y".
- Headers, footers and page numbers consistent across all documents of the package; every document has a unique title, version and date matching CTIS metadata and the filename.
- Readability: short sentences, familiar words, descriptive headings (preferably as the participant's questions), short paragraphs, active sentences and enough white space. Unavoidable medical terms explained immediately at first use.
- Structure: Parte I (Informação) followed by Parte II (Declaração de Consentimento) on a new page; keep the consent declaration short and put the explanation in the information section.
- Visit schedule and procedures as a table (the templates' "Tabela de atividades e procedimentos"), with routine care and study-only procedures distinguished.
- Risks grouped and presented by likelihood and seriousness where reliable information supports it.
- Optional choices: each on its own line with separate yes and no boxes (☐ Sim / ☐ Não), never pre-ticked, visually separate from the main consent.
- Signature blocks: separate signature and date fields for each required signatory (participant, legally authorised representative, person conducting the consent interview, impartial witness) and only those that apply to the document's role and legal pathway; a representative block records the name and the relationship or authority; space for the reason a person could not write and the alternative means of recording consent in witness documents. All blocks blank in the CTIS copy.
- Statement that the participant or representative receives a copy of the signed form.
- Age-specific assent documents suited to the intended child or adolescent; adolescent assent separate and simple; limited-capacity documents adapted to the person's cognitive and communication abilities (large print, easy-read, pictograms where helpful).
- Accessibility: fonts, spacing, contrast, tables and form fields checked; readable printed, on a phone and electronically; accessible formats (large print, audio, easy-read) provide the same approved information.
- Neutral language; no promotional claims (also for recruitment material).
- CEIC model conventions in the templates: bold identifies sections or text whose inclusion is recommended (the suggested text may be adapted); text boxes and square-bracket notes are explanatory drafting notes; underlined italic text marks comprehension-check questions that support the investigator. Notes, text boxes and comprehension-check questions do not remain in the final document (`conventions/template-instructions.md`).
