Applies on top of `content/styles/base.md`. Sources: the `styling_instructions` of each Bulgarian participant template (BG set, supplied 27 November 2026) and the Bulgaria guides (BDA national document list, Ordinance No 31 on Good Clinical Practice, as summarised in the guides reviewed 23 September 2026). Where they are stricter than the base style, these rules win.

## Rules for all Bulgarian participant documents

- **Two language versions:** every participant document is laid out in Bulgarian and as the corresponding Bulgaria version in English. Both versions use the same structure, sections, tables, checkbox lines and signature blocks, and the same document title, version and date, so the Bulgarian text can be assessed against the English one.
- **Running footer** on every page, as in the templates' `footer`: `[document name] - [population label] - версия … - [date]` and `Номер на протокола: … | Номер на клиничното изпитване в ЕС: …`, plus page numbering "Страница x от y" (English version: "Page x of y"). Each recruitment and participant document carries its version and date (BDA).
- **Letterhead:** the Bulgaria guides set no letterhead rule and the Bulgarian templates have no `Header` section. The trial identity goes in the `Info table` at the top (title, short title, protocol number with version and date, EU trial number, sponsor with full address, principal investigator, site name and address, and the further rows of each template's `info_table`). A site letterhead is added only if the site requires it; it is never invented.
- **No summary-length or page limit and no prescribed font:** the templates record that no specific Bulgarian requirement for a maximum number of pages or a font was found. The base style's `Summary section` applies only where a template contains one (the Bulgarian templates do not).
- **Readability (Ordinance No 31):** free of medical jargon; short sections and sentences; terms and abbreviations explained at first use; headings that let the reader find risks, visits, alternatives, withdrawal, contacts and compensation; no dense blocks copied from the protocol; neutral, non-directive tone; benefits not overstated.
- **Tables** only where they improve readability; visit schedules, procedures, risks and sample volumes may be tables (main template).
- **Optional choices:** each truly independent optional activity has its own ДА ☐ / НЕ ☐ line, never pre-ticked; optional future use outside the protocol is a separate document or a clearly separated section with its own signature.
- **Signature blocks:** separate blocks per signer with name, signature and date fields left blank for the consent visit. Minors 14–17: the minor's own signature field (BDA). Parents form: keep only the applicable route (both parents, guardian(s) under 14, or custodian for 14–17, with the documented single-parent exception). Witness blocks only where the witness pathway applies, labelled so a witness block cannot be mistaken for a representative block.
- **Blank review copy:** the CTIS upload contains no completed participant data or signatures, no tracked changes, comments or hidden text, and none of the templates' author instructions (`Инструкция за автора`), conditional-block labels, examples, unselected alternatives or bracket notes.
