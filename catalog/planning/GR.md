Greek exclusions apply (compulsory military service, custodial sentence, barred by court decision, living in institutions).

    - **v114**: Greece: both parents who exercise parental responsibility consent jointly in writing with full dates, or the court-appointed guardian. One parent alone only when the other''s parental responsibility has ended (death, declaration of absence, legal removal), or the other is demonstrably unable to exercise it for factual reasons (formal declaration attached to the consent form) or lacks the relevant legal capacity; day-to-day custody alone is not enough; disagreement goes to the court. From age 10 the minor gives separate written, signed and dated assent after age-appropriate information from the principal investigator, an investigator or an authorised team member trained or experienced with children; below 10 the child is informed according to understanding and a capable child''s refusal is respected. A capable minor''s refusal or withdrawal is binding. Own adult consent when the participant turns 18. The 10th and 18th birthday transitions are operationally defined.

    - **v117**: Greece (JMD G5a/59676, Article 9): a deaf participant or one unable to speak receives the information in writing and asks questions in writing, with at least one independent witness present throughout who co-signs; a participant unable to read or write: the forms are signed by the authorised team member and two independent witnesses. Each witness has legal capacity and is not connected with the trial in any way; independence is confirmed and documented. Alternatively, consent may be recorded by an approved audio or video method with at least one independent witness present, who signs; method, storage, access, privacy and retention are described. An interpreter is not automatically a witness. A witness is never a legal representative.

    - **v121**: Greece (JMD G5a/59676, Article 12): all Article 35 conditions; operational inconvenience, staffing or inability to reach a preferred relative never substitute for the test. For Article 35 only, the legally designated representative may also be the spouse, civil partner, an adult descendant, an ascendant or a sibling, whoever''s informed consent can be obtained most quickly; this definition is not reused for ordinary consent. As soon as possible after the intervention, information and consent for continued participation from the participant or the representative; the participant''s own decision controls once capable. Refusal stops further trial-specific interventions; data and samples already obtained are handled as explained. Emergency minors move to the child and parental pathway as soon as possible; participants under court-appointed support use the judicial supporter when available.

# National rules – Greece

## Authorities and fee

- **competent authority**: EOF (National Organization for Medicines, Εθνικός Οργανισμός Φαρμάκων): Greek national competent authority; issues the national decision
- **ethics**: National Ethics Committee for Clinical Studies (Εθνική Επιτροπή Δεοντολογίας, ΕΕΔ): assesses the ethical aspects of Part II through CTIS
- **fee**: Not stated in the Greece guides (DECISIONS.md, Missing). Proof of fee, where needed, goes in the CTIS Forms section, not Part II (naming guide).

## Populations, age bands and representatives

- **authority**: Greek National Ethics Committee for Clinical Studies (Informed Consent Form guidance and Circular, 11 October 2019; clarification on financial benefits, 22 July 2026); Greek Joint Ministerial Decision G5a/59676, Government Gazette B 4131/22.12.2016 (Articles 2, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 15); Greek Part II Q&A, version 1, 23 November 2022; Regulation (EU) No 536/2014, Articles 28 to 35
- **minors**:
  - **age bands**:
    - **band**: 6-9
    - **template**: GR-PT-age-6-9
    - **reader**: child
    - **assent**: no automatic written assent below 10; child-facing information whenever the child can understand it; the child's agreement is sought and a capable refusal respected
    - **band**: 10-14
    - **template**: GR-PT-age-10-14
    - **reader**: adolescent
    - **assent**: written, signed and dated assent from age 10
    - **band**: 15-17
    - **template**: GR-PT-age-15-17
    - **reader**: adolescent
    - **assent**: written, signed and dated assent
  - **always**:
    - GR-PT-parents
  - **reconsent at 18**: GR-PT-main or GR-PT-healthy: own adult consent for continued participation when the participant turns 18; document the transition promptly
  - **under 6**: No age-band template. Inform the child according to age and maturity whenever the child can understand, seek the child's agreement and respect a capable child's clear refusal; a child-facing sheet only when the protocol population can understand one.
  - **assent from 10**: Mandatory written assent from age 10 (JMD G5a/59676, Article 10); the minor is informed by the principal investigator, an investigator or an authorised team member trained or experienced with children. The minor's signature never replaces the parents' consent.
  - **parents**: Both parents exercising parental responsibility consent jointly (signature and full date for each), or the court-appointed guardian. Single-parent exceptions: the other's parental responsibility ended (death, declaration of absence, legal removal), or the other is demonstrably unable for factual reasons (formal declaration attached) or lacks legal capacity. Day-to-day custody alone is not enough; disagreement goes to the court.
  - **band split**: The guide asks for separate versions when one form cannot serve the whole 10-17 range. The parents template cites the National Ethics Committee's split into 2 or 3 bands by maturity with indicative ranges 10-12, 13-15 and 16-18; the supplied templates give two bands (10-14, 15-17) (DECISIONS.md D5).
  - **conflicts**:
    - Template descriptions say 'aged 12 - 17 years' (10-14 and 15-17 templates) and 'aged 6 - 11 years' (6-9 template); the Greek written-assent threshold of 10 wins. File names and content already follow it.
- **adults without capacity**:
  - **always**:
    - GR-PT-legal-rep
    - GR-PT-limited-capacity
  - **note**: Only adults under court-ordered judicial support covering the case; representative = the court-appointed judicial supporter exercising the care function. The participant's own written agreement when and to the extent able (JMD G5a/59676); capable refusal binding; representative expresses the presumed will. Regained capacity: GR-PT-main. Greek exclusions: compulsory military service, custodial sentence, barred by court decision, living in institutions.
- **emergency**:
  - **always**:
    - GR-PT-emergency
  - **note**: Participant information and subsequent consent (GR-PT-emergency) and a separate emergency-representative information and consent form (no dedicated template; interim basis GR-PT-emergency, DECISIONS.md D3). Article 35 representative list: spouse, civil partner, adult descendant, ascendant or sibling, whoever can consent fastest; Article 35 only. Minor participants move to GR-PT-parents and the age-band forms; adults under court-appointed support to GR-PT-legal-rep.
- **impartial witness**:
  - **always**:
    - GR-PT-witness
  - **note**: One witness for a deaf participant or one unable to speak (written exchange); two witnesses for a participant unable to read or write; audio or video record with at least one witness where approved. For minors the parents' consent and the child's assent still apply.
- **pregnancy**:
  - **participant**: GR-PT-pregnancy when follow-up goes beyond the main trial information
  - **partner**: GR-PT-pregnant-partner whenever identifiable information is collected from the partner or her records
  - **infant**: Both pregnancy templates carry a separate dated infant-data page signed by both parents or guardians and the investigator (pregnancy.infant_follow_up).
- **shareable templates**:
  - GR-PT-pregnant-partner
  - GR-PT-secondary
- **separate document rules**:
  - Optional activities not necessary for the main trial (future research with coded data, long-term storage or future use of samples, optional genetic research, sharing for a new research purpose) need a separate focused Greek form (GR-PT-secondary) or, for optional activities within the protocol, a clearly separate optional choice in the main form; refusal must not affect the main trial.
  - Infant or newborn data need a separate consent from both parents or guardians of the child (a separate dated signature page at the end of the pregnancy form is allowed).
  - A pregnant partner receives her own document; the enrolled participant cannot authorise access to her information.
  - Representative and participant forms for adults under court-appointed support may be integrated when that is clearer; separate forms are the default.
  - Emergency enrolment needs a participant subsequent-consent form and a separate emergency-representative form.

## Drafting rules

- Participant documents follow the Greek National Ethics Committee ICF guidance (11 October 2019) as recorded in the guides; keep the supplied template structure and model text and add every study-specific item. Record any deviation from the model text as a finding.
- Information and consent form one unified, continuously paginated Greek document (Σελίδα Χ από Υ, total page count); every page shows document type, protocol code or acronym, version and date.
- The wording must be accurate, complete and understandable without relying on the oral discussion to repair omissions; plain, non-promotional Greek; abbreviations and medical terms explained.
- State that participants and their public or private insurers do not bear trial-specific costs for investigational or auxiliary products, examinations, procedures or treatment of trial-related injury.
- Privacy information together in one section: controller and DPO contacts, purposes and legal bases, recipients, third-country transfers and safeguards, retention, rights, complaint to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, automated decision-making if any; no promise of deletion that CTR safety, scientific-integrity or retention duties limit; consistent with R1.
- Direct access to medical records by authorised sponsor representatives, EOF, the National Ethics Committee and other competent inspectors; public registration and lay summary of results.
- The National Ethics Committee contact is for ethics questions and is not described as the complaints authority.
- Consent: prior in-person interview by a qualified investigator or authorised team member, paper form, full dates for every signer in the same encounter, signed copy to the participant, original at the site. Blank, unsigned forms in CTIS with every signature and full-date field that could apply; representative and witness fields only where the protocol can use those pathways.
- Main-trial consent separate from optional activities: each genuinely separable optional activity is described fully and recorded with its own dated signature of the person consenting and the investigator, not with a YES/NO box or initials (supplied templates); refusal does not affect the main trial.
- Risks by likelihood and seriousness where the evidence supports it; blood volumes in ml/cc, not in comparisons such as spoonfuls.
- Healthy volunteers: no therapeutic benefit; known human evidence distinguished from animal or laboratory findings; uncertainty not converted into reassurance.
- Do not include groups the Greek implementing decision excludes from clinical trials (compulsory military service, custodial sentence, barred by court decision, living in institutions).
- Insurance and injury-compensation wording matches the certificate, including the pregnancy clause; nothing promised beyond the policy.
- Modifications: final Greek clean version and a tracked-change version against the previously approved form; define which enrolled participants are re-consented and when.
- Participant templates (JSON): italic text, [square-bracket] notes and "..........." gaps are drafting instructions or gaps; follow them and remove them. Part II templates (DOCX): yellow [INSERT/IF/OTHERWISE/CHOOSE/NOTE: …] labels. Nothing of either may remain in a final document.
