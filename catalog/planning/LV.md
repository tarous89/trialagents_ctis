# National rules – Latvia

## Authorities and fee

- **competent authority**: ZVA (Zāļu valsts aģentūra, State Agency of Medicines of Latvia); coordinates the national assessment and issues the decision.
- **ethics**: ZVA assigns a medicinal-product clinical-trial ethics committee, which assesses the ethical aspects and prepares the Part II assessment report.
- **data protection authority**: Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija); its contact details are required in the main consent set.
- **fee**: Not stated in the Latvia guides (DECISIONS.md, Missing).

- **authority**: ZVA (State Agency of Medicines of Latvia) and the medicinal-product clinical-trial ethics committee ZVA assigns; Cabinet Regulation No. 192 (26 March 2024), paragraphs 28 to 34 and 46 to 47; Law on the Rights of Patients, Sections 7, 10, 11 and 13; Regulation (EU) No 536/2014 Articles 28 to 35; Overview Part II Requirements, Latvian Local Requirements, version 2.0 (4 February 2025); as recorded in the Latvia guides reviewed 23 September 2026.
- **template status**: missing: no Latvian participant templates were supplied. Every template id below is a planned id from participant_templates_missing, with no file ("template": "missing" in population_sets).
- **minors**:
  - **age bands**:
    - **band**: 0-13
    - **template**: LV-PT-age-0-13
    - **template status**: missing
    - **reader**: child
    - **basis**: Latvia minors guide: under 14, legal-representative written consent; child informed according to age and maturity; child information or assent document when the child can understand; the younger child's own agreement recommended depending on maturity.
    - **condition**: child can understand the trial
    - **band**: 14-17
    - **template**: LV-PT-age-14-17
    - **template status**: missing
    - **reader**: adolescent
    - **basis**: Latvia minors guide and Part II checklist: from age 14 until the 18th birthday the minor's own written consent and the legal representative's written consent are both required (Cabinet Regulation No. 192, paragraphs 28 to 30; Law on the Rights of Patients).
  - **always**:
    - LV-PT-legal-rep-minor
  - **band rule**: The two national bands are fixed by Latvian law (written consent of the minor from 14). The under-14 child document may be split further by maturity when the protocol's age range is wide (DECISIONS.md D4).
  - **band conflict**: None: no Latvian templates were supplied, and the hub, Part II checklist, minors guide and QC checklist all use 0 to 13 and 14 to 17.
  - **representatives**: The legal representative recognised under Latvian law gives written consent; authority is documented at the site. The national rule uses the singular legal representative and does not require two parental signatures; no universal two-parent rule without a trial-specific legal basis. Shared custody, guardianship, disagreement or unavailable documentation handled by a site process planned before recruitment (DECISIONS.md D5).
  - **assent dissent**: Minor involved according to age and maturity; a capable adolescent's refusal means no enrolment; a younger child's verbal or behavioural objection is taken seriously; the explicit wish of a minor able to form an opinion to refuse or withdraw is respected (Article 32). Rules for necessary medical treatment do not remove these research protections. A young child's signature never substitutes for the legal representative's consent; an assent form must not imply legal consent.
  - **adolescent information**: Direct and understandable, not a copy of the parent document with minor wording changes; confidentiality boundaries, pregnancy and contraception where relevant, data and sample use, withdrawal and contacts.
  - **reconsent at 14**: LV-PT-age-14-17: the minor's written consent for continued participation alongside the legal representative's consent.
  - **reconsent at 18**: LV-PT-main or LV-PT-healthy (current adult version) as soon as possible; earlier representative permission is not adult consent.
  - **payment**: Reimbursement of expenses and loss of earnings only; no financial inducement (CTR).
- **adults without capacity**:
  - **always**:
    - LV-PT-legal-rep
    - LV-PT-limited-capacity
  - **decision makers**: Patient unable to express wishes and not under a relevant court arrangement: the patient's authorised person, spouse, partner or closest relative (Law on the Rights of Patients), in writing, and only if the patient did not previously object. Under guardianship or trusteeship: the lawful representative, or the guardian together with the patient according to the court-defined restriction of legal capacity. Lawful person confirmed for the individual case; no generic hierarchy copied into the consent form; no enrolment while authority is uncertain.
  - **note**: Not used merely because of a diagnosis, disability, low literacy or temporary communication difficulty; capacity assessed for the specific decision, with support or an impartial witness where the person can consent. Objection, including non-verbal resistance, respected and not overridden by the representative. Own written consent with LV-PT-main when capacity is regained; optional data and sample choices revisited.

    - LV-PT-emergency-legal-rep
  - **optional**:
    - LV-PT-emergency-initial
  - **note**: All Article 35 conditions; no Latvian national emergency-consent form. Prior objection excludes the route. Subsequent consent without undue delay; the participant's own consent when capacity returns; no promise of automatic deletion of legally required safety or trial records.
- **impartial witness**:
  - **always**:
    - LV-PT-witness
  - **note**: Participant can consent but cannot write; at least one impartial witness present; not for a language barrier and not instead of a legal representative. The witnessed consent remains the record if the barrier resolves.

  - **partner**: LV-PT-pregnant-partner whenever partner data are collected.

- **secondary use**:
  - **always**:
    - LV-PT-secondary
  - **note**: Separate section or document; refusing does not prevent participation or affect care; withdrawal distinguishes stopping new use, destroying remaining samples where feasible, retaining results already generated, use after irreversible anonymisation and legally required retention.
- **non latvian speakers**: If a participant group cannot understand Latvian, plan a suitable additional language version and consent process (study fact population.non_latvian_speakers); no unapproved ad hoc translation. The guides do not require or mention Russian-language documents (DECISIONS.md D2).
- **shareable templates**:
  - LV-PT-pregnant-partner
  - LV-PT-secondary
- **shareable note**: One shared document for several populations only when nothing in it would differ; a secondary-use document for minors or represented adults needs representative and age-appropriate elements and is not shared with capable adults. Agree with the user (DECISIONS.md D16).
- **separate document rules**:
  - A separate document or clearly tailored version for each population and consent pathway in the protocol (Latvia patient information and consent guide).
  - Pregnant-participant and pregnant-partner pathways are separate because the partner is a separate data subject.
  - Main-trial consent is separate from optional future research, optional biopsies, recordings, genetic analysis beyond the trial purpose and re-contact.
  - Healthy volunteers: separate consents for optional genetic work, future use, pregnancy follow-up, recordings or other activities not necessary for the main trial; screening consent scope made clear or a separate screening consent.
  - Adults unable to consent: separate legal-representative document and participant information.
  - Minors 14 to 17: the minor's own consent and the legal representative's consent, as coordinated documents or a combined set with separate signature lines.
  - Re-consent: at 14 (minor's own consent), at 18 and when capacity is regained (participant's own adult consent), and after new information that may affect willingness to continue.

## Drafting rules

- Participant documents are in Latvian, concise and understandable to the intended population; plain Latvian, short sentences, familiar terms, abbreviations defined at first use, technical detail that does not help the decision removed.
- Document identity: descriptive title, version and date, sponsor protocol number, EU CT number, study-centre name and address, data-controller identity, and contact details for ZVA, the assigned ethics committee and the Data State Inspectorate (Latvia national overview). Controlled fields only where the final site or committee detail is not yet known; completed before use.
- The information allows a reasonable person to decide without the protocol: title, purpose, research nature, sponsor, responsible site; why invited and expected number of participants; groups, randomisation, placebo, blinding and chance of assignment; visits, procedures, samples, questionnaires, recordings and total period; risks, burdens, side effects, reproductive risks and risk control; expected benefit (clear statement when none is expected); alternatives and usual care; pregnancy testing, contraception, breastfeeding and pregnancy reporting; sample purpose, destination, storage, coding, transfer and future use; personal-data use, recipients, transfers, retention, safeguards and rights; expenses, payments, free trial medicines and procedures, insurance and injury arrangements; voluntariness, withdrawal and its consequences, treatment after withdrawal; new information; contacts for trial questions, urgent medical issues, participant rights, privacy, ZVA and the ethics committee.
- Latvian rules: the investigational medicinal products, the devices needed to administer them and the other medicines, devices and examinations specified in the protocol are provided free of charge; state this where it helps the participant understand costs, consistently with the payment and insurance sections.
- Consent statements are specific; the mandatory agreement to participate is separate from optional activities; refusing an optional choice does not block participation unless the protocol makes the activity scientifically necessary and the information explains this; no bundling of trial participation with broad unrelated data or sample use.
- Signatures at consent: the participant or authorised decision-maker and the person conducting the consent interview sign and date; impartial witness only in the witness pathway; minors from 14 sign their own consent; copy of the signed documentation given. Blank CTIS copies carry no signatures or personal data.
- Withdrawal from participation is explained separately from withdrawal of optional future-use permission; what happens to data and samples already collected is stated without promising deletion where lawfully retained trial records must remain.
- Privacy: controller and processors identified without confusing sponsor, site and investigator roles; data categories, purposes, recipients, retention, international transfers, safeguards and rights consistent with R1; Data State Inspectorate contact; direct access to medical records for authorised persons explained; consent to participate is not presented as the sole legal basis for every processing activity; privacy contacts separate from medical-emergency contacts.
- Samples: coded or pseudonymised samples and data are never called anonymous; genetic analysis and return of individual or incidental findings stated; withdrawal wording aligned with S1, the protocol and the sample-management plan.
- Reimbursement and payment wording matches P1 and the draft site budget; injury and insurance wording matches the Latvia-compliant insurance evidence (Cabinet Regulation No. 192).
- Healthy volunteers: payment not contingent on finishing in a way that discourages withdrawal; state what is payable after screen failure, early withdrawal, investigator discontinuation or trial suspension. Minors, incapacitated participants and breastfeeding women receive only reimbursement of expenses and loss of earnings.
- Changes during the trial: new information that may affect willingness to continue is given promptly and voluntary written consent to continue is obtained; a material change goes through a substantial modification before implementation unless an urgent safety measure applies.
- Final native-language proofread after all content changes; do not rely only on back-translation or automated checks.
- Participant templates were not supplied; where a reference document or a later template is used, its instructions ([square-bracket] notes, italic notes, '...........' gaps) and the Part II form instructions (yellow [INSERT/IF/OTHERWISE/CHOOSE/NOTE: ...] labels) are drafting instructions; none may remain in a final document.
