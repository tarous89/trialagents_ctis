  national_notes: 'CTIS title: K1_Recruitment arrangements. Signed at CTIS submission: no

    How potential participants are identified, approached, informed and consented, with the Croatia-specific parts completed and a description of who conducts the interview (a physician or dentist who is the principal investigator or a delegated investigator). Every population pathway in the plan (minors, adults unable to consent, impartial witness, emergency) has its section completed; the detailed emergency enrolment procedure and its Croatian decision sequence are described here.

    National additions to the form: - **v105**: Required in Croatia: in Croatian, state that every advertisement, invitation, social-media item, poster, telephone script, letter or other recruitment material that potential participants will see or hear is submitted in its final proposed Croatian version under Recruitment arrangements, carries a version and date, and is evaluated by the Central Ethics Committee case by case; for healthy volunteers, describe prescreening, databases and repeat-participation checks.

    - **v108**: Required in Croatia: in Croatian, state that the informed-consent interview is conducted by a physician or dentist who is the principal investigator or an investigator to whom the principal investigator has delegated the activity, with the delegation documented before the person performs it; that consent is obtained in person on paper from the participant (or parent, guardian or legal representative) before any trial-specific procedure, using one unified Croatian informed consent document (Informirani pristanak), submitted blank in CTIS; that the participant and the interviewer sign and date it and the participant receives a signed copy; that routine electronic consent is not used (electronic consent only in exceptional situations, followed by in-person paper consent as soon as possible); and that trial health information is obtained from the participant or legal representative, not by asking for the family physician''s contact details or contacting other healthcare professionals. Where they apply, state that a required caregiver or study partner and a named emergency contact give their own consent, and that home delivery of investigational medicine has a separate consent.

    - **v110**: Required in Croatia when adults unable to consent are included: in Croatian, state that consent is given by the guardian or legal representative appointed by a court, whose appointment and scope are verified before enrolment with the evidence retained at the site (a spouse, adult child, caregiver, study partner or family member is not automatically the legal representative); that the participant receives Croatian information suited to their ability and assent is recorded where appropriate; that the explicit wish of a participant capable of forming an opinion to refuse or withdraw is respected; when capacity is reassessed and by whom if it fluctuates; that the participant''s own consent is obtained when capacity is gained or regained; and, for trials in people with mental disorders, how the Central Ethics Committee restriction is addressed (people under guardianship who cannot decide independently may not take part in trials of psychopharmaceuticals; trials in people with mental disorders should plan to include adults with legal capacity; written advice from the Ministry of Health and the Central Ethics Committee sought beforehand).

    - **v114**: Required in Croatia when minors are included: in Croatian, state the assent age bands used (4 to 7, 8 to 11, 12 to 14 and 15 to 17 years, with a separate Izjava o pristanku za sudjelovanje u kliničkom ispitivanju for each band recruited); that one parent signs (Central Ethics Committee guidance provides for one parent signature field) or, if the child has no parent, the guardian or legally appointed representative appointed by a court, whose authority is verified before enrolment; that the child receives information suited to age and mental maturity from an investigator or trial-team member trained or experienced in working with children, while the informed-consent interview is conducted by a physician or dentist who is the principal investigator or a delegated investigator; that a minor who can form an opinion and evaluate the information signs alongside the legal representative, that a minor takes part only with a signature or oral assent, and how understanding is assessed and oral assent documented (silence or failure to resist is not assent); that the child''s refusal or later wish to stop is decisive; and that the participant''s own adult consent is obtained after turning 18, including consent for continued storage and use of samples.

    - **v117**: Required in Croatia when an impartial witness may be needed: in Croatian, state that a witness is used only when the participant or legal representative can understand and decide but cannot write (not because the person cannot read Croatian, has low literacy, lacks capacity, is unconscious or is in an emergency); how decision-making ability and the reason a conventional signature cannot be used are confirmed and documented; how the witness''s independence from the sponsor, investigator and trial team is checked (no subordinates of the investigator, recruiters or persons paid for trial performance; family members are not automatically impartial); that the witness is present for the whole consent process and signs and dates the witness attestation; that the physician or dentist interviewer signs and dates; which approved alternative means records the decision; that a translator is not automatically the impartial witness; and that no remote witness or electronic signature is used unless a specific exceptional process has prior approval and later paper consent is planned.

    - **v121**: Required in Croatia when emergency enrolment is used: in Croatian, explain every Article 35 condition for the specific qualifying condition and therapeutic window; the Central Ethics Committee decision sequence, read together with Article 35 (consent is first sought from a parent, guardian or legally appointed representative if the person has one who can be contacted; otherwise a family member who is present may act in the described pathway; otherwise the investigator decides when the investigator is also the treating physician), with each decision-maker''s role, authority and order; the prior-objection check (available records, advance directives where relevant, information from people present) without delaying care; that the emergency enrolment decision is not informed consent and is followed as soon as possible by information and in-person paper consent of the participant, or of the legally designated representative if the participant remains unable to decide; how exact dates, times, contact attempts, decision-maker, capacity changes and signatures are documented; and that later refusal stops future trial interventions under the approved withdrawal and safety process.

# National rules – Croatia

## Authorities and fee

- **competent authority**: Ministry of Health (Ministarstvo zdravstva): receives the application in CTIS and issues the decision after obtaining the Central Ethics Committee's opinion.
- **ethics**: Central Ethics Committee (Središnje etičko povjerenstvo, SEP): performs the ethical review and gives its opinion to the Ministry of Health.
- **fee**: Not stated in the Croatia guides (DECISIONS.md, Missing).
- **site start**: A Croatian site may start only after the site agreement has been signed, the Central Ethics Committee has issued a positive opinion and the Ministry of Health has authorised the trial. Liability insurance for trial-related injury, death or treatment is in place before the trial starts.
- **national guidance**: Croatian Act implementing Regulation (EU) No 536/2014, Official Gazette 14/2019 (7 February 2019), Articles 6 to 16; Central Ethics Committee national specifics related to CTIS, Version 1, February 2022; Central Ethics Committee detailed instructions to applicants, Version 3; Central Ethics Committee application forms (accessed 31 August 2026); Croatian Ordinance on clinical trials of medicinal products and good clinical practice, Official Gazette 25/2015 with the 2021 amendment, Official Gazette 32/2021; Croatian Family Act, Official Gazette 103/2015, Article 26; EudraLex Volume 10 including the July 2026 CTR Q&A; CTIS Sponsor Handbook (17 July 2026); Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).

## Drafting rules

- Participant documents are in Croatian, professionally translated and proofread, locally adapted rather than a literal translation of a global English template; Croatia-specific authority, cost, insurance, privacy and contact information replaces country-neutral wording; no phrases such as 'in your country' or 'in some countries'.
- The document title includes the Croatian term Informirani pristanak; the title and text do not use vague labels such as information, form or document instead of that term.
- The main consent is one unified document with all participant information before the signature section; never an information sheet plus a signature-only form. Basic trial and sponsor details in a first-page header; header and footer in Croatian.
- Address the participant directly with the formal pronoun Vi and its forms capitalised (Vi, Vas, Vam, Vaš), avoiding excessive repetition. State visits and weeks with Croatian ordinal numbers. Explain medical terms in language the intended participant understands; complete but not unnecessarily long.
- Cover, in an order a participant can follow: title, sponsor, purpose, phase where useful and why the person is invited; planned number of participants globally and in Croatia; voluntariness and the right to refuse or withdraw without loss of ordinary care; design, groups, randomisation, blinding, investigational medicine, comparator and placebo (a substance without the active trial medicine, not a 'false medicine'); every procedure; duration, visits, restrictions, hospital stays and what happens after treatment stops; foreseeable risks, burdens, reproductive risks and unknowns; expected benefit or clear statement that none is expected; alternatives; new information, suspension, early termination and withdrawal.
- Contraception: where reproductive effects are unknown or potentially harmful, both partners use reliable contraception (for example hormonal contraception plus condoms), with every acceptable method and period defined; state what happens if the participant or a partner becomes pregnant and that a separate follow-up consent will be offered.
- State that the sponsor bears all protocol-related trial costs, including treatment, procedures, tests, visits and treatment of trial-related injury; describe travel and other expense reimbursement with the maximum net amount in euros where required by the arrangement; payment is not a medical benefit or a penalty for withdrawal; amounts match the financial documents.
- Insurance and the process for reporting and seeking help for a trial-related injury match the insurance policy.
- Contacts: medical questions and emergencies, participant rights, data protection and complaints. Trial questions go first to the investigator, then the trial lead. The only Central Ethics Committee information normally needed is its role, that it approved the trial once approval has been obtained, and its address for written questions about participant rights. Include a route to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).
- Privacy: controller and representatives with usable contacts, purposes, data and health-data categories, recipients and vendors, coding, access, international transfers, retention, safeguards, rights and complaint routes; consent to trial participation is not presented as the legal basis for every processing operation; explain which data may continue to be used after withdrawal and why.
- Health information is obtained from the participant or legal representative. Do not ask for the family physician's contact details or contact other healthcare professionals for health information; remove global clauses authorising unrestricted direct access to outside physicians. Encourage the participant to inform the family physician and offer a physician letter; the decision and communication stay with the participant.
- Optional choices are distinct from the main decision; each offers both DA and NE; refusing an optional activity does not prevent main-trial participation unless the protocol genuinely requires it. An optional pharmacogenetic or other substudy has a separate Croatian informed consent; a required, integral analysis is explained in the main consent.
- Biological samples: type, quantity, schedule and purpose; required or optional; who collects, controls, processes and receives; storage location and duration; coding or anonymisation (never 'anonymous' if a re-identification key remains); permitted uses and users; transfers outside Croatia or the EEA; commercial use; return of results; withdrawal and what cannot be reversed; final handling.
- Caregiver or study partner, named emergency contact, home delivery of investigational medicine and other third parties: separate consent for each; no tracing agency or disclosure to an outside party for tracing; home-delivery, home-nursing, transport, reimbursement and technology vendors receiving identifiable data are identified with their role and privacy arrangements.
- Signature section: participant (or parent, guardian or legal representative) and the physician or dentist conducting the interview (principal investigator or delegated investigator) sign and date, in person on paper, before any trial-specific procedure; the participant receives a signed copy; witness, representative and assent fields only in the pathway where they apply, correctly labelled; date and time fields where the protocol or emergency process needs exact sequence evidence.
- Routine electronic consent is not proposed; electronic consent only in exceptional situations, followed by in-person paper consent as soon as possible.
- Participant-completed questionnaires and rating scales are validated and translated into Croatian; for an instrument that is not scientifically validated, submit the English original and the Croatian translation.
