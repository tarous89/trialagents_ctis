# National rules – Hungary

## Authorities and fee

- **competent authority**: NNGYK (National Center for Public Health and Pharmacy, Nemzeti Népegészségügyi és Gyógyszerészeti Központ); competent authority for clinical trial authorisation in Hungary
- **ethics**: ETT-KFEB (Clinical Pharmacology Ethics Committee of the Medical Research Council, Egészségügyi Tudományos Tanács Klinikai Farmakológiai Etikai Bizottsága) assesses the Part II ethical content and publishes Hungary-specific Part II requirements, Q&A and templates (CV, site suitability)
- **fee**: The Hungary guides do not describe the fee route or proof of payment (DECISIONS.md, Missing).

## Language rules

- **participant documents**: hu
- **administrative documents**:
  - hu
- **rule**: Final SIS/ICF and every other participant-facing document for Hungarian participants, and recruitment material, are in final Hungarian form. The cover letter and the list of Part II documents are in Hungarian (ETT-KFEB). ETT-KFEB checks linguistic quality and warns against literal translations, inconsistent forms of address and references to foreign laws that do not apply in Hungary.
- **administrative note**: The guides state no language rule for the other administrative documents (K1, M1, M2, N1, P1, P2). They are set to Hungarian, matching the supplied CTIS_HU template set and variable sheet, pending DECISIONS.md D1. Third-party documents (insurance, GCP certificate, protocol signature pages) are checked as issued.
- **translation**: A translation must be clinically accurate and locally natural; replace foreign legislation with the Hungarian and EU rules that apply; keep terminology consistent across ICF, recruitment material, emergency card, pregnancy, genetic and substudy documents. A literal translation of an adult global ICF is not acceptable for a child-facing document.
- **form answers**: Part II forms (shared EU templates in content/eu/templates/part-ii) are answered in Hungarian (DECISIONS.md D1).
- **drafting notes language**: drafting_notes_hu for every document.

## Answer languages for forms

- hu

## Populations, age bands and representatives

- **authority**: ETT-KFEB (Clinical Pharmacology Ethics Committee of the Medical Research Council): current Part II requirements and Q&A, GDPR recommendation of 14 March 2023, patient emergency card requirements of 15 December 2015; Act CLIV of 1997 on Health (Eütv.) Sections 16, 159, 160, 161; Act XXI of 2008 on human genetic data (Section 8); Decree 35/2005 (VIII. 26.) EüM; Medical Research Council Bioethics Code 2022; as recorded in the Hungary guides reviewed 23 September 2026.
- **minors**:
  - **age bands**:
    - **band**: 6-11
    - **template**: HU-PT-age-6-11
    - **reader**: child
    - **band**: 12-17
    - **template**: HU-PT-age-12-17
    - **reader**: adolescent
  - **always**:
    - HU-PT-parents
  - **band rule**: The official Hungary Part II sources publish no fixed national assent age-band table ('do not invent one'). The template bands 6-11 and 12-17 are drafting bands; per study, create child and adolescent versions from the protocol population, developmental ability and the CTR requirement for age- and maturity-appropriate information, defensible to ETT-KFEB. A short child sheet may suit younger children; adolescents may need a document closer to the adult information. (DECISIONS.md D14).
  - **band conflict**: None: the authority sets no bands, so the template bands are kept as drafting bands (recorded in templates/README.md).
  - **age 16**: Minors aged 16 or older may designate who exercises healthcare consent or refusal rights; this is not automatic independent clinical-trial consent.
  - **age 14 template rule**: The 12-17 and genetic templates record the own consent of a minor aged 14 or older together with the representative's consent; the guides state this only for genetic consent of minors with limited capacity (DECISIONS.md D12).
  - **reconsent at 18**: HU-PT-main or HU-PT-healthy, as soon as required by law and the approved process when participants reach adulthood during the trial; also reassess when legal status or capacity changes earlier.
  - **under 6**: No template. Inform the child in a form appropriate to age and maturity where the child can understand, and document the child's involvement (DECISIONS.md D13).
  - **payment**: No financial compensation for participation (Bioethics Code); only documented expenses.
  - **genetics**: Genetic consent of a minor with limited capacity is made with the legal representative's consent or later approval; no sampling before approval.
- **adults without capacity**:
  - **always**:
    - HU-PT-legal-rep
  - **as applicable**:
    - HU-PT-limited-capacity
  - **note**: Representative per the Section 16 Health Act order, authority verified by the site, identity details in the ICF. Participant information adapted to ability; opinion considered to the professionally possible extent; refusal respected. Own consent when capacity is regained. Genetic consent per Act XXI of 2008. No payment for participation.
- **emergency**:
  - **always**:
    - HU-PT-emergency
  - **note**: Section 160 Health Act and Article 35 CTR. Representative documents (HU-PT-legal-rep) where a representative consents after enrolment; participant's own consent once capable.
- **impartial witness**:
  - **always**:
    - HU-PT-witness
  - **note**: Two witnesses, not interested in or participating in the research, with address or contact; only for a capable person unable to write or illiterate.
- **pregnancy**:
  - **participant**: HU-PT-pregnancy when the protocol or safety plan collects pregnancy, maternal, outcome or infant information beyond what the main ICF explains; otherwise the main ICF explains follow-up.
  - **partner**: HU-PT-pregnant-partner when identifiable pregnancy or medical information is collected from a participant's partner; she signs her own consent.
- **genetic testing**:
  - **always**:
    - HU-PT-genetic-testing
  - **note**: Separate genetic information and consent for every population with planned genetic testing, mandatory or optional; never merged into the main ICF.
- **shareable templates**:
  - HU-PT-pregnant-partner
  - HU-PT-secondary
- **shareable note**: One shared document for several populations only when nothing in it would differ; future-use consent from minors or represented adults needs the representative logic.
- **separate document rules**:
  - Main information and consent: one combined document per population with one signature set (ETT-KFEB).
  - Genetic information and genetic consent: separate document(s), mandatory or optional genetic testing (ETT-KFEB; Act XXI of 2008).
  - Each distinct data subject or separate consent decision (minor and parent, representative, healthy volunteer, pregnant partner, substudy) gets its own document with the privacy information relevant to that person, or a clear reference to the main document where ETT-KFEB allows it (ETT-KFEB GDPR recommendation).
  - Optional future use: a separate or clearly separable choice; renewed consent for non-anonymous secondary use not explained at first use (ETT-KFEB).
  - Patient emergency card: final Hungarian card as an addendum to the main ICF for applicable trials (not Phase IV or bioequivalence).
  - A separate privacy appendix, if used, has its own signature field and is referenced by the main ICF.

## Drafting rules

- Participant documents follow the supplied HU templates and the ETT-KFEB rules recorded in the Hungary guides; templates are used as supplied, and every study-specific addition is made in final, natural Hungarian.
- Main information and consent: one combined 'Tájékoztatáson alapuló beleegyező nyilatkozat' per population with one signature set; do not duplicate signature pages.
- Consent record: participant's name, place and date of birth; identity details of the Section 16 authorised person where applicable; the participant's study code number never appears in the ICF.
- Identify the trial (EU CT number, protocol number, title), the Hungarian healthcare provider and the PI or person providing the information by name and role.
- Insurance: insurer's name, Hungarian contact, contact person and telephone number, and how to seek treatment and compensation for trial-related injury (Act XCV of 2005, Section 21); never a generic statement that the sponsor is insured.
- Reimbursement: list exact documented cost types; no 'may reimburse' wording; separate participation payments (only within the Bioethics Code) from expense reimbursement; describe any third-party reimbursement service, its fees and data flows.
- Privacy integrated into each participant document: data categories, coded/pseudonymised/anonymous used consistently, access to identifiable records at the site only, recipients, storage period and location, third-country transfers and safeguards, purposes, legal basis (consent as expected by ETT-KFEB, checked against the sponsor's GDPR analysis), rights and NAIH contact.
- Genetic testing: separate genetic information and consent documents; the main ICF only announces it.
- Optional decisions (genetics, future use, substudies) are separate from the main participation decision; refusal never blocks main participation.
- Two-witness oral-consent route with both witnesses' signature and address or contact where a capable participant cannot read or write.
- Patient emergency card for applicable trials, announced in the ICF, carried by the participant; identity plus study code on the card only with a stated patient-safety reason and agreement.
- Use Hungarian and EU rules only; remove references to foreign laws; consistent form of address (Ön in adult documents, tegező in child and adolescent documents as the templates set).
- Participant templates (JSON): italic text, [square-bracket] notes and "..........." gaps are drafting instructions or gaps; follow them and remove them. Part II templates (DOCX): yellow [INSERT/IF/OTHERWISE/CHOOSE/NOTE: …] labels. Nothing of either may remain in a final document.
