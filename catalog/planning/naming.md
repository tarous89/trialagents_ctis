Every document in a plan has a regulatory code, a name and a filename.

- **Code**: the Annex I section letter and number, then a short name, as used in the planning checklist: `K1_recruitment-arrangements`, `L1_SIS-ICF`. Part I documents use their Part I codes in the same way.
- **Variant**: the population or version the document is for, in lower case: `adults`, `minors-12-17`, `pregnant-partners`, `healthy-volunteers`. Leave it empty when there is only one version.
- **Name**: the plain document name in English, then the variant: "Subject information sheet and informed consent form – adults".
- **Filename**: `<study code>_<code>[_<variant>]_<country>_<language>`, without spaces, for example `EX101_L1_SIS-ICF_adults_ES_es` or `EX101_K1_recruitment-arrangements_DE_de`. Part I documents use `EU` as the country. The version is added when files are produced (`_v2`).
- Keep codes, variants and filenames stable across versions and across a duplicated project, so reviewers can follow a document from one submission to the next.
