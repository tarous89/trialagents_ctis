# National rules – Romania

## Authorities and fee

- **competent authority**: ANMDMR (National Agency for Medicines and Medical Devices of Romania) coordinates the national procedure and communicates the final decision through CTIS.
- **ethics**: CNBMDM (National Bioethics Committee for Medicines and Medical Devices) performs the ethical assessment: participant information and consent, recruitment, investigator and site suitability, insurance, compensation and financial arrangements.
- **fee**: Order 3390/2022 includes a Romanian payment commitment form for ANMDMR invoices, required where the national process requests it (RO-FEE, CTIS Forms section). Check the current ANMDMR clinical-trials pages before submission; the guides give no amounts.
- **legal basis**: Regulation (EU) No 536/2014; Government Emergency Ordinance 29/2022; Ministry of Health Order 3390/2022 (Annexes 1 to 10, national Part II forms); CNBMDM Regulation (Ministry of Health Order 80/2023).

## Language rules

- **participant documents**: ro
- **administrative documents**:
  - ro
  - en
- **rule**: Documents intended for participants in Romania, including recruitment materials, must be in Romanian. Government Emergency Ordinance 29/2022 allows documentation not intended for participants to be submitted in English.
- **non romanian speakers**: If people who do not speak Romanian may be enrolled, K1 explains how valid informed consent will be obtained; plan translated information and consent documents for any such population the protocol and sites are expected to enrol.
- **form answers**: Part II forms (shared EU templates in content/eu/templates/part-ii) are answered in Romanian, matching the CTIS_RO variable sheet; English is also permitted for these non-participant documents (DECISIONS.md D1).

## Answer languages for forms

- ro

## Populations, age bands and representatives

- **authority**: Ministry of Health Order 3390/2022 (Articles 14 and 15, Annex 3) and CNBMDM, as recorded in the Romania guides reviewed 23 September 2026; Romanian Civil Code (full legal capacity normally at 18; joint parental authority); Regulation (EU) No 536/2014 Articles 28 to 35.
- **minors**:
  - **age bands**:
    - **band**: 6-11
    - **template**: RO-PT-age-6-11
    - **reader**: child
    - **band**: 12-17
    - **template**: RO-PT-age-12-17
    - **reader**: adolescent
  - **always**:
    - RO-PT-parents
  - **band rule**: Order 3390/2022 publishes no fixed CTIS-specific assent age bands; information is adapted to age and mental maturity. The template bands 6-11 and 12-17 are readability bands, not legal thresholds; capacity to form an opinion and assess the information is assessed individually.
  - **band conflict**: None: the authority sets no band, so the supplied bands are kept and no file is renamed.
  - **agreement**: The minor's agreement is mandatory, in addition to the legally designated representative's consent, when the minor can form an opinion and assess the information. The explicit wish of such a minor to refuse or withdraw is respected.
  - **representative**: Parental authority is generally joint and equal; verify who has legal authority in the individual case rather than assuming a particular family member can sign.
  - **reconsent at 18**: RO-PT-main or RO-PT-healthy: new informed consent before further protocol-related actions when the participant reaches 18 (normal age of full legal capacity). Do not build the routine pathway on the Civil Code exceptions (marriage, court-recognised capacity from 16) unless the participant's legal status is confirmed.
  - **under 6**: No template. The guide says a short child information or assent document may be appropriate for younger children (DECISIONS.md D4).
  - **team**: A paediatrician is part of the investigator team when the trial involves the paediatric population (operational; checked in N1 and M1).
  - **payment**: No financial inducement beyond compensation for expenses and loss of earnings directly related to participation.
- **adults without capacity**:
  - **always**:
    - RO-PT-legal-rep
  - **as applicable**:
    - RO-PT-limited-capacity
  - **signature rule**: Where the participant can form an opinion and assess the information, both the participant's and the representative's signatures on the informed-consent form are mandatory (Order 3390/2022); the representative's form keeps the participant block, and RO-PT-limited-capacity documents the adapted information and the participant's will.
  - **regained capacity**: RO-PT-main: the participant is re-informed and asked to consent to continued participation.
  - **payment**: No financial inducement beyond compensation for expenses and loss of earnings directly related to participation.
  - **note**: Combine with the impartial-witness pathway if the participant cannot write. A relative does not automatically have legal authority.
- **emergency**:
  - **always**:
    - RO-PT-emergency
  - **as applicable**:
    - RO-PT-legal-rep
    - RO-PT-limited-capacity
    - RO-PT-witness
  - **note**: Subsequent consent from the participant or the representative, whichever can validly give it first. If the participant lacks capacity beyond the emergency, apply the limited-capacity pathway; if the participant cannot write, the witness pathway.
- **impartial witness**:
  - **always**:
    - RO-PT-witness
  - **note**: Capable participant unable to write. The witness signs and dates the consent document. Not combined into a minor or limited-capacity pathway; apply those in addition where they apply.
- **healthy volunteers**:
  - **always**:
    - RO-PT-healthy
  - **note**: Phase I or bioequivalence units need ANMDMR authorisation (RO-N-PHASE1).
- **pregnancy**:
  - **participant**:
    - RO-PT-pregnancy
  - **partner**:
    - RO-PT-pregnant-partner
  - **note**: Participant document only where data beyond the main consent are collected; partner document whenever the partner is asked directly for information or record access.
- **non romanian speakers**: Plan translated information and consent documents for any non-Romanian-speaking population the protocol and sites are expected to enrol; K1 explains how valid consent is obtained.
- **shareable templates**:
  - RO-PT-pregnant-partner
  - RO-PT-secondary
- **shareable note**: May be one shared document for several populations when nothing in it would differ; agree with the user. Secondary-use consent for minors and adults lacking capacity follows the population's consent giver, so separate versions may be needed.
- **separate document rules**:
  - Optional future use of samples or data: separate choice or clearly separated signature section, so the main trial can be accepted without the future research; the supplied templates use a separate document (RO-PT-secondary). Do not label integral protocol research optional.
  - A pregnant partner asked directly for information receives a dedicated document; the participant cannot consent for the partner.
  - Each genuinely optional activity has its own YES/NO choice, signature and date (supplied templates).

## Drafting rules

- Participant documents in Romanian, in plain Romanian suitable for the intended population, with no unexplained scientific abbreviations; translations complete, not only the signature page. Non-participant documents may be in English (Government Emergency Ordinance 29/2022).
- Use the supplied Romanian templates as the baseline; keep the model text where it fits, add every study-specific detail and remove every drafting instruction and non-applicable module.
- Describe the process actually planned at Romanian sites, not generic wording.
- The consent interview is conducted by a physician who is a member of the investigation team and appropriately trained on the protocol and GCP (Order 3390/2022); reflect this in the physician's declaration and signature block.
- Blank ICF with the correct signature blocks for every planned pathway: participant or legally designated representative and the physician; impartial witness signs and dates where the participant cannot write; participant and representative both sign for an adult lacking capacity who can form an opinion and assess the information.
- Distinguish consent to participate from the legal basis for processing personal data; do not present trial consent as the only legal basis, and do not promise deletion of data or samples where legal or scientific retention applies.
- Optional choices (future use, additional procedures) clearly optional, each with a separate choice and signature; integral protocol procedures are not labelled optional.
- Compensation and insurance wording match the national compensation form and the insurance evidence; for minors, adults lacking capacity and pregnant or breastfeeding participants only expenses and loss of earnings directly related to participation.
- Recruitment material in Romanian, consistent with K1, with no claims beyond the protocol; electronic recruitment resources need prior CNBMDM approval.
- Every document carries protocol number, country, language, version and date; no tracked changes, hidden comments or placeholder text.
- Participant templates (JSON): italic text, [square-bracket] notes, 'Instrucțiune pentru autor' passages and '...........' or '____' gaps are drafting instructions or gaps; follow and remove them, except consent-visit fields that stay blank. Part II templates (DOCX): yellow [INSERT/IF/OTHERWISE/CHOOSE/NOTE: …] labels. Nothing of either may remain in a final document.
