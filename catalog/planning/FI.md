    Mandatory content: the inclusion procedure and a clearly identified first act of recruitment. A separate document is required under Annex I section K when the information is not already in the protocol. Tukija requests the EU Recruitment and Informed Consent Procedure template; the Finland-specific parts are completed in Finnish and/or Swedish. Submitted under Recruitment arrangements, unsigned, PDF or PDF/A.

    National additions to the form: - **v105**: Required in Finland: in Finnish and/or Swedish, state that every advertisement, invitation and other recruitment communication is submitted under Recruitment materials in Finnish and/or Swedish before use, that audio and video are submitted as a transcript (audio) or a transcript or storyboard (video), not as media files, and how responses are handled and people found unsuitable are informed (Tukija T/448/2026).

    - **v108**: Required in Finland: in Finnish and/or Swedish, state that a qualified member of the investigating team gives the prior information, answers questions, allows enough decision time and verifies understanding; that the participant or legal representative and the person receiving consent sign and date the approved Finnish/Swedish form when consent is obtained (blank and unsigned in CTIS); that the original stays in the investigator''s archive and a paper or electronic copy is given; that participants who speak neither Finnish nor Swedish receive all patient-facing documents in a language they understand, with an interpreter familiar with clinical trials, the participant''s culture and the Finnish operating environment throughout the research unless the team speaks the language; and, for a cluster trial, that the Article 30 simplified consent route is not used in Finland.

    - **v110**: Required in Finland when adults with reduced capacity are included: in Finnish and/or Swedish, describe the documented capacity assessment (diagnosis, disability or residence in care does not by itself prove incapacity); who consents under section 13 of the Clinical Trials Act 983/2021 (the legal representative; if there is none, a close family member or another person close to the participant) and how the site identifies that person and documents the relationship; the adapted participant information and any assent or acknowledgement; how verbal, behavioural or supported-communication dissent is recognised and respected; reassessment of capacity and the participant''s own consent when capacity returns; and how a decision made before capacity was lost is evaluated under Article 31.

    - **v114**: Required in Finland when minors are included: in Finnish and/or Swedish, describe the justified developmental groups and their documents (Tukija sets no fixed bands); guardian or other legal representative consent plus the written consent of a minor who can form an opinion and evaluate the information (section 14, Act 983/2021); the age-15 pathway, where an adolescent aged 15 to 17 consents independently only in an Article 32(1)(g)(i) direct-benefit trial and when able to understand its significance, with the guardian informed, including how understanding is assessed and how the guardian is notified; respect for a capable minor''s refusal; and re-consent at 18 and on entering the age-15 pathway, as defined in the protocol.

    - **v117**: Required in Finland when an impartial witness may be used: in Finnish and/or Swedish, state that the witness route applies only when the consenting participant or legal representative cannot write (not for another language, limited literacy or interpreter use alone); who checks the witness''s independence and eligibility and what happens when no suitable witness is available; how the interpreter and witness roles are separated or, if one person does both, why; and that the witness and the person who conducted the discussion sign and date the consent record.

    - **v121**: Required in Finland when emergency enrolment is used: in Finnish and/or Swedish, describe how the sudden serious condition, the therapeutic window, why the participant and the representative could not be informed in time, the direct-benefit basis, the check for a previously expressed objection and the time of the first intervention are recorded in the source; later consent without undue delay (representative for minors and adults with reduced capacity; otherwise participant or representative, whichever can decide sooner, and the participant''s own consent once capable); the right to object to use of data already obtained; and staff instructions that separate trial documentation from urgent clinical care. No form may state retrospectively that consent was given before enrolment.

    Completed by: assistant

# National rules – Finland

## Authorities and fee

- **competent authority**: Fimea (Finnish Medicines Agency): national contact point, supervises clinical trials, contributes to the assessment and issues the national authorisation decision in CTIS.
- **ethics**: Tukija (National Committee on Medical Research Ethics) leads the Finnish Part II assessment, prepares the Part II assessment report and performs the ethical review. No separate ethics application outside CTIS; requests for information, responses and the decision are handled in CTIS.
- **fee**: One joint fee through Fimea, invoiced to the sponsor after Part I has been accepted for processing. Invoicing details go in the CTIS Proof of payment section (FI-FEE), not Part II.
- **accelerated procedure**: Since 11 February 2026, Finland-only applications of any phase and sponsor type may receive a decision within 30 calendar days from CTIS submission when no corrections or requests for information are needed. A processing pathway, not a reduced dossier.
- **file format**: PDF or PDF/A; no signatures on dossier documents at CTIS submission (Tukija).

## Language rules

- **participant documents**:
  - fi
  - sv
- **administrative documents**:
  - fi
  - sv
  - en
- **rule**: The general application dossier may be in Finnish, Swedish or English (Clinical Trials Act 983/2021). Recruitment advertising, the recruitment and informed-consent procedure and Annex I section L materials (information sheets and consent forms) must be in Finnish and/or Swedish (Tukija T/448/2026).
- **when finnish**: Default and reference language: the Finnish documents are drafted from the supplied Finnish templates and submitted in CTIS. The trial code and a concise, clear Finnish trial title identify the document.
- **when swedish**: Swedish participant documents are needed whenever Swedish-speaking participants or representatives are enrolled (study fact population.swedish_speakers), because each participant receives information in a language they understand. A Swedish version does not need to be submitted in CTIS after the Finnish version has been approved. The guides give no rule by site region.
- **other languages**: Participants who speak neither Finnish nor Swedish receive all patient-facing documents in a language they understand; unless the research team speaks it, an interpreter familiar with clinical trials, the participant's culture and the Finnish operating environment is needed throughout the research, not only at the signature visit (study fact population.non_finnish_swedish_speakers).
- **patient forms**: Questionnaires, diaries and other participant forms: Part I in Finnish, Swedish or English, generally following the protocol language; no separate Part II translations required (Tukija).
- **form answers**: Shared EU forms (content/eu/templates/part-ii) answered in the package language (fi or sv). English accepted for M1, M2, N1, O, P, R1 and S1; not for K1 and the consent procedure.

## Drafting rules

- Participant documents: Tukija's template T/447/2026 (25 June 2026) is the drafting baseline (future use: T/171/2025), adapted to the protocol, population and Finnish operating environment; translated examples adapted to Finland.
- Finnish and/or Swedish for recruitment advertising, the recruitment and consent procedure, information sheets and consent forms; the trial code and a concise, clear Finnish trial title identify each Finnish document.
- Main information sheet about 5 to 8 pages, normally no more than 10; a longer document needs a real complexity justification. Detailed operating instructions go to a separate participant guide, but decision-relevant burdens and restrictions stay in the sheet.
- Clear everyday language; define medical or foreign terms at first use; consistent units, time periods and form of address; no commands, inducements, promotional wording or wording that assumes agreement.
- State that it is a clinical trial on medicinal products; trial name, code and EU trial number; why the person is invited; voluntariness and withdrawal without reason or loss of treatment rights near the start.
- Do not promise benefit. Most severe and most common adverse reactions, comparator and procedure risks in the main sheet; separate standard care from trial-specific procedures and costs.
- Ionising radiation: explain each examination, purpose, dose and risk; state when exposure is no greater than conventional care; frequency and total dose for several examinations.
- GDPR Articles 12 to 14 information; legal bases cited by article, paragraph and point; balancing test when legitimate interest is used; pseudonymised data never called anonymous; 'ethnic background' instead of 'race' in Finnish text; Data Protection Ombudsman complaint route.
- Retain Tukija's Finnish insurance explanation (Pharmaceutical Injuries Insurance vs the site's patient insurance) unless the actual cover differs; explain an alternative arrangement when the medicine is not covered.
- Blank, unsigned, version-controlled documents in CTIS, as PDF or PDF/A; signature and date fields stay empty for the consent visit (participant or representative, and the person receiving consent).
- Article 30 simplified cluster consent is not available in Finland.
- Optional choices never preselected; withdrawal from future use distinguished from withdrawal from the trial.
- Test drafts with lay readers and research staff; consult the patient group concerned; consider an easy-language version when the population needs it (recommended).
