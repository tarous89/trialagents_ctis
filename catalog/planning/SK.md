    - **v117**: Required in Slovakia when an impartial witness may be needed: in Slovak, state that a witness is used when the participant or representative cannot read the information; that a participant who understands and can consent but cannot write gives oral consent entered into a record signed by at least one witness (Slovak law); that the witness is independent of the trial, understands the language and is present for the whole discussion, and that a study-team member is not used routinely; that the witness''s role differs from an interpreter''s; and that the reason for witness use and the witness''s presence are documented in the source notes.

    - **v121**: Required in Slovakia when emergency enrolment is used: in Slovak, explain each Article 35 condition for the exact emergency condition and therapeutic window (not only that the trial follows Article 35); who confirms that the conditions are met and what is recorded (emergency facts, therapeutic window, why the participant could not consent, why a representative could not be reached in time, prior-objection check, enrolment decision); whether an independent physician or other safeguard is involved as stated in the protocol; the later information and consent of the participant or legal representative without undue delay; refusal and withdrawal handling for intervention, follow-up, data and samples; and that persons in custody, serving a prison sentence or held in detention are not enrolled even in emergencies (Act No. 362/2011 Coll.).

# National rules – Slovakia

## Authorities and fee

- **competent authority**: ŠÚKL (Štátny ústav pre kontrolu liečiv, State Institute for Drug Control).
- **ethics**: One national Ethics Committee established by the Ministry of Health assesses Part II for CTIS medicinal-product trials. Separate institutional ethics review may still be relevant to local governance but does not replace the national CTIS Part II assessment.
- **fee**: Not stated in the Slovakia guides (DECISIONS.md, Missing).
- **national guidance**: Act No. 362/2011 Coll. on medicinal products and medical devices (sections 29a and 29h); Act No. 576/2004 Coll. (section 6); ŠÚKL Good Clinical Practice for investigators (section 4.8); ŠÚKL Annual Report 2025 (consent-document deficiencies: missing Ethics Committee telephone details and travel-cost information); Slovakia section of the Commission's Overview of Part II requirements per Member State (accessed 31 August 2026).

## Language rules

- **participant documents**: sk
- **administrative documents**:
  - sk
- **rule**: Slovak law requires the application dossier and participant documentation to be submitted in Slovak (Act No. 362/2011 Coll., section 29a(9)). Translate the approved meaning, not only the words; medical terms, withdrawal language, data-protection rights and contact information stay understandable to the intended population.
- **english versions**: National guidance expects submitted patient-facing documents in English and Slovak. Keep the English master and the Slovak translation synchronised by version and date and aligned in meaning; the Slovak version is the participant-use version. If only a Slovak participant version is legally needed for an item, keep a certified or documented translation trail in the sponsor file (DECISIONS.md D1).
- **bilingual allowed**:
  - **SK-K1**: Slovak or bilingual Slovak and English
  - **SK-M1**: Slovak or a bilingual version
- **form answers**: Shared EU forms (content/eu/templates/part-ii) are answered in Slovak.

## Populations, age bands and representatives

- **authority**: Ethics Committee established by the Ministry of Health of the Slovak Republic (national Part II assessment) and ŠÚKL; Slovak Act No. 362/2011 Coll., sections 29a(5), 29a(7) to 29a(9) and 29h; Act No. 576/2004 Coll., section 6; ŠÚKL Good Clinical Practice for investigators, section 4.8; Regulation (EU) No 536/2014 Articles 28 to 35; as recorded in the Slovakia guides reviewed 23 September 2026.
- **template status**: missing: no Slovak participant templates were supplied. Every template id below is a planned id with no file ("template": "missing" in population_sets). Keys ending in _missing hold the template ids that the Germany-shaped keys (always, age_bands) would hold; the audit script checks those keys against participant_templates, which is empty.
- **minors**:
  - **young children**: Parent or legal-representative information and permission, plus a short verbal or visual explanation suited to the child; no signed child form. Children below 12 are not excluded from information; capacity to understand is assessed individually.
  - **band rule**: Explain and justify the age bands in K1 (v114). The Ethics Committee may expect more than one child version when the age range is wide or the procedures are complex.
  - **representatives**: Parent or legal representative recognised under Slovak law; whose permission is obtained in each family situation and how legal authority is verified is stated in the submission. If national law or the custody arrangement requires two representatives, plan two signature blocks; where one signature is proposed, document the legal basis and site procedure (DECISIONS.md D10).
  - **assent dissent**: Child information and assent mandatory when the child can form an opinion; assent does not replace the representative's consent; verbal assent recorded when a written form is not suitable; explicit refusal and later dissent of a minor capable of forming an opinion is respected.
  - **reconsent at 18**: SK-PT-main or SK-PT-healthy (approved adult form) promptly when participation or follow-up continues; earlier parental permission is not adult consent.
  - **payment**: No financial inducement to the parent or legal representative beyond compensation for expenses and loss connected with participation.
  - **always**:
    - SK-PT-parents
  - **age bands**:
    - **band**: 12-17
    - **template**: SK-PT-adolescents-12-17
    - **reader**: adolescent
    - **basis**: Slovak national Part II guidance identifies a separate information and consent set for ages 12 to 18 as a typical submission document; the minors guide gives 'commonly 12 to 17'. Both mean 12 up to the 18th birthday.
    - **band**: school-age children under 12 who can understand (lower bound defined per study)
    - **template**: SK-PT-children
    - **reader**: child
    - **basis**: Minors guide: child information and assent material for younger children who can understand; no fixed band (DECISIONS.md D9).
- **adults without capacity**:
  - **note**: SK-PT-limited-capacity when the participant can understand part of the trial. Capacity is decision-specific; legally designated representative under Slovak law with verified authority; objection of a participant who can form an opinion cannot be overridden; the participant's views recorded (Act No. 576/2004 Coll.). Participant's own consent with SK-PT-main when capacity is regained.
  - **always**:
    - SK-PT-legal-rep
    - SK-PT-limited-capacity
- **emergency**:
  - **note**: Full participant and legal-representative documents; short initial notice only if it does not delay urgent care. No national emergency-consent template exists; do not invent a national waiver form.
  - **always**:
    - SK-PT-emergency
    - SK-PT-legal-rep
  - **optional**:
    - SK-PT-emergency-notice
- **impartial witness**:
  - **note**: Witness when the participant or representative cannot read; oral consent record signed by at least one witness when the person can consent but cannot write. A representative who cannot read needs the witness block in the representative document (DECISIONS.md D17).
  - **always**:
    - SK-PT-witness
- **pregnancy**:
  - **participant**: SK-PT-pregnancy only for data beyond what the main trial information covers
  - **partner**: SK-PT-pregnant-partner whenever partner or infant data are collected from the partner
- **secondary use**:
  - **note**: Separate form or clearly separate optional section; separate choices for disease-related storage, genetic research, sharing with external researchers, transfer outside the EEA and re-contact, as applicable.
  - **always**:
    - SK-PT-secondary
- **prohibited population**: Slovak law prohibits enrolment of people in custody, serving a prison sentence or held in detention. No consent pathway or document is created for them (study fact population.persons_in_custody must be false).
- **shareable templates**:
  - SK-PT-pregnant-partner
  - SK-PT-secondary
- **separate document rules**:
  - A separate document pathway whenever the reader, decision-maker or data subject changes (Slovakia patient information and consent guide).
  - Patients and healthy volunteers in one study get separate information and consent documents.
  - Emergency enrolment cannot be created by shortening the standard form.
  - Optional future data or sample use, genetic research, recordings and other substudies are separate choices, not bundled with main-trial consent.
  - The pregnant partner receives a separate document addressed to her; the enrolled participant cannot consent on her behalf.
  - Re-consent: participants who turn 18 or regain capacity give their own consent with the approved adult form; new safety information that could affect willingness to continue is communicated promptly and may require revised information and re-consent.

## Drafting rules

- Participant documents are in Slovak: familiar words, short sentences, direct explanations; abbreviations expanded at first use; no unexplained protocol terminology or legal formulas; no wording that asks the participant to waive rights or releases the investigator, institution or sponsor from liability.
- Translate the approved meaning, not only the words; replace literal translations that do not match Slovak clinical or regulatory usage; keep defined trial terms consistent across all documents; verify numbers, units, dates, probabilities, treatment names, visit frequencies, sample volumes, contraception periods and contacts sentence by sentence against the English source.
- State that the activity is research; give full and short title, protocol identifier, sponsor and EU trial number where available; explain purpose, investigational treatment, comparator, randomisation, blinding and expected duration.
- Separate standard care from research-only procedures; explain invasive procedures, optional activities, recording devices and anything affecting daily life.
- Describe reasonably foreseeable risks and burdens, including risks to an embryo, fetus or breast-fed child; contraception, pregnancy testing, prohibited medicines and what to do if pregnancy occurs, consistent with the protocol and IB.
- Do not imply personal benefit when none is expected; explain alternatives; healthy volunteers are told the trial is not intended to treat them.
- Voluntariness and withdrawal: refusal does not affect care or legal rights; explain separately what withdrawal means for treatment, safety follow-up, data already collected and remaining samples; never promise deletion of all data.
- Injury arrangements and insurance wording match the valid Slovakia certificate; state participant payments and reimbursement of travel and other expenses, conditions and what happens after early withdrawal (ŠÚKL 2025 deficiency).
- Privacy: controllers, purposes, data categories, legal basis, recipients, source-record access by monitors, auditors and authorities, transfers, retention, rights and complaint route, consistent with the CTIS GDPR statement; consent to the trial is not a blanket GDPR consent; no claim of absolute confidentiality.
- Contacts: a reachable trial medical contact (not only a generic global mailbox), a contact for participant rights or complaints, and the national Ethics Committee contact route including the telephone details required by current Slovak participant-information guidance (ŠÚKL 2025 deficiency).
- Consent section: separate statements that the participant received and understood the information, had time and opportunity to ask questions, understands voluntariness, agrees to the main trial procedures, understands source-record access, received information on privacy, injury, reimbursement and withdrawal, and makes separate optional choices; signature and personal date of participant and of the person conducting the discussion; legal-representative, witness or parent blocks only in the pathway where they apply.
- Optional choices: a distinct yes or no for each materially different optional activity, never preselected; no unlimited wording such as 'any research forever'; say directly if genetic analysis is planned and whether individual results are returned.
- Blank documents in CTIS: no participant signatures or personal data; filename and footer carry version number, date, language, population and country.
- Participant templates were not supplied; where a reference document or a later template is used, its instructions ([square-bracket] notes, italic notes, '...........' gaps) and Part II form instructions (yellow [INSERT/IF/OTHERWISE/CHOOSE/NOTE: …] labels) are drafting instructions; none may remain in a final document.
