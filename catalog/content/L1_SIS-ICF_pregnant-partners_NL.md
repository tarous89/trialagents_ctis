Template supplied for Netherlands, in Dutch: **Informatiebrief en toestemmingsformulier voor follow-up van zwangerschap en kind – zwangere partner** (Pregnancy Follow-up and Consent - Partners).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Gebruik uitsluitend voor de zwangere partner van een mannelijke onderzoeksdeelnemer wanneer informatie over haar gezondheid, de zwangerschap, de zwangerschapsuitkomst of de gezondheid van het kind wordt verzameld vanwege mogelijke blootstelling van de mannelijke deelnemer aan het onderzoeksmiddel. Maak duidelijk dat de zwangere partner niet aan het hoofdonderzoek deelneemt, geen onderzoeksmiddel krijgt en zelf toestemming geeft voor haar eigen gegevens; de mannelijke deelnemer kan dit niet namens haar doen. Beperk de inhoud strikt tot zwangerschaps- en kindfollow-up en herhaal geen diagnose, behandelalternatieven, onderzoeksarmen, studiebezoeken of andere onderdelen van het hoofdonderzoek, behalve de relevante blootstelling van de mannelijke deelnemer. Neem informatie over verzekering of schadevergoeding alleen kort en conditioneel op als aanvullende onderzoekshandelingen binnen deze follow-up dit vereisen. Neem alleen werkelijk verzamelde gegevens en bekende of redelijkerwijs te verwachten risico’s van vaderlijke blootstelling op. Aanvullende onderzoeken, lichaamsmateriaal, genetische gegevens, langere follow-up of ander/toekomstig onderzoek mogen alleen conditioneel, volledig beschreven en met een afzonderlijke ja/nee-keuze worden opgenomen. Weigering of intrekking mag geen nadeel hebben voor de zwangere partner, het kind of deelname van de mannelijke deelnemer aan het hoofdonderzoek. Verwerk gegevens van het kind na de geboorte alleen wanneer dit is voorzien en nadat de ouders die het gezag uitoefenen of de voogd daarvoor na de geboorte toestemming hebben gegeven of de toestemming hebben bevestigd. Schrijf op B1-niveau, behoud toepasselijke Nederlandse standaardtekst en verwijder alle niet-toepasselijke alternatieven.

## Checklist
- C1.01 The public title, protocol number, EU Clinical Trial number, sponsor and version date are correct.
- C1.02 The purpose is stated in plain language and does not imply proven benefit.
- C1.03 Trial arms, randomisation, placebo and comparator are described accurately.
- C1.04 Screening, treatment, visits, calls, procedures, questionnaires and follow-up are complete.
- C1.05 Research procedures are distinguished from normal care.
- C1.06 The duration and number of visits match the protocol schedule.
- C1.07 Main risks appear early (cover page), with fuller detail in the correct appendix.
- C1.08 Unknown risks and burdens are not hidden in technical language.
- C1.09 Expected direct benefit is described accurately; healthy volunteers are told that direct benefit is absent.
- C1.10 Alternatives to participation are explained when relevant.
- C2.01 Inclusion populations match every information and consent document.
- C2.02 Contraception, pregnancy testing, reporting and post-treatment periods match the protocol and investigator brochure. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C2.03 Stopping rules and follow-up after withdrawal match the protocol.
- C2.04 Data and sample retention periods match the protocol, the R1 form and the biological-samples form.
- C2.05 Optional future use is not described as a core trial procedure. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C2.06 The insurer, policy, coverage status and claims contact match the Part II evidence (O1/O2 or the exemption).
- C2.07 Participant compensation and reimbursement match the Dutch P1 form.
- C2.08 The informed-consent process described in the protocol matches the harmonised recruitment and consent procedure template (K1).
- C2.09 Any use of medical records, a general practitioner, specialist or pharmacist is supported by an explicit consent statement.
- C3.01 The core consent to join the trial is clearly identifiable.
- C3.02 Optional choices use separate Yes or No responses; no blanket consent for all optional items.
- C3.03 Refusal of future data use, remaining sample storage, future contact, DNA analysis or external travel support does not block trial participation.
- C3.04 Trial-required data and samples are not presented as optional.
- C3.05 The person who must consent is correct for age and decision-making capacity (16 or older capable: the participant; under 12: both parents with parental authority or the guardian; 12-15: the child and both parents or the guardian; lacking capacity: the legally designated representative).
- C3.06 The participant or representative receives enough time, can ask questions and receives a signed copy.
- C3.07 The blank form is submitted in CTIS; participant-signed forms are retained at site and are not uploaded.
- C3.08 Re-consent triggers are operational, including new safety information, a child reaching age 12 and recovery of capacity.
- C4.01 Every population named in the protocol has an appropriate Dutch document; populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage and every inclusion pathway has its full document set. (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Participants aged 16 or older use the adult IVO pathway unless capacity or another special circumstance changes it.
- C4.03 Children under 12 receive an age-appropriate discussion sheet and their parents or guardian receive the consent information. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.04 Children aged 12 through 15 receive their own information and consent document, in addition to parent or guardian consent. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.05 Adults lacking capacity receive information adapted to what they can understand, with a separate legal-representative form. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.06 Emergency enrolment has an authorised deferred-consent process and documents for the participant or representative at the earliest possible time. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.07 A witness pathway is available only where the participant understands but cannot complete ordinary written consent. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.08 Pregnancy follow-up distinguishes the trial participant from a pregnant partner who is a separate person. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up)
- C4.09 Healthy-volunteer documents cover absence of direct benefit, compensation, restrictions and relevant washout. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C5.01 The document identifies the data collected and the purpose of each use.
- C5.02 Coded or pseudonymised data is not called anonymous.
- C5.03 The controller or joint controllers and data-protection contacts are correct.
- C5.04 People who may see identifiable data are named by role.
- C5.05 The Health and Youth Care Inspectorate access statement is accurate.
- C5.06 Storage locations and fixed retention periods are clear.
- C5.07 Transfers outside the European Union are described with safeguards. (applies when: data.transfer_outside_eu)
- C5.08 Withdrawal language distinguishes future collection from data already lawfully collected.
- C5.09 External travel or reimbursement providers receive data only through a separate optional choice. (applies when: data.external_travel_provider)
- C5.10 Privacy wording matches the mandatory Dutch R1 form and the application-level GDPR statement.
- C6.01 Each sample, volume or procedure and its purpose is understandable. (applies when: samples.collected or samples.archive_used)
- C6.02 Trial testing is separated from remaining-sample storage and future research. (applies when: samples.collected or samples.archive_used)
- C6.03 Data and bodily material have separate optional consent choices. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.04 The future-research scope is defined by condition, treatment or another understandable field. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.05 The storage period is a fixed duration and does not say only 'at least' a number of years. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.06 Sharing with other researchers and transfers outside the EU are explained. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.07 Withdrawal explains destruction of remaining samples and treatment of results already generated. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.08 Genetic analysis, artificial-intelligence analysis and commercial collaboration are described when applicable. (applies when: genetics.optional_dna_research or data.optional_ai_analysis or samples.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.09 The consent text matches the Part II biological-samples form (S1). (applies when: samples.collected or samples.archive_used or samples.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C7.01 The participant information and consent form submitted for review is Dutch.
- C7.02 IVO version 1.1, dated 14 August 2026, is used for a new adult (16 or older) document unless the permitted PIF transition (until 31 December 2026) is intentionally used.
- C7.03 The IVO standard text is unchanged except where the instructions allow adaptation or deletion.
- C7.04 The cover page is no more than two pages.
- C7.05 The complete IVO is no more than 4,000 words including consent form and appendices, or the excess is justified in the cover letter.
- C7.06 The text is written at B1 level and uses an accessible sans-serif font listed in the model at no less than 12 pt or 16 px.
- C7.07 The general CCMO information references required by the IVO are preserved.
- C7.08 The participant's insurance appendix is included unless an exemption was granted.
- C7.09 Planned non-Dutch enrolment has an operational translation and certification plan. (applies when: population.non_dutch_speakers)
- C8.01 Every document has a unique title, version and date.
- C8.02 The CTIS filename clearly identifies the population and Dutch language.
- C8.03 The submitted consent forms contain blank participant, representative and researcher signature fields.
- C8.04 Adult consent contains participant and researcher or authorised interviewer signatures and dates.
- C8.05 Minor documents contain the correct child and parent or guardian signature blocks (both parents with parental authority, or the guardian; the 12-15 child's own signature). (applies when: population.minors)
- C8.06 Legal-representative documents identify the representative and relationship. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity or consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C8.07 Witness documents contain the required witness attestation and signature. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C8.08 Version dates match the cover letter and document list.
- C8.09 Superseded or tracked-change drafts are removed from the final upload set unless a comparison is requested.
- C8.10 Correct CTIS section and title per the naming guide and requirements.json.
- C8.11 No drafting instruction remains in the final version (italic notes, <angle-bracket> and [square-bracket] notes, 'Vul in'/'Kies' labels, '......' gaps, yellow [INSERT/IF/CHOOSE/NOTE] labels); fields completed by hand at consent stay blank.
- C9.01 The first two pages answer what participation involves, why the study is being done, main risks, time, payment, insurance and withdrawal.
- C9.02 Headings are questions or statements a participant can understand.
- C9.03 Sentences are short and medical terms are explained.
- C9.04 Risk frequencies use consistent, understandable wording.
- C9.05 Long protocol lists are replaced by participant-relevant summaries.
- C9.06 Tables are used only when they simplify schedules or choices.
- C9.07 Consent choices are visually distinct and cannot be skipped by mistake.
- C9.08 Contact details are easy to find.
- C9.09 Page numbers, appendix references and cross-references are correct.
- C9.10 A Dutch lay reader from the intended population has reviewed the trial-specific wording where possible.
- C10.01 The package has been read once from the participant's perspective (comprehension and voluntariness).
- C10.02 The package has been read once from the assessor's perspective (traceability to the protocol, national forms and CTIS categories).
- C10.03 The document list has been compared against the Netherlands Part II document checklist.
- C11.01 Healthy volunteers (NL-HV): IVO used (or the PIF transition intentionally); no direct medical benefit implied; risks and uncertainty match the dose and available human exposure (first-in-human or first dose, formulation or combination stated); screening and confinement burden, total blood volume and worst realistic time commitment complete; restrictions have clear start and end points; washout and any research-participation register check explained; pregnancy-prevention wording matches the protocol; compensation (gross amount or calculation, schedule, partial payment, tax) matches P1 and is not coercive or tied to optional choices; insurance wording matches the certificate or exemption; contacts cover urgent symptoms during and after confinement. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C11.02 Minors (NL-MINORS): every minor population has a Dutch age-appropriate document; the under-12 discussion sheet and 12-15 information sheet use the official CCMO models where applicable; both parents with parental authority, or the guardian, are correctly identified; the 12-15 child signature is not omitted; resistance language (consent lapses if the minor resists) appears in the parent consent declaration; the protocol explains how resistance is recognised and handled; re-consent at 12 is scheduled; turning 16 is handled per CCMO (no new consent solely for age); optional future use does not affect participation and an older child's own decision is not bypassed; risks, procedures and pregnancy language fit the child's age. (applies when: population.minors)
- C11.03 Adults lacking capacity (NL-CAPACITY): inclusion justified under the protective Dutch and EU conditions; capacity assessed for this decision, not inferred from diagnosis; the participant receives understandable Dutch information; the representative receives the full Dutch information and consent form; the Dutch representative order and authority are documented; objection and withdrawal are respected; optional choices separate from participation and no broader than the participant could have given; signatures identify the representative, relationship and researcher; reassessment and own re-consent planned if capacity changes; emergency enrolment not used outside an authorised Article 35 pathway. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C11.04 Emergency (NL-EMERGENCY): every Article 35 condition answered; emergency directly related to the trial question; therapeutic window medically justified; prior objection checked and respected; K1 section 6 complete; participant and representative Dutch documents explain prior enrolment, what already happened and what remains; new collection stops after refusal; data-retention wording matches current EU and Dutch guidance (data lawfully gathered under Article 35 remain); death before consent addressed in the protocol; the pathway is not used when ordinary prior consent is possible. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C11.05 Impartial witness (NL-WITNESS): the participant or representative has capacity; the need is inability to complete ordinary written consent (write, sign or read); accessible Dutch information available; translation or interpreter need handled separately; K1 section 5 complete; witness independent and present throughout; alternative recording method authorised and privacy-protective; the participant's own decision stays visible; witness attestation, witness signature and researcher signature included; an accessible copy given. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C11.06 Pregnancy (NL-PREGNANCY): participant and partner pathways separate; original consent scope reviewed before adding a form; the pregnant partner consents before direct follow-up; maternal, outcome and infant data limited to the stated purpose; medical-record access and professional contact explicit; follow-up duration, end point and contact frequency defined; privacy covers everyone whose data is collected; optional samples and future use have separate choices; insurance language matches the authorised coverage (harm to an unborn child may not be covered in an unrelated trial); CCMO is the reviewing committee where pregnant or breastfeeding participants are enrolled. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up or population.pregnant_or_breastfeeding)
- C11.07 Secondary use (NL-SECONDARY): core and optional future use separated; data and bodily material have different Yes/No choices; refusal does not affect participation, payment or care; research field and allowed users understandable; genetic, AI and commercial use explicit where applicable; storage period fixed and location named; coded material not called anonymous; EU and non-EU sharing described; IVO, protocol, R1 and S1 agree; withdrawal explains destruction of remaining samples and limits for prior data and results; age, capacity and re-consent changes planned; a separate secondary-use ICF stays linked to the main L1. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
