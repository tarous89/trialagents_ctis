Template supplied for Austria, in German: **Angepasste Teilnehmerinformation und Zustimmungserklärung** (Subject Information and Assent Form - Limited Capacity).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** LÄNGENLIMIT: Zielumfang 3 bis höchstens 5 Seiten; nur Informationen aufnehmen, die der betroffenen Person helfen, den Kern der Prüfung und ihr Recht auf Ablehnung zu verstehen. Bei mehr als 10 Seiten wäre zusätzlich eine 1- bis höchstens 2-seitige Kurzzusammenfassung erforderlich, ein derartiger Umfang ist jedoch zu vermeiden. Nur für eine erwachsene nicht einwilligungsfähige Person verwenden, die noch Informationen verstehen oder einen Willen äußern kann. Die rechtswirksame Einwilligung der Erwachsenenvertretung bleibt in einem getrennten Dokument. Verwenden Sie kurze Sätze, konkrete Beispiele, Bilder, Symbole oder unterstützte Kommunikation. Erklären Sie Forschung, Behandlung/Placebo, wichtigste Maßnahmen, Dauer, möglichen Nutzen, Hauptgefahren, einfache Vertraulichkeit und Kontakte. Das Recht auf Nein, Ablehnung einzelner Maßnahmen und späteres Aufhören muss zentral stehen. Dokumentieren Sie Kommunikationshilfe und den geäußerten oder beobachteten Willen. Kein langer Datenschutz-, Versicherungs- oder Vertretungsrechtstext in diese angepasste Fassung aufnehmen. Neu ergänzte oder angepasste Texte sind in kurzen, leicht verständlichen Sätzen zu formulieren; Fachbegriffe aus der österreichischen Musterinformation bleiben unverändert, neu eingeführte Fachbegriffe sind kurz zu erklären.

## Checklist
- C1.01 The German full study title on the participant document is identical to the German full title entered for Austria in CTIS.
- C1.02 Study purpose, treatment groups, randomisation, blinding and treatment duration match the protocol.
- C1.03 All participant procedures, visits and study-specific tests are described at the level needed for informed consent.
- C1.04 Stated risks and burdens reflect the current protocol and the current safety information used for the submission.
- C1.05 Expected benefit is not overstated and alternatives are described where relevant.
- C1.06 Pregnancy, contraception and pregnancy follow-up language matches the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C1.07 Reimbursement and compensation amounts, methods and conditions match the Part II financial-arrangements information (AT-P1).
- C2.01 Visit frequency, sample collection, imaging, biopsies, homecare, digital tools and optional procedures compared with the protocol line by line.
- C2.02 Every optional activity is clearly separated from mandatory trial procedures.
- C2.03 Patient-facing endpoint materials that belong in Part I are identified and the German language requirement is applied to their use in Austria. (applies when: endpoint_documents.participant_facing)
- C2.04 The document does not promise procedures, access, treatment or follow-up that the protocol does not support.
- C2.05 Withdrawal language does not imply that routine study visits or new study-purpose data collection continue after full trial withdrawal.
- C3.01 The main trial participation decision is distinct from optional choices.
- C3.02 Optional genetic investigations use a separate consent form (AustrianEthics). (applies when: genetics.optional_investigation)
- C3.03 Optional third-party services include the information and explicit choice required by the current Austrian ICF guidance. (applies when: reimbursement.external_service_provider)
- C3.04 Future research, sample anonymisation and new-technology secondary use have clear yes or no options where required. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol or samples.anonymisation_planned or data.secondary_use_new_technologies)
- C3.05 Signer logic matches the population: the capable adult signs for themselves; a legally designated representative signs when the participant cannot validly consent; an impartial witness signs only where the consenting person cannot write.
- C3.06 The person conducting the consent interview also signs and dates the informed consent document.
- C4.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol maps to a population, populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage, and every population has its full document set; the standard adult German PIS and ICF is present for the main population. (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Healthy volunteers (AT-HV): standard Austrian ICF structure and verbatim insurance and data-protection passages; no implied therapeutic benefit; purpose, exposure, procedures, confinement or visit burden, sampling, restrictions and risks explained; compensation consistent with P1; Austrian insurance contact point present. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.03 Minors (AT-MINORS): parent or representative document present; separate age-appropriate document for each enrolled band 8-9, 10-13, 14-17; decision-capable minor's own consent; child's right to refuse; trial stops if a required consent is missing or withdrawn; representative entitlement not hard-coded to one or two parents; re-consent at adulthood planned; privacy and insurance explained at the child's level without contradicting the representative document. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.04 Adults unable to consent (AT-CAPACITY): separate legal-representative document; information adapted to the participant's capacity; refusal or withdrawal respected; section 39 AMG no-prior-refusal condition where population-benefit research; section 39 AMG excluded groups not included. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.05 Temporary incapacity or emergency (AT-CAPACITY, AT-EMERGENCY): post-hoc information and consent document present; it explains what already happened, proposed continuing procedures, risks and benefits, the right to refuse continued participation and handling of data already collected; no retroactive approval; right to object to use of data on refusal; Article 35 conditions for emergencies. (applies when: population.temporary_incapacity or consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.06 Participant unable to write (AT-WITNESS): witness route used only for inability to write, not for a language barrier or lack of capacity; witness and interviewer signature and date fields; how consent was expressed is recorded; the participant receives a copy including the witness documentation. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.07 Pregnancy follow-up (AT-PREGNANCY): participant pregnancy handling stated (medication stopped, withdrawal right, continuing measures, unblinding, additional data and how obtained); separate document where data go beyond the protocol or pregnancy-monitoring form, child data after birth or treating-physician contact; planned duration stated; child follow-up beyond six months justified; partner document only when protocol-driven. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up or pregnancy.infant_follow_up or pregnancy.treating_physician_contact)
- C4.08 Secondary and future use (AT-SECONDARY): optional, separate from the main decision, refusal without loss of main participation, with the data and sample rules of section C6. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C5.01 The Austrian national sample data-protection passage is reproduced verbatim.
- C5.02 Any added privacy wording is placed after the required passage and does not contradict or repeat it.
- C5.03 The participant or representative is expressly informed in writing about the purpose and scope of personal-data collection and processing (section 41 AMG).
- C5.04 The effect of withdrawal on data already processed is explained in line with Austrian law and the current national wording.
- C5.05 Transfer of pseudonymised data to the United States: the current AustrianEthics Guidance V5 transfer information is addressed. (applies when: data transferred to the United States)
- C5.06 Collection of data on ethnic origin is explicitly described as special-category data. (applies when: data.ethnic_origin_collected)
- C5.07 Reuse of pseudonymised trial data for AI, machine learning or other new technologies: separate understandable description, commercial nature where relevant, optional yes or no consent. (applies when: data.secondary_use_new_technologies)
- C5.08 Known recipient countries of pseudonymised data are named; any country without an adequacy decision is named specifically and informed explicit consent is obtained (Guidance V5). (applies when: data.transfer_third_countries)
- C6.01 The main study explains which samples are collected for protocol-defined trial purposes.
- C6.02 Residual or additional samples stored for future research: the future research field is restricted, not described as unlimited research. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.03 The document identifies which samples are retained, storage location and duration, and who is responsible for storage and destruction. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.04 Participants can refuse optional future use without losing access to the main trial. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.05 For pseudonymised samples, the right to request destruction is explained.
- C6.06 Proposed later anonymisation is separately optional and the loss of the ability to request destruction after anonymisation is clearly explained. (applies when: samples.anonymisation_planned)
- C6.07 Consistent with the S1 biological-samples form and the R1 statement.
- C7.01 All Part II section K and L participant-facing documents are German.
- C7.02 Certified translations into a language understood by participants are available for a planned non-German-speaking population. (applies when: population.non_german_speakers)
- C7.03 The national Austrian ICF template was used or every topic in it has been covered.
- C7.04 The insurance passage from the Austrian sample ICF is reproduced verbatim.
- C7.05 The participant information includes a national insurance contact.
- C7.06 Site-specific contact information includes the centre, PI and deputy PI where applicable, 24-hour emergency number, site data-protection officer and patient-advocacy contact.
- C7.07 Where placeholders are used in the master ICF, the site contact list is current and marked 'not for publication' in CTIS. (applies when: sites.contact_placeholders)
- C7.08 Both insurers are named in combination studies with different sponsors and insurers (Guidance V5). (applies when: trial.combination_different_sponsors_insurers)
- C7.09 All required third-party reimbursement details are in the main ICF or in separate information (Guidance V5). (applies when: reimbursement.external_service_provider)
- C8.01 The CTIS submission contains blank controlled consent templates, not signed participant copies.
- C8.02 Each document has a clear version and date matching the application package.
- C8.03 The consent process is designed for wet-ink or qualified electronic signature (Austrian national requirements portal).
- C8.04 No pasted scanned signature is used where the Austrian site-suitability form requires wet ink, advanced electronic or qualified electronic signature.
- C8.05 All population-specific consent documents use consistent titles, protocol identifiers, sponsor details and contact logic.
- C8.06 Witness signature and date fields only where the impartial-witness pathway can apply (AT-WITNESS).
- C8.07 Representative name and basis of authority recorded where a representative signs (AT-MINORS, AT-CAPACITY).
- C8.08 Statement that the participant or representative receives a copy of the signed consent record (AT-ICF).
- C8.09 Correct CTIS section and naming convention (naming guide).
- C8.10 No site personal data in a master ICF meant for publication where the site contact list route is used (AT-ICF).
- C8.11 No drafting instruction remains in the final version (italic notes, [bracket] notes, ...... gaps, yellow [INSERT/IF/CHOOSE/NOTE] labels); fields completed by hand at consent stay blank.
- C9.01 Plain German that the intended participant population can understand.
- C9.02 Unavoidable medical and regulatory terminology explained at first use.
- C9.03 Repeated explanations that lengthen the ICF without helping consent are removed (without altering the verbatim insurance and data-protection passages).
- C9.04 Headings, short paragraphs and, where useful, simple tables or graphics make complex procedures understandable.
- C9.05 Optional decisions are visually distinct from the main study-consent signature.
