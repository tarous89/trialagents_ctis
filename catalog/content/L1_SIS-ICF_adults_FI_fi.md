Template supplied for Finland, in Finnish: **Tiedote- ja suostumusasiakirja** (Subject Information and Consent Form - Adult Patients).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Use the official Tukija T/447/2026 Finnish model as the primary drafting anchor and preserve its concise participant-facing structure. The information sheet and consent together must provide enough information for an informed decision on the participant's rights, study purpose and nature, methods and procedures, personal-data processing, and relevant risks and harms. Include only information material to the participation decision, in concise lay Finnish. Target approximately 5–8 pages for the information sheet and consent together and normally no more than 10 pages; a somewhat longer document is acceptable only when justified by a complex design. Avoid repetition, repeated direct address, protocol-level detail, and detailed operational instructions. Put detailed instructions such as step-by-step IMP administration in a separate participant instruction unless they are decision-relevant; still state burdens such as injections, food restrictions or other meaningful participation requirements in the SIS/ICF. Do not use commanding, coaching, promotional or inducement-like wording, and do not write as though participation has already been decided. Use one form of address consistently throughout all participant materials. Explain foreign or technical terms in plain language on first use, and use consistent, Finland-appropriate units and time expressions. Where practicable, have the draft reviewed by laypersons and study staff; consultation with the relevant patient group is recommended. The participant must also receive oral information, have the opportunity to ask questions and have adequate time to decide. The participant-information heading must identify the document as information about a clinical medicinal-product trial. Always render the exact heading "TIEDOTE KLIINISESTÄ LÄÄKETUTKIMUKSESTA" before the study-information table/content, even though this heading is not stored in template_content because template_content begins only after the first section marker. Identify the study unambiguously by Finnish-language title and study code where one exists; keep the displayed title short and clear and optionally place the longer official title as a subtitle. Explain briefly why the participant is invited. Keep voluntariness, withdrawal without disadvantage and independence of ordinary care immediately after the invitation. Include alternative treatment only for patient studies. Identify the relevant investigator/site, sponsor/implementing organisation and controller(s). Prefer site-specific information; if one document covers several Finnish sites, centres and principal investigators may be listed, but avoid a long multi-site list. Describe the study background and rationale neutrally, the investigational product, prior human exposure where relevant, the primary objectives and key secondary objectives, target population without reproducing all inclusion/exclusion criteria, participant numbers overall and in Finland, and pregnancy/lactation restrictions where applicable. Explain placebo use and rationale in understandable terms when applicable. Adapt terminology and content to the actual study type, including combination studies; do not mechanically reuse therapeutic-study wording for other designs. Describe the design and procedures at concise, high level: duration, visits, contacts, follow-up, phases, comparator/placebo, randomisation, blinding, placebo probability, and the participant's additional procedures and burdens compared with usual care. Include blood tests/examinations, diaries/questionnaires, prohibited or withdrawn treatments, washout, rescue medication, practical restrictions and other material burdens when applicable, while keeping detailed operating instructions outside the SIS/ICF. If decentralised-trial elements such as remote visits or electronic consent affect participation, explain them in the SIS/ICF. If genetic testing is within the trial, explain its purpose and sample use and state clearly that the testing is limited to this trial and that results are not processed for another purpose. Protocol-external future/secondary use requires a separate information and consent document. Describe contraception where applicable; for studies, especially with teratogenic products, list the acceptable reliable contraception methods in the participant information or a referenced appendix. If the participant becomes pregnant, explain pregnancy follow-up and whether infant data will be collected. If pregnancy data are to be collected from the participant's pregnant partner, use a separate partner information and consent document. If ionising radiation causes no additional exposure compared with usual care, state this. If the trial causes additional ionising-radiation exposure, the participant information must describe in understandable language the imaging procedure and its research benefit, radiation dose and explanation, imaging-specific cancer-mortality risk, frequency when repeated, and total radiation exposure; apply the applicable Finnish radiation requirements and study-specific exposure plan/constraints. State expected personal benefit or uncertainty. Explain whether treatment allocation, study results, incidental findings or other participant-relevant health information will be communicated; if they will not, give an appropriate reason in the participant information. Describe the most serious and most common expected risks of the IMP, comparator and study procedures. Describe serious harms and their probability separately. Also describe decision-relevant practical burdens or restrictions, including alcohol/food/supplement restrictions, withholding standard care or previous treatment, washout, rescue medication and time-consuming daily questionnaires where applicable. State that unexpected harms may occur, explain relevant mitigation and how important new safety information affecting continuation will be communicated. For privacy, inform the participant about controller(s) and processing under GDPR Articles 12–14; a portion may be provided in a separate privacy appendix. State the selected legal basis in words and at article/subparagraph level. If legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) is used, briefly explain the balancing test. Do not use the Finnish equivalent of 'race' in participant-facing material; use 'ethnic background' where that concept is needed. Describe data minimisation, pseudonymisation, relevant clinical information entered into patient records, retention (at least 25 years where applicable), all specified external health-data sources/registries and data categories, authorised access, Fimea/other inspectors and sponsor quality assurance, regulatory submission/use, any direct remote access to confidential health records, recipients, transfers outside the EU/EEA including known countries and transfer mechanism, use of data collected before withdrawal, and the rights that actually apply under the chosen legal basis. Provide the controller/DPO contact and ensure service is available in the same language as the consent process, including Finnish where Finnish is used, plus the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman complaint route. Explain participant costs and reimbursements. If standard care is used as the control, state that it is also free to the participant, including relevant outpatient charges. Describe travel reimbursement, any compensation for loss of earnings and/or inconvenience, and justify inconvenience compensation. Describe funding, site/investigator compensation and relevant financial interests. Use the Finnish Pharmaceutical Injuries Insurance and patient-insurance wording essentially as provided by Tukija unless a different insurance arrangement applies; optionally add the patient ombudsman/contact route. Explain end-of-study/post-trial care where relevant, treatment allocation after the study, publication of trial information/results in the EU database, and possible investigator/sponsor discontinuation and related measures. If several databases are mentioned, present the EU database first. For re-consent after important new information or document changes, make changes/additions clear and optionally add a summary of changes at the beginning. Do not use this adult-capable-participant document as the representative, minor or reduced-capacity document. Separate appropriately adapted information and consent/participation documents are required for those groups. The consent must confirm written and oral information, questions answered, adequate decision time, voluntariness, withdrawal rights, continued use of data collected before withdrawal, relevant foreign-authority/sponsor quality-assurance access, and dated signatures of the participant and consent recipient. Keep the original signed record in the investigator archive and provide the participant or representative with a paper copy or electronic record.

## S08 · Tietojen luottamuksellisuus ja tietosuoja
Fill: standard
Layout elements: H3 module title, Rich body text
Standard wording: Tietojen luottamuksellisuus ja tietosuoja
Standard wording: Tässä tutkimuksessa käsitellään henkilötietoja. Tutkimuksen rekisteriin tallennetaan vain tutkimuksen tarkoituksen kannalta välttämättömiä henkilötietoja. Ihmisillä tehtävä tutkimus on erilaisten tietojen keräämistä tutkittavasta, jotta voidaan muodostaa käsitys lääkkeen vaikutuksista ja ominaisuuksista. Tutkittavan henkilöllisyys on ainoastaan tutkimuksen henkilökunnan tiedossa, ja he kaikki ovat salassapitovelvollisia. Kaikkia tutkimuksessa kerättäviä tietoja ja otettuja näytteitä käsitellään pseudonymisoituina eli koodattuina. Yksilöivät tunnisteet (henkilötunnus, nimi jne.) on korvattu tunnistekoodilla, eikä tietoja tai näytteitä voida tunnistaa tutkimukseen liittyvistä tutkimustuloksista, selvityksistä tai julkaisuista. Nämä yksilöivät tunnisteet säilytetään erillään koodatuista tiedoista. Tutkittavan nimeä, henkilötunnusta tai yhteystietoja ei anneta tutkimuksen toimeksiantajalle (<i>poikkeuksena tutkijalähtöiset tutkimukset</i>). <i>(Tässä kohdassa kerrotaan myös se, että tutkimuksessa kerättyjä, tutkittavan hoidon kannalta relevantteja tietoja kirjataan potilastietojärjestelmään.)</i>
Standard wording: Henkilötietojen käsittelyperusteena on kliinisestä lääketutkimuksesta annetun lain 33 §:n mukaisesti keskeisten tutkimuksen suorittamiseen liittyvien käsittelytoimien osalta yleinen etu ja kansanterveyteen liittyvä yleinen etu (EU:n yleisen tietosuoja-asetuksen artiklat 6.1.e ja 9.2.i) sekä turvallisuusraportointiin ja muihin viranomaisille tehtäviin ilmoituksiin liittyen osalta lakisääteisen velvoitteen noudattaminen ja kansanterveyteen liittyvä yleinen etu (artiklat 6.1.c ja 9.2.i) <i>(JA/TAI muu rekisterinpitäjän valitsema EU:n yleisen tietosuoja-asetuksen mukainen käsittelyperuste)</i>.
Standard wording: Tutkimustuloksissa ja muissa asiakirjoissa tutkittavaan viitataan vain tunnistekoodilla. Rekisteriä säilytetään vähintään 25 vuotta <i>(tutkimuksen päättymisen jälkeen; niin kauan kuin lääkkeen myyntilupaan liittyvät tai muut säännökset edellyttävät jne.)</i>. ## Checklist
- C1.01 Identifies a clinical trial on medicinal products, not only a study.
- C1.02 Correct trial title, code and EU trial number.
- C1.03 Sponsor, Finnish site, principal investigator and controller identified correctly.
- C1.04 Primary and principal secondary objectives described in neutral lay language.
- C1.05 Investigational medicinal product, comparator, placebo, randomisation and blinding explained accurately.
- C1.06 Correct participant duration, visit count, remote contacts and follow-up period.
- C1.07 Standard care separated from trial-specific procedures and costs.
- C1.08 Planned global and Finnish participant numbers stated consistently.
- C1.09 Current site and research-team contact details included.
- C2.01 Dose, route and treatment duration match the protocol.
- C2.02 Invasive procedures, blood volumes, biopsies and radiation exposure match the protocol.
- C2.03 Contraception methods and duration match the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C2.04 Pregnancy testing, reporting and follow-up match the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C2.05 Prohibited medicines, washout and rescue treatment match the protocol.
- C2.06 Questionnaires, diaries, devices and home procedures match the protocol.
- C2.07 Genetic testing, sample types, storage and destruction match the protocol and sample plan. (applies when: samples.collected or samples.archive_used)
- C2.08 Severe and common risks given for every investigational and comparator product.
- C2.09 Stopping rules, post-trial treatment and unblinding match the protocol.
- C3.01 The person giving consent is correctly identified.
- C3.02 The qualified person conducting the prior interview is identified.
- C3.03 The participant or legal representative and the consent recipient sign and date at consent.
- C3.04 No signature or date is required on the blank CTIS submission copy.
- C3.05 No exculpatory wording waives legal rights or releases the sponsor or site from liability.
- C3.06 Withdrawal is possible without reason or detriment.
- C3.07 The document distinguishes stopping trial treatment, leaving trial procedures and withdrawing optional future-use consent.
- C3.08 The original signed record is archived and a copy is given to the participant or representative.
- C3.09 The procedure explains electronic or remote elements. (applies when: consent.electronic or any decentralised element)
- C3.10 Individual informed consent is used; Article 30 simplified cluster consent (non-objection) is not used in Finland. (applies when: trial.cluster)
- C4.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol maps to a population, populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage, and every population has its full document set (main, representative, age-adapted, re-consent and other applicable templates). (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Standard adult participants have the main information and consent set. (applies when: population.adult_patients)
- C4.03 Healthy volunteers have a tailored set: no therapeutic expectation, exposure and procedures, reproductive precautions, alternative of not participating, payment without undue influence. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.04 Children, adolescents and guardians: guardian document, age-appropriate documents per justified developmental group, the capable minor's written consent, the age-15 independent pathway only where Article 32(1)(g)(i) applies (guardian informed), dissent respected, re-consent at 18. - C4.05 Adults with reduced capacity and their representatives: separate representative documents, adapted participant information, representative per section 13 Act 983/2021, dissent respected, own consent when capacity returns. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.06 Emergency participants and later consent: every Article 35 condition, no retrospective consent statement, later consent without undue delay, right to object to use of data already obtained. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.07 Participants or representatives who cannot write: impartial-witness and alternative-record fields; inability to write not confused with language, literacy or incapacity. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.08 Pregnant participants and pregnant partners: participant follow-up within or beyond the main consent; separate partner document before partner data are collected; infant data pathway stated. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up or pregnancy.infant_follow_up)
