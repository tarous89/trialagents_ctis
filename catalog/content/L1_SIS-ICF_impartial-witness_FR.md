Template supplied for France, in French: **Document d’information et formulaire de consentement avec attestation d’un tiers indépendant** (Subject Information and Consent Form - Impartial Witness).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Adapt the document to the specific research, RIPH category, investigational product or device and participant population. Ensure consistency with the protocol without copying scientific protocol language. Use clear and understandable French, short sentences, active voice, non-technical terminology and consistent wording for each concept. Define abbreviations at first use and, where needed, in the glossary. Do not use the expression « tomber enceinte ». Clearly distinguish standard care from research-specific treatments, procedures, risks and constraints. Structure the information in three parts: information about the research; participant rights and management of collected data; and a glossary where useful. The version submitted to the Comité de protection des personnes must correspond to the version presented to participants; provide access links for electronic information or consent materials. Adapt and complete the participant-rights and data-protection wording with the controller and DPO details, legal basis, purposes, data categories, recipients, confidentiality measures, transfers, retention period, participant rights, CNIL complaint route, and secondary-use or SNDS information where applicable. Include specific prior written consent for genetic examinations and separate consent for optional studies. Include consent for sharing strictly necessary information with the treating physician where applicable. Prepare separate adapted documents for minors, protected adults and participants unable to consent where applicable. Use this version only for an adult who has capacity, makes the decision personally and cannot express consent in writing. Inability to read alone is not sufficient. Under Article L.1122-1-1, the consent may be attested by the trusted person, a family member or, failing that, a close relative, provided that person is independent of the investigator and sponsor. The attesting person does not decide for the participant. Read and explain all information and every consent choice in an understandable way, document the participant’s own oral or otherwise expressed decision, the reason written consent is impossible and the approved alternative recording method. The attesting person and the consent-taker sign and date the record. Do not add incapacity, representative or deferred-consent wording.
