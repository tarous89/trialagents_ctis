## Checklist
- C1.01 The German full study title on the participant document is identical to the German full title entered for Austria in CTIS.
- C1.02 Study purpose, treatment groups, randomisation, blinding and treatment duration match the protocol.
- C1.03 All participant procedures, visits and study-specific tests are described at the level needed for informed consent.
- C1.04 Stated risks and burdens reflect the current protocol and the current safety information used for the submission.
- C1.05 Expected benefit is not overstated and alternatives are described where relevant.
- C1.06 Pregnancy, contraception and pregnancy follow-up language matches the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C1.07 Reimbursement and compensation amounts, methods and conditions match the Part II financial-arrangements information (AT-P1).
- C2.01 Visit frequency, sample collection, imaging, biopsies, homecare, digital tools and optional procedures compared with the protocol line by line.
- C2.02 Every optional activity is clearly separated from mandatory trial procedures.
- C2.03 Patient-facing endpoint materials that belong in Part I are identified and the German language requirement is applied to their use in Austria. (applies when: endpoint_documents.participant_facing)
- C2.04 The document does not promise procedures, access, treatment or follow-up that the protocol does not support.
- C2.05 Withdrawal language does not imply that routine study visits or new study-purpose data collection continue after full trial withdrawal.
- C3.01 The main trial participation decision is distinct from optional choices.
- C3.02 Optional genetic investigations use a separate consent form (AustrianEthics). (applies when: genetics.optional_investigation)
- C3.03 Optional third-party services include the information and explicit choice required by the current Austrian ICF guidance. (applies when: reimbursement.external_service_provider)
- C3.04 Future research, sample anonymisation and new-technology secondary use have clear yes or no options where required. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol or samples.anonymisation_planned or data.secondary_use_new_technologies)
- C3.05 Signer logic matches the population: the capable adult signs for themselves; a legally designated representative signs when the participant cannot validly consent; an impartial witness signs only where the consenting person cannot write.
- C3.06 The person conducting the consent interview also signs and dates the informed consent document.
- C4.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol maps to a population, populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage, and every population has its full document set; the standard adult German PIS and ICF is present for the main population. (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Healthy volunteers (AT-HV): standard Austrian ICF structure and verbatim insurance and data-protection passages; no implied therapeutic benefit; purpose, exposure, procedures, confinement or visit burden, sampling, restrictions and risks explained; compensation consistent with P1; Austrian insurance contact point present. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.03 Minors (AT-MINORS): parent or representative document present; separate age-appropriate document for each enrolled band 8-9, 10-13, 14-17; decision-capable minor's own consent; child's right to refuse; trial stops if a required consent is missing or withdrawn; representative entitlement not hard-coded to one or two parents; re-consent at adulthood planned; privacy and insurance explained at the child's level without contradicting the representative document. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.04 Adults unable to consent (AT-CAPACITY): separate legal-representative document; information adapted to the participant's capacity; refusal or withdrawal respected; section 39 AMG no-prior-refusal condition where population-benefit research; section 39 AMG excluded groups not included. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.05 Temporary incapacity or emergency (AT-CAPACITY, AT-EMERGENCY): post-hoc information and consent document present; it explains what already happened, proposed continuing procedures, risks and benefits, the right to refuse continued participation and handling of data already collected; no retroactive approval; right to object to use of data on refusal; Article 35 conditions for emergencies. (applies when: population.temporary_incapacity or consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.06 Participant unable to write (AT-WITNESS): witness route used only for inability to write, not for a language barrier or lack of capacity; witness and interviewer signature and date fields; how consent was expressed is recorded; the participant receives a copy including the witness documentation. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.07 Pregnancy follow-up (AT-PREGNANCY): participant pregnancy handling stated (medication stopped, withdrawal right, continuing measures, unblinding, additional data and how obtained); separate document where data go beyond the protocol or pregnancy-monitoring form, child data after birth or treating-physician contact; planned duration stated; child follow-up beyond six months justified; partner document only when protocol-driven. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up or pregnancy.infant_follow_up or pregnancy.treating_physician_contact)
- C4.08 Secondary and future use (AT-SECONDARY): optional, separate from the main decision, refusal without loss of main participation, with the data and sample rules of section C6. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C5.01 The Austrian national sample data-protection passage is reproduced verbatim.
- C5.02 Any added privacy wording is placed after the required passage and does not contradict or repeat it.
- C5.03 The participant or representative is expressly informed in writing about the purpose and scope of personal-data collection and processing (section 41 AMG).
- C5.04 The effect of withdrawal on data already processed is explained in line with Austrian law and the current national wording.
