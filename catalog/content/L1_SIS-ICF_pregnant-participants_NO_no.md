Template supplied for Norway, in Norwegian: **Informasjon og samtykke til graviditetsoppfølging – studiedeltaker** (Pregnancy Follow-up and Consent - Participants).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Bruk bare for en kvinnelig studiedeltaker som blir gravid under hovedstudien eller i den protokollbestemte perioden etter siste eksponering. Dokumentet supplerer hovedsamtykket og gjelder kun innsamling av opplysninger om graviditet, mors helse, graviditetsutfall og eventuell oppfølging av barnet. Det skal ikke brukes som samtykke til å fortsette studielegemiddel eller andre eksperimentelle prosedyrer under graviditeten. Beskriv studiespesifikke risikoer og konkrete sikkerhetstiltak, avblinding, behandlingsstopp eller medisinsk oppfølging. Samle bare nødvendige opplysninger og bruk separat samtykke etter fødselen dersom nye opplysninger om barnet skal innhentes. Avslag eller tilbaketrekking skal ikke påvirke ordinær behandling. Ikke oppgi GDPR-behandlingsgrunnlag eller rettigheter i dette samtykket; separat personverninformasjon skal gis. Bruk som standard et konkret år som er minst 25 år etter End of Trial. Bruk samme år som i hovedstudiens informasjonsskriv, protokoll, personverninformasjon og sikkerhetsdokumentasjon, med mindre protokollen eller en uttrykkelig godkjenning fastsetter en annen periode.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Trial title, acronym, sponsor, EU trial number and participant group are correct and match the protocol and CTIS.
- C1.02 States clearly that the project is research, why the person is invited, and identifies the investigational treatment.
- C1.03 Randomisation, blinding, placebo, treatment duration, visit schedule and invasive procedures are correct.
- C1.04 Foreseeable risks and burdens are described without minimising uncertainty.
- C1.05 Expected benefit is stated accurately, including when no direct benefit is expected.
- C1.06 Alternatives to participation are explained where relevant.
- C1.07 Pregnancy testing, contraception, breastfeeding and partner precautions match the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C1.08 Payments, expense reimbursement and injury-insurance wording match the Part II documents (NO-P1, NO-O1).
- C1.09 Relevant conflicts of interest or institutional connections of those responsible for the study are disclosed.
- C1.10 Contacts are given for questions about the trial, injury, participant rights and privacy; the EU trial number and how results will become available are stated.
- C2.01 Population, age range, disease and inclusion pathway match the protocol.
- C2.02 Visit numbers, visit duration, home procedures, hospitalisation and follow-up match the protocol.
- C2.03 Sample types, volumes, timing, analyses and optional components match the protocol.
- C2.04 The protocol definition and expected date of the end of the clinical trial are stated and match.
- C2.05 Realistic dates are given for the end of all research activity (including publication) and for data deletion.
- C2.06 The data deletion date is at least 25 years after the end of the clinical trial.
- C2.07 Safety contacts, emergency arrangements and withdrawal procedures match the protocol.
- C2.08 Every optional substudy is presented as optional (and in its own document where the template requires it).
- C3.01 The correct consent-giver is identified for the population.
- C3.02 Participation is voluntary and the text has no waiver or liability-release language; it does not read as a contract.
- C3.03 The right to refuse and withdraw without disadvantage or a need to justify the decision is explained.
- C3.04 Main-study consent is separate from optional future use, extra samples and optional substudies.
- C3.05 Consent choices that can be accepted or refused independently are shown as such (separate dated signature modules).
- C3.06 The CTIS submission copy is unsigned.
- C3.07 At the consent visit, signatures and dates are required from the participant or legal representative and the person conducting the interview.
- C3.08 The participant or representative receives a copy of the signed consent record.
- C3.09 If consent is electronic, the recruitment procedure (K1) describes secure identity verification (BankID or another two-factor method). (applies when: consent.electronic)
- C3.10 If an impartial witness is needed, the witness signature and attestation logic are included. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol has a document set in the package (population x template table confirmed).
- C4.02 The standard Norwegian adult Patient Information Sheet and Informed Consent Form is present. (applies when: population.adult_patients)
- C4.03 A healthy-volunteer variation is present where healthy adults are enrolled. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.04 Age-appropriate forms follow the Norway pathway: guardian form, under-12 leaflet, 12-15 form, 16-17 form with own consent. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.05 Participant and representative versions are present under the limited-capacity pathway, with distinct titles, versions and dates. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.06 Pre-approved documents for emergency enrolment are present, including the Article 35(3) right-to-object letter. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.07 An impartial-witness process is present where a person cannot read or write. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.08 Separate information and consent for pregnancy follow-up are present where identifiable partner, pregnancy or infant data are collected. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up or pregnancy.infant_follow_up)
- C4.09 A separate broad-consent form is present where optional future research is proposed. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C5.01 The controller or controllers and relevant contact details are identified.
- C5.02 The categories and sources of personal data are explained.
- C5.03 Recipients, access and coding or pseudonymisation are described.
- C5.04 Transfers outside Norway and the European Economic Area are explained. (applies when: data.transfer_outside_eea)
- C5.05 Retention and deletion periods are stated consistently across the package.
- C5.06 Data handling after withdrawal is explained without promising deletion where applicable law requires continued retention.
- C5.07 Informed consent to trial participation is distinguished from the legal basis for processing under the GDPR; CTR consent is not presented as the GDPR legal basis.
- C5.08 The separate Part II GDPR statement (NO-R1) identifies its issuer and the party represented.
- C6.01 Every material collected and each planned analysis is identified. (applies when: samples.collected or samples.archive_used)
- C6.02 Trial samples are linked to the protocol endpoints. (applies when: samples.collected or samples.archive_used)
