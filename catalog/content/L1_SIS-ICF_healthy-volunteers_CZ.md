## Checklist
- C1.01 The full clinical trial title and EU CT Number are correct in every PIS/ICF version.
- C1.02 Sponsor identity and study purpose are correct.
- C1.03 Treatment arms, randomisation, placebo or comparator information and assignment probabilities match the protocol.
- C1.04 Visit schedules, procedures, sample collection, imaging, questionnaires and participant responsibilities match the protocol.
- C1.05 Treatment duration, follow-up period and any long-term follow-up match the protocol.
- C1.06 Investigational medicinal product route, frequency and relevant handling instructions match the protocol.
- C1.07 Participant-facing device instructions are included when self-use or device use requires them. (applies when: imp.self_administered or devices.used_in_trial)
- C1.08 No procedures or claims carried over from an older protocol version.
- C2.01 Foreseeable treatment and procedure risks are represented in understandable language; the document translates the protocol rather than reproducing it.
- C2.02 Pregnancy, contraception and breastfeeding restrictions match the protocol.
- C2.03 Prohibited concomitant treatments or lifestyle restrictions that materially affect participants are explained.
- C2.04 Expected benefits are stated cautiously and no benefit is promised.
- C2.05 Alternatives to participation are described where relevant.
- C2.06 Withdrawal procedures and post-withdrawal follow-up match the protocol.
- C2.07 Injury, insurance and reimbursement statements agree with the Part II insurance documents (CZ-O1, CZ-O2, CZ-O-POSTTRIAL) and the compensation information (CZ-P1).
- C3.01 The main trial participation decision is distinct from optional activities.
- C3.02 Optional future research, optional substudies or optional sample retention can be refused without making the main trial consent internally contradictory.
- C3.03 Each optional future-sample choice has the separate signature required by SÚKL or is in a separate signed document. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C3.04 An optional substudy independent of the main trial has its own information and consent document (KLH-22). (applies when: substudy.optional_independent)
- C3.05 The participant or legally designated representative signs only at the time of consent, not in the blank CTIS submission file.
- C3.06 The person conducting the consent interview signs and dates the executed consent record under the CTR process.
- C3.07 The form states that one dated and signed copy is provided to the participant and one remains at the site.
- C3.08 Re-consent triggers are defined when a child reaches a new age band, reaches adulthood, or an adult participant's capacity changes. (applies when: population.minors or population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol maps to a population, populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage, and every population has its full document set; the standard Czech PIS/ICF is present for the main population. (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Minors (CZ-MINORS): parent or representative document present; separate written Czech information for each enrolled band 12-14 and 15-17; age-appropriate information for children under 12 where meaningful (signature under 11 not mandatory); one-parent signature route; the minor's signature documents willingness and does not replace parental consent; dissent respected and not described as symbolic; re-consent at the next band and at 18. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.03 Limited capacity (CZ-CAPACITY): who gives legally valid consent follows the participant's legal status and the scope of any court ruling; legally designated representative document where required; information adapted to the participant's ability; participant involvement and right to object preserved; no invented representative hierarchy. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.04 Emergency enrolment (CZ-EMERGENCY): the package describes the Article 35 inclusion conditions, how urgency is identified and documented, and how consent to continue is obtained without undue delay; the right to object to use of data already obtained is stated. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.05 Impartial witness (CZ-WITNESS): for a participant who cannot write or cannot read, the witness procedure, the witness's independence from the study team and the witness signature statement are described; witness wording is not confused with representative consent or emergency enrolment. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.06 Healthy volunteers (CZ-HV): absence of therapeutic intent, study procedures, restrictions, risks (not softened for First-in-Human) and the compensation structure are explained accurately. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.07 Pregnancy follow-up (CZ-PREGNANCY): follow-up of a pregnant participant is separated from collection of information about a pregnant partner; the partner's own consent, data scope, record access and follow-up duration match the protocol. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up or pregnancy.infant_follow_up)
- C5.01 Participant-facing privacy information matches the actual data flow, recipients and transfers.
- C5.02 The PIS/ICF contains SÚKL's required access-to-source-record wording covering monitors, auditors, regulators or medicines agencies, Ethics Committee members and study-team members as applicable.
- C5.03 Sponsor and controller descriptions are consistent across the PIS/ICF and the Part II data-protection documents (CZ-R1).
- C5.04 Data withdrawal language does not promise deletion that the trial's legal and scientific record cannot support.
- C5.05 The separate sponsor-signed SÚKL Template No. 5 is included in the Part II data-protection category (CZ-R1).
