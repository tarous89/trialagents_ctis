Template supplied for Spain, in Spanish: **Hoja de información y consentimiento para el seguimiento del embarazo de la pareja** (Pregnancy Follow-up and Consent - Partners).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Prepare a separate, narrowly focused information and consent form for the pregnant partner of a male trial participant. State clearly that she is not enrolled in the clinical trial, receives no investigational product and is deciding only about pregnancy-safety follow-up and the collection and use of the data described. Explain the relevant paternal exposure and any known, suspected or unknown reproductive risk in concise lay language. Collect only protocol-required data and specify the real sources, contact methods, timepoints and end of follow-up. Do not add research procedures, samples, genetics, secondary or future use, treatment alternatives, trial-treatment discontinuation, participant insurance or other main-trial modules. Refusal or withdrawal must not affect the pregnant partner, the baby, ordinary healthcare or the male participant's trial participation; the male participant cannot consent for the partner. When identifiable health follow-up of the child after birth is planned, use the conditional post-birth consent block or another arrangement expressly approved for the study, adapt it to the actual parental-responsibility situation, and do not assume that antenatal consent automatically covers new collection of the child's data. Include a concise pregnancy-specific data-protection appendix covering the actual controllers, purposes, data categories, coded linkage, source-record access, recipients, international transfers, retention, rights, withdrawal and contacts. State the real legal basis or bases for each controller and purpose; do not present consent as the sole GDPR basis where legal obligations, safety duties or public-interest grounds apply. Comply with Spanish CEIm/AEMPS requirements, GDPR, LOPDGDD and, where applicable, the Farmaindustria Code of Conduct. Remove every option and instruction that does not apply to the study before use.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Study title, protocol code, sponsor and EU trial number are the same as in the application.
- C1.02 The document title clearly identifies the population or use case.
- C1.03 Site and investigator fields are correctly completed or intentionally left as controlled placeholders for site completion.
- C1.04 The introduction states that participation is offered in a research study that has received the required CEIm and AEMPS review, and invites the person to read, ask questions and consult others.
- C1.05 Study purpose, intended population, total planned participant number, treatments, treatment groups and the chance of receiving each option match the protocol; no full inclusion and exclusion list.
- C1.06 States whether the study medicine is authorised and whether its use is within the authorised indication.
- C1.07 Benefits described only when supported; states that the participant may receive no health benefit; free medicine, tests, results and closer follow-up are not presented as benefits.
- C1.08 Main effective treatment alternatives listed, including not joining; says so if the same medicine may be available in normal care.
- C1.09 Treatment after the trial: whether and under what conditions the study treatment may remain available.
- C1.10 Contacts: study doctor name, service, telephone and availability; urgent issues directed to usual or emergency care; participants told to disclose participation elsewhere and carry any trial card.
- C1.11 New information affecting willingness to continue will be communicated promptly; circumstances in which the sponsor or investigator may stop participation and follow-up that continues are explained.
- C1.12 No statements carried over from another protocol, country, cohort or study phase.
- C2.01 Treatment groups, allocation ratio, blinding, placebo, dose, route, schedule and treatment duration match the current protocol.
- C2.02 Number and timing of visits, remote contacts, hospital stays and follow-up match the protocol; total participation period with treatment and follow-up separated.
- C2.03 All trial-specific procedures (optional procedures, biopsies, imaging, genetic tests, digital tools, home nursing, medical-record review) are described and each marked as routine care or research-specific where relevant.
- C2.04 Contraception, pregnancy testing, breastfeeding and pregnancy follow-up match the protocol, without requirements absent from it. (applies when: participants or their partners could become pregnant)
- C2.05 Risk section reconciled with the IB or SmPC and the protocol: most frequent and most serious risks without an exhaustive technical list; unknown risks acknowledged; procedure-related risks not omitted.
- C2.06 Participant responsibilities: attending activities, reporting adverse events and medication changes, consulting the study doctor before changing medicines or herbal products except in an emergency.
- C2.07 Home activities, digital tools, travel to another site or access to medical records are explained. (applies when: any decentralised element or record access)
- C2.08 After a protocol change, every affected participant document has been reopened, not only the main adult form. (applies when: modification or revised protocol)
- C3.01 Every mandatory trial activity is distinguished from optional research; a participant cannot decline a protocol-essential procedure while apparently consenting to the main trial.
- C3.02 Optional substudies, future data use, future sample use and recontact each have a separate yes or no decision.
- C3.03 Refusing an optional activity does not imply withdrawal from the main study.
- C3.04 Optional boxes are not preselected and each choice has the correct signature logic.
- C3.05 Withdrawal wording does not require a signed revocation form; if the protocol has one, the PIS and the form say withdrawal remains valid without signing it.
- C3.06 Withdrawal distinguishes leaving trial visits, withdrawing trial consent and withdrawing optional future-use consent, and explains what happens to data and samples (no use in later analyses after withdrawal unless another lawful route or new consent applies).
- C3.07 Participation is voluntary; refusal does not affect care; consent can be withdrawn at any time without penalty or loss of the normal relationship with the treating team.
- C3.08 The consent form confirms that the participant read the PIS and the data-protection appendix, could ask questions, received enough information, understood voluntariness and agrees, without repeating the information sheet.
- C4.01 A population matrix from the protocol and recruitment plan exists; every group that may be enrolled or followed has a distinct, appropriate document pathway (see requirements.json population_sets); populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage. (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Transition points covered: adult consent at legal adulthood; consent before continuing when an adult regains capacity; information as soon as possible and consent or ratification after emergency enrolment. (applies when: always)
- C4.03 Healthy volunteers: no expected therapeutic benefit stated; reason for healthy volunteers; full burden (confinement, fasting, blood volume, restrictions with periods); first-in-human uncertainty and safeguards; handling of screening findings. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.04 Adults unable to consent: accessible participant information with support to consent personally where possible; representative or person connected by family or factual relationship identified with basis; prior instructions checked; objection and non-verbal resistance respected; re-consent when capacity returns. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.05 Emergency enrolment: used only when the protocol and CEIm-approved documents contain the pathway; Article 35 and Royal Decree 1090/2015 article 7 conditions; family consultation; what already happened versus what continues; consent or ratification; right to object to use of data. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)

- C4.07 Pregnancy: the main PIS has the pregnancy section; the pregnant partner of a male participant has her own specific consent document; infant data have the parent or guardian route; follow-up has a defined endpoint. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up or pregnancy.infant_follow_up)
- C4.08 Optional future data and sample use has its own choice or document (see C6). (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C4.09 An optional substudy for a defined subpopulation has a separate information and consent document; a substudy open to all is a separate section of the main PIS with a yes or no choice. (applies when: substudy.optional_subpopulation)
