Template supplied for Hungary, in Hungarian: **Tájékoztatáson alapuló beleegyező nyilatkozat – nyilatkozattételre jogosult személy** (Trial Information and Consent Form - Legal Representative).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Ezt a sablont kizárólag olyan felnőtt vizsgálati alany esetén használja, aki nem képes érvényes tájékoztatáson alapuló beleegyezést adni, és a részvételhez az Eütv. 16. § szerinti nyilatkozattételre jogosult személy döntése szükséges. A dokumentum címzettje a nyilatkozattételre jogosult személy; a klinikai vizsgálati kezelést, vizsgálatokat, kockázatokat, adatkezelést és mintavételeket mindig a képviselt felnőtt résztvevőre vonatkoztatva írja le. Ellenőrizze és dokumentálja a nyilatkozattételi jogosultság jogalapját és terjedelmét. A résztvevőt saját megértési képességéhez igazodó módon is tájékoztatni kell és véleményét/ellenkezését a lehető legnagyobb mértékben figyelembe kell venni; ha képes érdemi véleményt kifejezni, használja mellette a limited-capacity sablont. Ha visszanyeri beleegyezési képességét, a folytatás előtt a standard felnőtt ICF-fel saját beleegyezését kell kérni. A teljes fő vizsgálati tartalmat a felnőtt fő sablon logikája szerint őrizze meg, beleértve a magyar KFEB adatvédelmi, betegkártya-, biztosítási/kártérítési, mintakezelési és genetikai szabályokat. A fő vizsgálaton kívüli jövőbeni/másodlagos felhasználást ne tartalmazza. A nyilatkozattételre jogosult személy és a résztvevő személyes adatait világosan különítse el. A képviselő döntéséért vagy a sérülékeny résztvevő részvételéért ösztönző díjazást ne adjon; kizárólag a ténylegesen felmerült és igazolt költségek térítését ismertesse. Egy dokumentumhoz egyetlen végleges aláírássorozat tartozzon. A végleges, jogilag beleegyezést tartalmazó dokumentum címében tartsa meg a KFEB által használt „Tájékoztatáson alapuló beleegyező nyilatkozat” megnevezést. A nyilatkozattételre jogosult személy beleegyezése tükrözze a vizsgálati alany feltételezett akaratát. Nem vonható be az a személy, aki korábban cselekvőképessége birtokában általában kizárta a klinikai vizsgálati részvételt, vagy az adott vizsgálathoz korábban megtagadta a beleegyezést. A bevonás előtt ellenőrizze az Eütv. 159. § (4)–(5) és a 35/2005. EüM rendelet 7/A–9. § szerinti feltételek teljesülését. Ha a nagykorú csak részlegesen korlátozott és az alkalmazandó jog szerint saját beleegyezése is szükséges, a résztvevő saját beleegyezését/aláírását is dokumentálja; a limited-capacity véleménynyilatkozat ezt nem helyettesíti.

## Checklist
- C1.01 EU clinical trial number, protocol number and trial title are correct and identical across all participant documents.
- C1.02 The Hungarian site and healthcare-provider details match the submitted site package (N1).
- C1.03 Investigator or information-provider names and roles are current.
- C1.04 Study purpose, duration, planned participant numbers, procedures, intervention frequency and visit burden match the protocol.
- C1.05 Accepted alternatives to trial participation are described accurately, including whether joining means interrupting an existing treatment and what that could mean.
- C1.06 Known and reasonably foreseeable risks, burdens and discomforts are consistent with the protocol and current safety information, including the possibility of unforeseen adverse events.
- C1.07 The document says explicitly when no direct participant benefit is expected.
- C1.08 The probability and practical meaning of placebo assignment are explained. (applies when: trial.placebo)
- C1.09 The investigational medicinal product and any necessary plain-language mechanism description are correct.
- C2.01 Contraception, pregnancy testing and pregnancy-reporting wording matches the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C2.02 Sample types, volumes, collection times and analyses match the protocol. (applies when: samples.collected)
- C2.03 Data and sample retention periods match the approved plan.
- C2.04 Reimbursement and travel arrangements match the financial documents (HU-P1).
- C2.05 Emergency contact arrangements match the site information and the patient emergency card. (applies when: not trial.phase_iv_or_bioequivalence)
- C2.06 Every optional substudy in the protocol has a corresponding optional consent pathway where needed.
- C2.07 The protocol contains no participant-facing procedure that is absent from the Hungarian information.
- C3.01 The participant receives the information before signing.
- C3.02 The participant or authorised person has the correct signature and date fields; where a Section 16 Health Act authorised person consents, their identity details are in the consent document.
- C3.03 The principal investigator or person providing the information has the required signature field.
- C3.04 Each separate consent document has one coherent signature set; signature pages are not duplicated.
- C3.05 Optional decisions are separated from mandatory participation decisions.
- C3.06 Withdrawal is described as possible without reason and without disadvantage.
- C3.07 The document explains any lawful consequences of withdrawal, such as handling of data already collected.
- C3.08 Genetic consent is not merged into the main ICF: separate genetic information and consent documents are present. (applies when: genetics.testing_planned)
- C4.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol maps to a population, populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage, and each population has its full Hungarian document set (playbook section 2a). (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Adults with capacity have the standard Hungarian ICF. (applies when: population.adult_patients)
- C4.03 Healthy volunteers have wording suitable for a person with no therapeutic indication and, where relevant, transparent compensation information (healthy-volunteer guide). (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.04 Minors have age- and maturity-appropriate information plus the correct parent or legal-representative pathway (minor consent and assent guide); the minor's opinion and dissent handling are explained. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.05 Adults with restricted capacity have participant and representative material appropriate to the legal situation (limited-capacity guide). (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.06 Emergency-enrolment trials have a prospectively described later-information and consent pathway (emergency enrolment guide). (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.07 If a participant cannot read or write, the form supports Hungary's two-witness oral-consent process (impartial witness guide). (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.08 Pregnancy follow-up has separate documents for the participant or pregnant partner where the data subject and consent decision differ (pregnancy follow-up guide). (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up)
- C4.09 Optional future use is covered by a separate or clearly separable choice (secondary-use guide). (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C5.01 The sponsor and other controllers or processors are identified as required by the approved privacy model.
- C5.02 The categories of personal and special-category data actually collected are listed, with consistent terminology.
- C5.03 'Coded', 'pseudonymised' and 'anonymous' are used consistently; the same transfer is not described as anonymous in one section and coded in another.
- C5.04 The document explains which parties may access source records and under what conditions (at the site, as described by ETT-KFEB).
- C5.05 The document explains how data leave the site and whether the code key stays at the site.
- C5.06 Recipients or recipient categories are named specifically enough for the participant to understand.
- C5.07 Purpose of processing and the legal basis described in the Hungary-approved wording are stated (ETT-KFEB expects consent; checked against the sponsor's GDPR analysis).
- C5.08 Storage duration and location are stated.
- C5.09 Third-country transfers and their safeguards are explained. (applies when: data.transfer_outside_eea)
- C5.10 The participant's rights and NAIH contact information are included.
- C5.11 The third-party reimbursement service: exactly which data it receives and why. (applies when: reimbursement.external_service_provider)
- C5.12 A separate pregnant-partner information sheet contains privacy information for the partner. (applies when: pregnancy.partner_follow_up)
- C5.13 A substudy with a separate information sheet includes or appropriately cross-references the privacy information for that substudy. (applies when: consent.separate_substudy_decision)
- C5.14 Privacy information is integrated into the participant document; a separate privacy appendix, if used, has its own participant or healthy-volunteer signature field and the main ICF refers to it.
- C6.01 The biological samples taken and the primary study purpose are stated. (applies when: samples.collected)
- C6.02 Storage duration, location, coding and transfer are stated. (applies when: samples.collected)
- C6.03 What happens to samples after the primary research purpose ends is stated. (applies when: samples.collected)
- C6.04 Future use is optional when it is not required for the main study. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.05 For non-anonymous secondary use, the future purpose was explained at the original consent; otherwise renewed consent is planned (ETT-KFEB). (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.06 Genetic sampling uses the separate Hungarian genetic information and consent structure with the choices required under the Genetics Act (permitted uses and storage forms). (applies when: genetics.testing_planned)
- C6.07 Withdrawal instructions distinguish identifiable or coded material from material already irreversibly anonymised. (applies when: samples.collected)
- C7.01 The insurer is named.
- C7.02 The insurer's Hungarian contact details are included.
- C7.03 The contact person's name and telephone number are included.
- C7.04 The document explains whom the participant contacts after trial-related injury.
- C7.05 Treatment and compensation rights are explained in language consistent with Hungarian law (Act XCV of 2005, Section 21).
- C7.06 If reimbursement is offered, the exact documented cost types are listed. (applies when: reimbursement offered)
- C7.07 Vague wording such as 'may reimburse' is removed where the sponsor has defined reimbursable costs. (applies when: reimbursement offered)
- C7.08 An external reimbursement provider's relationship with the participant and the data flows are explained. (applies when: reimbursement.external_service_provider)
- C7.09 A final Hungarian patient emergency card is included for the trial. (applies when: not trial.phase_iv_or_bioequivalence)
- C7.10 The card has participant name and date-of-birth fields. (applies when: not trial.phase_iv_or_bioequivalence)
- C7.11 The card identifies the protocol and the investigational medicinal product. (applies when: not trial.phase_iv_or_bioequivalence)
- C7.12 The mechanism is added when the product name alone does not explain the relevant safety context. (applies when: not trial.phase_iv_or_bioequivalence)
- C7.13 The card gives indication, contraindications and dangerous interactions. (applies when: not trial.phase_iv_or_bioequivalence)
- C7.14 The card gives the site address and a 24-hour medical contact (investigator or designated physician). (applies when: not trial.phase_iv_or_bioequivalence)
- C7.15 The ICF tells the participant that the card will be issued, what personal data it shows, and to carry it during the trial. (applies when: not trial.phase_iv_or_bioequivalence)
- C7.16 If participant identity and study code appear together on the card, the patient-safety reason is explained and consented in the ICF. (applies when: not trial.phase_iv_or_bioequivalence)
- C7.17 The ETT-KFEB exception for Phase IV and bioequivalence trials was checked before creating or omitting a card; the exception is applied only where the trial genuinely falls within those categories.
- C8.01 Every document has a version number and date consistent with the Hungarian Part II document list (HU-DOC-LIST).
- C8.02 No participant-facing form is pre-signed for CTIS submission.
- C8.03 The main ICF contains participant or authorised-person and investigator or information-provider signature and date fields.
- C8.04 Two witness signature fields plus witness address or contact fields are present where the oral-consent pathway can apply. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C8.05 Genetic documents each have their own coherent signature set. (applies when: genetics.testing_planned)
- C8.06 Pregnancy-partner documents are signed by the pregnant partner when her data or records are collected. (applies when: pregnancy.partner_follow_up)
- C8.07 The Hungarian cover letter and document list identify the same versions.
- C8.08 The consent record contains the participant's name, place of birth and date of birth, and no participant study code number.
- C8.09 Correct CTIS section and naming convention (requirements.json ctis_title, _HU_hu).
- C8.10 No personal data that should not appear in the CTIS upload.
- C8.11 No drafting instruction remains in the final version (italic notes, [bracket] notes, ........... gaps, yellow [INSERT/IF/OTHERWISE/CHOOSE/NOTE] labels); fields completed at the consent visit stay blank.
- C9.01 Final Hungarian text is used for participant-facing and recruitment material.
- C9.02 The text has been reviewed by a competent Hungarian-language reviewer with clinical-trial knowledge.
- C9.03 Formal address and verb forms are consistent (Ön in adult documents; tegező in child and adolescent documents as the templates set).
- C9.04 Literal English constructions that sound unnatural or change meaning are removed.
- C9.05 Foreign-law references that do not apply in Hungary are removed and replaced by the Hungarian and EU rules that apply.
- C9.06 Medical and regulatory terms are explained understandably.
- C9.07 Headings and consent choices are visually clear.
- C9.08 Terminology is consistent across the main ICF, recruitment materials, emergency card, pregnancy documents, genetic consent and substudy information.
- C9.09 A child-facing document is written for the child, not a literal translation of the adult ICF. (applies when: population.minors)
- C10.01 No protocol fact, consent giver, optional choice, data transfer, sample purpose, payment or signature block is unresolved; each resolution is recorded and the affected checks rerun.
