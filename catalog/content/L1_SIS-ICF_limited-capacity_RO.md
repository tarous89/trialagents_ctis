Template supplied for Romania, in Romanian: **Informații adaptate pentru participantul adult cu capacitate limitată / Exprimarea voinței privind participarea** (Subject Information and Assent Form - Limited Capacity).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Utilizați numai pentru un adult care nu poate oferi singur consimțământ informat valabil, dar este în măsură să primească informații adaptate, să își formeze o opinie și să evalueze cel puțin informațiile relevante pentru participare. Documentul însoțește obligatoriu consimțământul reprezentantului legal și nu îl înlocuiește. Conform art. 15 alin. (3) din Ordinul nr. 3390/2022, pentru această categorie sunt obligatorii atât semnătura participantului, cât și semnătura reprezentantului legal pe formularul de consimțământ informat. Asigurați-vă că formularul reprezentantului legal conține ambele semnături; semnătura din acest document adaptat este suplimentară și documentează informarea și voința participantului. Folosiți propoziții scurte, materiale vizuale sau mijloace de comunicare asistată după nevoie. Explicați natura de cercetare, ce se va întâmpla, principalele riscuri/neplăceri, beneficiul posibil sau lipsa lui, alternativele, voluntaritatea, datele/probele și contactele. Participantul poate refuza sau se poate opune participării ori unei proceduri, iar opoziția sa trebuie respectată. Dacă își recapătă capacitatea de consimțământ, obțineți propriul consimțământ informat pentru continuare. Nu includeți cercetare viitoare/secundară. Nu oferiți stimulente; numai cheltuieli/pierderi de venit direct legate de participare pot fi compensate, conform Anexei nr. 7.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Trial title, protocol number, investigational treatment names and study duration are the same as in the current protocol.
- C1.02 Randomisation, placebo or comparator use, treatment assignment and the possibility of receiving no direct benefit are described accurately.
- C1.03 Visit frequency, procedures, hospital stays, remote activities, sample collection, imaging and participant burden match the protocol.
- C1.04 Purpose, procedures, foreseeable risks and burdens, expected benefits where applicable, alternatives, voluntariness and the right to withdraw are explained.
- C1.05 Trial-related injury arrangements, reimbursement or compensation, privacy and data handling, access to medical records where relevant, biological samples, pregnancy precautions and follow-up where applicable, and contacts for the trial and participant rights are explained.
- C2.01 Important risks and safety procedures match the current protocol and Investigator's Brochure.
- C2.02 Pregnancy prevention, contraception, pregnancy testing and follow-up language match the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C2.03 Withdrawal language does not promise deletion of data or samples where legal or scientific obligations require retention.
- C2.04 Every optional procedure in the ICF is genuinely optional in the protocol.
- C3.01 The participant-facing document is in Romanian and the translation is complete, not only the signature page.
- C3.02 Plain Romanian suitable for the intended population; no unexplained scientific abbreviations.
- C3.03 Information adapted to age and ability to understand. (applies when: population.minors or population.adults_without_capacity)
- C3.04 Non-Romanian speakers: translated information and consent documents are planned and the recruitment and consent procedure explains how valid consent will be obtained. (applies when: population.non_romanian_speakers)
- C4.01 The consent interview at each Romanian site is conducted by a physician who is a member of the investigation team and appropriately trained on the protocol and GCP (Order 3390/2022); the physician's declaration and signature block say so.
- C4.02 The blank ICF has the correct signature blocks for every planned pathway.
- C4.03 Impartial-witness pathway added where the participant can consent but cannot write; the witness signs and dates the consent document. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.04 Consent to participate in the trial is distinguished from the legal framework for processing personal data.
- C4.05 Optional choices are clearly optional with a separate choice or clearly separated signature or initials section; refusing them does not affect main participation.
- C5.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol maps to a population with the correct document set, and populations are mutually exclusive within a consent stage. (applies when: always)
- C5.02 Minors: representative consent, age-appropriate participant information, the capable minor's agreement, respect for refusal, re-consent at 18. (applies when: population.minors)
- C5.03 Adults lacking capacity: how capacity is assessed, who acts as legal representative, how the participant is involved; both participant and representative signatures where the participant can form an opinion and assess the information; re-consent if capacity returns. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C5.04 Emergency enrolment: the protocol and national recruitment form describe why prior consent cannot be obtained and how consent is sought without undue delay; known prior objection respected; data already collected not promised to be deleted. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C5.05 Pregnancy follow-up: the intended person (participant or pregnant partner) and the exact maternal, pregnancy-outcome or infant data, follow-up period, record access and recipients are clear. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up)
- C5.06 Future research: the intended person and the exact data or samples are clear, and the optional choice does not look like a condition of joining. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C5.07 Healthy volunteers: absence of therapeutic benefit, investigational status, dose escalation and uncertainty where applicable, admissions, fasting, restrictions, blood volume and confinement are explained. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C6.01 Privacy language matches the actual data flow, including sponsor access, monitoring, transfers, retention and rights.
- C6.02 Participation consent is separate from the explanation of personal-data processing; trial consent is not presented as the only legal basis for all processing.
- C6.03 No wording suggests that withdrawal automatically erases data already lawfully collected.
- C6.04 Privacy contact details are correct for the sponsor and Romanian sites where applicable.
- C7.01 Lists the samples collected, why, where they are analysed or stored and how they are linked to the participant. (applies when: samples.collected or samples.archive_used)
- C7.02 Future use outside the main protocol: purpose, storage period, access and withdrawal logic stated clearly. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C7.03 Withdrawal of future-use permission distinguishes material still linkable from irreversibly anonymised material; no promise of destruction where impossible or where retention applies. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C7.04 Consistent with the S1 biological-sample information and the data-protection declaration. (applies when: samples.collected or samples.archive_used)
- C8.01 The participant information matches the compensation form (P1) and the insurance evidence (O1) submitted for Romania.
- C8.02 Where the national compensation form refers to the participant information instead of repeating payment details, the cross-reference is exact.
- C8.03 Minors, adults lacking capacity and pregnant or breastfeeding participants: compensation limited to expenses and loss of earnings directly related to participation, not presented as a financial inducement.
- C8.04 Payments are not presented as a guarantee of benefit from the investigational product. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C9.01 Correct protocol number, country, language, version and date.
- C9.02 Signature blocks correspond to the intended consent pathway.
- C9.03 No participant, representative, witness or physician signature in the blank CTIS submission copy.
- C9.04 The site suitability and investigator forms of the package use the current national forms and required signatures.
- C9.05 No tracked changes, hidden comments or placeholder text.
- C9.06 No drafting instruction remains in the final version (italic notes, [bracket] notes, 'Instrucțiune pentru autor' passages, gaps), except consent-visit fields left blank.
- C9.07 Correct CTIS section and naming convention.
- C10.01 Recruitment materials are in Romanian and consistent with the recruitment procedure (K1). (applies when: recruitment.material_used)
- C10.02 Electronic recruitment resources: the plan reflects the requirement for prior CNBMDM approval. (applies when: recruitment.electronic_resources)
- C10.03 No claims about efficacy, safety or guaranteed benefit beyond the protocol and approved participant information. (applies when: recruitment.material_used)
- C11.01 For a new Part II submission since 1 September 2026, the recruitment and consent procedure uses the EU v2.0 template and the current ANMDMR form was verified before upload.
- C12.01 The participant-facing set matches the Romania Part II document checklist and the Romania patient information and consent guide; names, visits, study duration, allocation, risks, pregnancy rules, reimbursement, sample handling, privacy contacts, withdrawal and version dates tell the same story across all documents.
