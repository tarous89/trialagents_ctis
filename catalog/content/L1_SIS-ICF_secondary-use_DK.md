Template supplied for Denmark, in Danish: **Information og valg om sekundær eller fremtidig brug af data og biologisk materiale** (Secondary and Future Use of Data or Biosamples).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Brug som et selvstændigt og frivilligt dokument kun til data eller biologisk materiale, som enten indsamles ekstra eller hentes fra et eksisterende arkiv alene med henblik på fremtidig forskning uden for hovedforsøgets formål. Vælg én af de to veje og slet den anden. Brug ikke skabelonen til overskydende biologisk materiale fra hovedforsøget; dette håndteres i den relevante hoveddeltagerinformation og hovedsamtykkeerklæring. Dokumentet må ikke gentage forsøgsbehandling, randomisering, besøg, almindelige forsøgsrisici eller hovedforsøgets obligatoriske prøver. Et afslag eller en senere tilbagetrækning må ikke påvirke hovedforsøget, almindelig behandling eller andre rettigheder. Et konkret senere forskningsprojekt skal have en særskilt projektspecifik information og samtykkeprocedure efter de regler, der gælder for det nye projekt. Efter danske regler kan et bredt samtykke ikke i sig selv godkende ukendte fremtidige projekter med biologisk materiale. Ethvert nyt projekt skal vurderes efter de gældende regler, anmeldes til videnskabsetisk komité, når det kræves, og der skal som udgangspunkt indhentes nyt samtykke, medmindre komitéen giver dispensation. Beskriv præcist data-/materialetyper, kilde, mængde, forskningsområde, genetiske analyser, ekstra procedurer, administrator, sted, adgang, modtagere, tredjelande, opbevaringsperiode, publikation, tilbagemelding og begrænsninger ved tilbagetrækning. Brug separate ja/nej-valg kun for reelt uafhængige muligheder. Vælg én dansk beslutningsvej og slet de andre: samtykkekompetent voksen; barn under 15 år med forældresamtykke og barnets inddragelse; ung 15-17 år med eget samtykke og forældresamtykke; eller voksen uden handleevne via den lovlige stedfortrædervej. For voksne uden handleevne må skabelonen kun bruges, hvis den konkrete danske juridiske og etiske vurdering accepterer stedfortrædende beslutning; deltagernes protest skal altid respekteres. Medtag ikke graviditetsopfølgning. <i>[Redaktionsnote: Hvis personoplysninger eller biologisk materiale behandles i eller overføres til lande uden for EU/EØS, angiv de konkrete lande, formålet med hver behandling eller overførsel og de relevante garantier efter GDPR kapitel V. Tilføj ikke et særskilt Ja/Nej-valg alene for overførslen. Hvis overførslen kun sker som led i en reelt valgfri og uafhængig aktivitet, knyttes Ja/Nej-valget til selve aktiviteten og ikke til GDPR-overførselsmekanismen.]</i> Bevar redaktionsnoterne i denne skabelon. Alle instruktioner til skabelonbrugeren skal markeres som <i>[Redaktionsnote: ...]</i>. Redaktionsnoter, tomme alternativer og ikke-relevante moduler fjernes først fra det endelige deltagerdokument før udlevering til deltageren eller beslutningstageren.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Trial purpose and treatment groups are described accurately.
- C1.02 Investigational and comparator treatments are described in terms a participant can understand.
- C1.03 Visit schedule and participant duration are described accurately.
- C1.04 Randomisation and blinding are explained where relevant.
- C1.05 Invasive procedures, imaging, biopsies, blood draws and other burdens are described.
- C1.06 Optional procedures and substudies are presented as optional, not as part of the mandatory main trial.
- C1.07 Expected risks, common important adverse effects and material uncertainties are described.
- C1.08 Expected benefit is stated without implying benefit the protocol does not support; a clear statement when direct benefit is not expected.
- C1.09 Alternatives to participation or standard treatment are described where relevant.
- C1.10 Pregnancy prevention and follow-up procedures are described where relevant. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C1.11 Compensation, reimbursement and the insurance arrangement (Patienterstatningen or independent insurance) are described.
- C1.12 Contacts for the trial and participant rights, and how trial results will be available as required by the CTR, are given.
- C1.13 The information explains what the protocol means for a Danish participant and does not merely copy protocol language.
- C2.01 Visit number and timing match the protocol.
- C2.02 Treatment duration and follow-up period match the protocol.
- C2.03 Sample volumes and sample frequency match the protocol. (applies when: samples.collected)
- C2.04 Optional versus mandatory biopsies or samples match the protocol. (applies when: samples.collected)
- C2.05 Contraception periods match the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C2.06 Record-based follow-up after treatment or withdrawal matches the protocol. (applies when: data.record_follow_up_after_withdrawal)
- C2.07 Countries and laboratories receiving data or material match the protocol.
- C2.08 Retention periods match the protocol and are consistent across documents.
- C2.09 Endpoints or analyses that can generate potentially significant findings are identified consistently with the protocol. (applies when: samples.significant_health_findings_likely)
- C2.10 No participant-facing statement changes a scientific or operational fact rather than explaining it.
- C2.11 After a protocol change, every affected participant document has been reopened, not only the main adult form. (applies when: modification or revised protocol)
- C3.01 The consent procedure states that oral information is provided by a duly qualified physician with Danish authorisation who has appropriate clinical-trial experience, GCP knowledge and knowledge of the specific trial.
- C3.02 The procedure normally names the interviewing physician as the person receiving consent; where consent follows a reflection period, another qualified, delegated physician may receive it, answer questions and attest separately, with a separate attestation block in the ICF. (applies when: consent.delegated_consent_physician)
- C3.03 Where a physician other than the principal investigator performs the process, a written delegation agreement with the principal investigator is confirmed. (applies when: consent.delegated_consent_physician)
- C3.04 The blank ICF submitted in CTIS is not pre-signed.
- C3.05 At consent the participant or legally designated representative and the interviewing physician sign and date; the witness pathway applies where the person cannot write.
- C3.06 The participant or representative receives a copy of the document by which consent was given; adequate time to decide; understanding verified during the interview.
- C3.07 The person signing has legal authority for the decision (participant, holder or holders of parental authority, nearest relative plus trial guardian, or a guardian whose guardianship covers research consent).
- C3.08 The consent form matches the participant category; the competent-adult form is not used for a legal-representative pathway.
- C3.09 The ethics consent to participation is not presented as GDPR consent; the main ICF does not regulate ordinary data-law matters; any distinct GDPR consent is clearly separated.
- C3.10 Re-consent triggers are defined: reaching 18, turning 15, regained capacity, subsequent consent after emergency inclusion, new information.
