**Content instructions:** Адаптирайте документа към протокола, популацията и центъра в България. Окончателният документ трябва да бъде на български език; за CTIS се подава и съответната версия за България на английски език. Спазвайте Регламент (ЕС) № 536/2014 и приложимите български изисквания. Участието трябва да е доброволно и решението може да бъде оттеглено без отрицателни последствия. Описвайте само реално приложимите процедури, рискове, проби, получатели на данни, трансфери, срокове, разходи и възстановявания. За личните данни прилагайте GDPR и Закона за защита на личните данни и не представяйте автоматично съгласието за участие като единствено правно основание. Незадължителното бъдещо или вторично използване извън основното изпитване се урежда с отделния шаблон Secondary and Future Use of Data or Biosamples. Използвайте за родителите/настойника/попечителя на участник под 18 години. Детето, а не родителят, е участникът: всички лечения, рискове, проби, данни и възможни ползи се отнасят до него. За малолетно лице под 14 години писменото информирано съгласие се дава от двамата родители или приложимия настойник/настойници. За непълнолетен на 14–17 години е необходимо собственото писмено информирано съгласие на непълнолетния плюс съгласието на двамата родители или попечителя; при законово приложимото изключение за един родител документирайте основанието. Детето трябва да получи разбираема, възрастово подходяща информация от лекар с опит с малолетни и непълнолетни. Използвайте отделния детски/юношески документ според възрастта. Изричното желание на детето да откаже или да се оттегли трябва да бъде взето предвид. Не предлагайте стимули за родителското решение; описвайте само одобрени възстановявания, свързани с участието.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Study title, protocol number, investigational treatment and study purpose match the current protocol.
- C1.02 Treatment arms, randomisation and placebo descriptions match the protocol, and the chance of being assigned to each treatment is explained.
- C1.03 Visit schedule and participant procedures are described accurately at a level a participant can understand.
- C1.04 Invasive procedures, optional procedures and remote or digital activities match the current protocol.
- C1.05 Expected duration of participation and any follow-up period are stated and correct.
- C1.06 The approximate participant number, where it appears, is correct.
- C1.07 Foreseeable risks and inconveniences reflect the current safety information and protocol procedures, including risks to an embryo, fetus or nursing infant where relevant.
- C1.08 If direct clinical benefit is not expected, this is stated clearly rather than implied.
- C1.09 The document states that the activity is research, the participant's responsibilities, which aspects are experimental, the available alternatives with their important risks and benefits, compensation or treatment for trial-related injury, planned payment or reimbursement, expected costs, voluntariness and the right to refuse or withdraw, new information that may affect willingness to continue, and foreseeable reasons participation may end.
- C1.10 Contacts are given for trial information, participant rights and trial-related injury.
- C2.01 Every participant procedure in the PIS matches the protocol schedule of activities.
- C2.02 Optional procedures are not described as mandatory participation requirements.
- C2.03 Contraception, pregnancy testing and pregnancy follow-up wording matches the protocol.
- C2.04 Biological sample types, volumes, purposes, storage and future-use statements match the protocol and sample plan.
- C2.05 Reimbursement and payment descriptions agree with the financial-arrangements documents (BG-P1, BG-P2).
- C2.06 Trial-related injury and insurance wording agrees with the Bulgaria insurance documents (BG-O1).
- C2.07 Withdrawal language does not promise destruction of data or samples where the protocol or applicable law provides otherwise.
- C2.08 Local and emergency contacts are current and suitable for Bulgaria.
- C3.01 The standard adult pathway requires written informed consent before enrolment, unless the protocol lawfully uses the Article 35 emergency exception.
- C3.02 The participant or legally designated representative and the person conducting the consent interview have the correct signature and date fields.
- C3.03 No witness signature is added to every ICF; witness fields appear only where the witness pathway is triggered.
- C3.04 Optional choices are distinguishable from the main decision; refusing optional future data or sample use is not treated as refusing the main study.
- C3.05 Enough time and opportunity to discuss the trial and decide is described; the participant or representative receives a copy of the signed and dated form and the written information.
- C4.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol maps to a population, populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage, and every population has its full document set (main, representative, age-adapted, re-consent and other templates that apply), each in Bulgarian with the Bulgaria English version. (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Healthy volunteers: no implied therapeutic need or expected treatment benefit; whether any direct benefit is expected is stated; confinement, fasting, dosing, sampling, restrictions and follow-up are explained; payment or reimbursement wording agrees with the Part II financial arrangements, its link to visits, time, inconvenience or expenses and the early-withdrawal rule are clear; payment is not presented as a benefit of the product. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.03 Minors under 14: written consent of both parents or the guardians; age-appropriate information given by a physician experienced with children and adolescents; the child's refusal or wish to withdraw is taken into account; the child's form is not presented as a legal consent. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.04 Minors 14 to 17: the ICF contains a signature field for the minor's own written informed consent (not only an assent line), plus fields for both parents or the custodian; the single-parent exception is identifiable and limited to the Article 97 cases. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.05 Minors: re-consent at 18 is planned for participants who can reach 18 during the trial, and a participant reaching 14 gives their own written consent before continuing; no single generic minor form hides which signatures apply to which age group. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.06 Adults who cannot legally consent: legal representative document (Article 96, or court-appointed person under Article 162(3) of the Health Act), participant information adapted to the person's understanding, participant signature and date where able, objection or dissent wording, and re-consent if capacity returns; incapacity not inferred from a diagnosis alone. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.07 Emergency enrolment: the Article 35 conditions are reflected; consent sought without undue delay after the first intervention; what has already happened is separated from what continues only with consent; the right to object to use of data obtained is stated; no retrospective consent date. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.08 Participant or legal representative who cannot read, or participant who cannot write: an independent witness pathway with a witness attestation stating what the witness confirms (present, information read and explained, decision freely given), the interviewer's signature kept, and the alternative means of recording described.
