Template supplied for Greece, in Greek: **Έντυπο Συναίνεσης μετά από Ενημέρωση με Αμερόληπτο Μάρτυρα/Μάρτυρες** (Subject Information and Consent Form - Impartial Witness).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Διατηρήστε το σώμα του ικανού ενήλικου ασθενούς: ο συμμετέχων αποφασίζει προσωπικά και το έντυπο εξακολουθεί να τον προσφωνεί άμεσα. Ο μάρτυρας δεν αντικαθιστά τον συμμετέχοντα ούτε λειτουργεί ως νομίμως ορισμένος εκπρόσωπος. Όταν ο συμμετέχων είναι κωφός ή άλαλος, η ενημέρωση και οι ερωτήσεις/απαντήσεις γίνονται εγγράφως και παρίσταται καθ’ όλη τη διαδικασία τουλάχιστον ένας μάρτυρας, ικανός προς δικαιοπραξία και μη συνδεόμενος με τη δοκιμή. Όταν ο συμμετέχων αδυνατεί να γράψει ή να διαβάσει, χρησιμοποιούνται δύο τέτοιοι μάρτυρες. Εναλλακτικά, στις παραπάνω περιπτώσεις, η συναίνεση μπορεί να καταγράφεται με κατάλληλο εναλλακτικό μέσο, όπως ηχητική εγγραφή ή βιντεοσκόπηση, παρουσία τουλάχιστον ενός τέτοιου μάρτυρα, ο οποίος υπογράφει τη συναίνεση. Μην ενσωματώνετε νομίμως ορισμένο εκπρόσωπο. Εφόσον και μόνο εφόσον το συγκεκριμένο πρωτόκολλο περιλαμβάνει πραγματικά προαιρετική διαδικασία εντός της κύριας δοκιμής (π.χ. προαιρετική γενετική/γονιδιακή ανάλυση, φαρμακοκινητική εξέταση, πρόσθετη λήψη δείγματος ή άλλη προαιρετική εξέταση), η διαδικασία πρέπει να περιγράφεται πλήρως και κατανοητά στο παρόν έντυπο, με σκοπό, τι ακριβώς θα γίνει, χρόνο/συχνότητα ή ποσότητα, σχετικούς κινδύνους, χειρισμό δεδομένων/δειγμάτων και επιστροφή ευρημάτων όπου εφαρμόζεται. Η προαιρετική απόφαση καταγράφεται με ξεχωριστή χρονολογημένη υπογραφή του προσώπου που συναινεί και του Ερευνητή· μην χρησιμοποιείτε απλό κουτάκι ΝΑΙ/ΟΧΙ ή μονογραφή ως υποκατάστατο υπογραφής. Διαγράψτε ολόκληρο το προαιρετικό τμήμα όταν δεν υπάρχει τέτοια διαδικασία.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Exact trial title, sponsor, EU trial number, protocol code and current protocol version are confirmed.
- C1.02 Correct Greek site, department, principal investigator and contact information.
- C1.03 Purpose and design stated in plain Greek without overstating efficacy or expected benefit.
- C1.04 Every treatment arm, randomisation, placebo or comparator and assignment probability described accurately.
- C1.05 Participant duration, visits, procedures and follow-up that change burden or risk are included.
- C1.06 Available alternatives outside the trial are explained.
- C1.07 Trial treatment distinguished from standard care; any post-trial treatment arrangements identified.
- C2.01 Inclusion-relevant facts such as age, reproductive status and disease population match the protocol.
- C2.02 Doses, administration routes, visit schedules, invasive procedures and sample volumes match the protocol.
- C2.03 Risks reconciled with the current protocol, Investigator's Brochure and safety information.
- C2.04 Contraception methods, duration and pregnancy-testing requirements match the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C2.05 Optional substudies are optional in both the protocol and the consent package.
- C2.06 Participant-facing endpoint questionnaires handled under Part I are not misplaced in Part II or omitted from the scientific dossier. (applies when: endpoint_documents.participant_facing)
- C3.01 Voluntary participation and withdrawal are clear.
- C3.02 Adequate space for questions and decision time.
- C3.03 Main-trial consent is separate from optional future research, genetic testing or long-term sample storage.
- C3.04 What happens to data and samples after withdrawal is explained without promising actions that conflict with law or scientific integrity.
- C3.05 States when new information may lead to re-consent.
- C3.06 Confirms that the person receives a signed copy after consent.
- C3.07 The approved process matches Greece's in-person paper-consent guidance unless a different approach has been expressly accepted for the trial.
- C3.08 Each genuinely separable optional activity has its own clear choice recorded with a separate dated signature of the person consenting and the investigator (no YES/NO box or initials); refusal does not affect the main trial, treatment, ordinary care or reimbursement. (applies when: optional activity planned)
- C4.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol maps to a population, populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage, and every population has its full document set (see requirements.json population_sets). (applies when: always)
- C4.02 The correct adult form is prepared for the ordinary pathway. (applies when: population.adult_patients)
- C4.03 Healthy volunteers: disease-treatment assumptions removed and the absence of therapeutic intent explained. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.04 Minors: age-appropriate information, written assent from age 10, and both-parent or guardian signatures. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.05 Minors: protocol, CTIS age ranges and every document agree; the 10th and 18th birthday transitions are operationally defined; the form does not treat parental permission as a substitute for the child's information; refusal, dissent and withdrawal described without pressure. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.06 Adults under court-appointed support: the participant's own information and expression of will plus the judicial supporter's consent. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.07 Emergency enrolment: Article 35 eligibility documented and the prompt later-consent route described; participant and emergency-representative forms present. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.08 Participants who cannot use the ordinary written route: the correct witness fields and procedure (one witness for deaf or unable to speak, two for unable to read or write, audio/video with one witness where approved). (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.09 Pregnancy follow-up: the pregnant person's permission is separate from later infant-data permission. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up or pregnancy.infant_follow_up)
- C4.10 Optional future use: separate choices and withdrawal language. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C5.01 Controller identified with contact details.
- C5.02 Data Protection Officer contact of the sponsor or applicable controller provided.
- C5.03 Each processing purpose and legal basis stated accurately.
- C5.04 Recipients or categories of recipients identified.
- C5.05 Transfers outside the European Economic Area and the safeguards used explained. (applies when: data transferred outside the EEA)
- C5.06 Retention period or the criteria used to set it stated.
- C5.07 Applicable access, correction, restriction, objection and complaint rights explained.
- C5.08 The Hellenic Data Protection Authority named as the national complaint authority.
- C5.09 Automated decision-making or profiling explained. (applies when: automated decision-making or profiling applies)
- C5.10 Privacy wording consistent with the sponsor's CTIS GDPR compliance statement (GR-R1).
- C6.01 Sample type, volume, collection schedule, storage location and planned retention described.
- C6.02 States whether samples are required for the main trial or optional.
- C6.03 Optional future research separated from main-study consent. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.04 Scientific scope of future use and any transfer or sharing defined. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.05 Coding, who can re-identify the sample, and when destruction or anonymisation is possible explained.
- C6.06 The Greek consent aligns with the CTIS biological-sample compliance template (GR-S1).
- C7.01 Every participant-facing version is in Greek.
- C7.02 A valid or certified translation exists for any expected participant who does not understand Greek. (applies when: population.non_greek_speakers)
- C7.03 No participant or public or private insurer is charged for trial-specific products, tests or procedures.
- C7.04 Insurance and injury-compensation information matches the certificate (GR-O1), including the pregnancy clause.
- C7.05 The document allows direct record access by authorised sponsor representatives and competent authorities while protecting confidentiality.
- C7.06 The National Ethics Committee contact is used correctly for ethics questions and is not described as the complaints authority.
- C7.07 Prohibited participant groups under the Greek implementing decision (compulsory military service, custodial sentence, barred by court decision, living in institutions) have not been included.
- C8.01 The blank submission form contains complete date fields for every signer.
- C8.02 The participant and investigator or authorised team member fields are present.
- C8.03 Both-parent fields appear for minors, subject to the documented national exceptions. (applies when: population.minors)
- C8.04 Legal-representative fields appear only for an applicable pathway.
- C8.05 One-witness or two-witness fields match the planned Greek procedure. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C8.06 Pregnant-partner follow-up forms contain the nationally requested representative and two-witness fields. (applies when: pregnancy.partner_follow_up)
- C8.07 Each page shows document type, protocol code or acronym, version and date.
- C8.08 Pagination is continuous and shows the total page count.
- C8.09 Clean and tracked-change versions are prepared for a modification. (applies when: modification or revised document)
- C8.10 CTIS publication versions have been checked for personal data and confidential information.
- C8.11 No drafting instruction remains in the final version (italic notes, [bracket] notes, ........... gaps, yellow [INSERT/IF/CHOOSE/NOTE] labels); fields completed by hand at consent stay blank; no participant, representative, witness or investigator signature in the blank CTIS copy.
- C8.12 Correct CTIS section and title per requirements.json (CTCG naming guide, _EL_el).
- C9.01 Plain, non-promotional Greek.
- C9.02 Abbreviations and medical terms explained.
- C9.03 Short sections, readable type and enough white space.
- C9.04 Risks presented by likelihood and seriousness where the evidence supports it.
- C9.05 Consent statements kept close to the signature page.
- C9.06 The final PDF is searchable, selectable and correctly rendered.
- C9.07 Translations reviewed by a qualified person and matching the approved source. (applies when: document is adapted from a reference document in another language)
