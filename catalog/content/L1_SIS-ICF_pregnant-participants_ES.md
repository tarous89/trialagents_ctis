Template supplied for Spain, in Spanish: **Hoja de información y consentimiento para el seguimiento del embarazo y del bebé** (Pregnancy Follow-up and Consent - Participants).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Prepare a separate, focused Pregnancy Follow-up Information and Consent Form for a trial participant who becomes pregnant during the trial or within the protocol-defined exposure period. Before use, adapt or delete every bracketed instruction, alternative, example and optional module. The final participant document must contain only the risks, consequences, procedures, data categories, sources, recipients, service providers, transfers, retention periods and consent choices that actually apply to the study. The form supplements and does not replace the original trial information and consent. Clearly separate: (1) voluntary maternal and pregnancy follow-up; (2) decisions on investigational-product interruption, discontinuation, medically necessary unblinding, medication changes and continued trial participation, which must follow the protocol and medical assessment; and (3) any post-birth infant follow-up. Use Part B only when identifiable post-birth infant-health data will be collected and obtain the applicable consent after birth, unless another arrangement has been specifically approved for the study by the competent CEIm. Include only pregnancy-specific known, suspected and unknown risks and relevant consequences of stopping or changing treatment. Define the minimum data, sources, contacts, schedule and end point required for safety evaluation; do not add examinations, samples, genetic testing or extended follow-up unless actually planned and separately explained and authorised where required. Use voluntary follow-up as the default. If a protocol-required safety assessment is indispensable for continued participation, explain the exact consequence of refusal without presenting discontinuation as a penalty. Refusal or withdrawal must not prejudice ordinary medical care. Obtain explicit authorisation before contacting named external healthcare professionals or accessing relevant external records. For infant data, adapt the parental-responsibility and signature arrangement to the applicable law and the CEIm-approved consent process; where one person signs, include only the declaration and documentation actually applicable. Keep consent declarations concise and non-repetitive. Provide pregnancy-specific GDPR and LOPDGDD information, including actual controllers, purposes and legal bases, coding and linkage with trial data, sources, recipients and direct access, international transfers only where applicable, retention, rights and lawful limitations, withdrawal, safety reporting, DPO contacts, AEPD complaint rights and the Farmaindustria Code complaint route only for an adhered sponsor.
