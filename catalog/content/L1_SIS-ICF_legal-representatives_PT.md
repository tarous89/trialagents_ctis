Template supplied for Portugal, in Portuguese: **Informação e Consentimento para Representante Legalmente Autorizado** (Trial Information and Consent Form - Legal Representative).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Dirigir todo o documento ao representante legalmente autorizado. Definir no início a pessoa representada como “participante” e distinguir sistematicamente o leitor/representante do participante. Manter a explicação clínica do ensaio tão próxima quanto possível do FCI CEIC para adultos, mas referir tratamento, riscos, benefícios, dados, amostras e consequências sempre ao participante. O representante decide sobre a autorização em nome do participante; a participação continua a ser voluntária e a recusa/retirada não pode prejudicar o participante. O participante deve receber informação adaptada à sua capacidade, ser envolvido tanto quanto possível e qualquer vontade expressa de recusar ou retirar-se deve ser respeitada. Utilizar o modelo separado de capacidade limitada quando o participante puder compreender informação adaptada e expressar vontade/objeção. Utilizar este modelo apenas quando o adulto não possa prestar consentimento esclarecido e não tenha dado nem recusado dar o seu consentimento esclarecido antes do início da incapacidade. Se recuperar capacidade para consentir, deverá ser obtido o seu próprio consentimento para continuar. Não incluir assinatura do participante nem testemunha neste modelo de representante. Utilização secundária/futura ou armazenamento opcional fora dos objetivos do protocolo deve ser tratado em documento separado.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Study title, short title, EU trial number, sponsor, site and principal investigator are correct and match CTIS.
- C1.02 Medicine name, formulation, dose, route and schedule are described consistently and at an appropriate level for participants.
- C1.03 Purpose, phase and experimental status are accurate.
- C1.04 Randomisation, blinding, placebo and comparator information is correct. (applies when: randomised, blinded, placebo or comparator design)
- C1.05 Visit frequency, treatment duration, follow-up and total participation time are correct.
- C1.06 All participant procedures, samples, questionnaires, digital tools and record reviews are described.
- C1.07 Standard-care procedures are distinguished from procedures that occur only because of the trial.
- C1.08 Alternatives to participation are described without steering the decision.
- C2.01 Every protocol procedure and risk maps to the relevant participant document.
- C2.02 The schedule of activities is reconciled with the visit table, diaries and appointment information.
- C2.03 Pregnancy testing, contraception, breastfeeding and donation restrictions are reconciled with the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C2.04 Sample type, volume, frequency, storage, testing and future use are reconciled with the protocol and sample plan. (applies when: samples.collected or samples.archive_used)
- C2.05 Remote visits, home nursing, direct-to-participant shipment and electronic consent are reconciled where used. (applies when: any decentralised element or consent.electronic)
- C2.06 Stopping rules, rescue treatment, safety follow-up and post-trial access are reconciled.
- C2.07 No recruitment claim overstates likely benefit or understates burden. (applies when: recruitment.material_used)
- C3.01 Main-trial consent is separate from optional future research, extra sampling, genetic testing and biobanking.
- C3.02 Each optional activity has its own yes and no choice.
- C3.03 The document states that refusing an optional activity does not affect main-trial participation.
- C3.04 The person signing has legal authority for that decision.
- C3.05 A pregnant partner whose data or records will be collected has a separate consent. (applies when: pregnancy.partner_follow_up)
- C3.06 An impartial witness is not treated as a legally authorised representative. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C3.07 Withdrawal is explained separately for treatment, follow-up, data and stored samples.
- C3.08 No promise of deletion of records that must be retained for trial integrity or legal compliance.
- C3.09 The main decision to participate is clear and unambiguous and the consent declaration is short, with the explanation in the information section (PT-ICF).
- C3.10 Consent is obtained before any trial-specific screening procedure; pre-screening that collects health data beyond normal recruitment is explained and permission obtained first (PT-HV).
- C4.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol maps to a population, populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage, and every population has its full Portuguese document set (main, representative, age-adapted, re-consent and other templates that apply). (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Healthy volunteers: no expected therapeutic benefit; first-exposure uncertainty; total blood volume and confinement; restrictions and washout; payment separate from expense reimbursement and prorated after withdrawal; screening-data and sample handling. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.03 Minors: representative information and consent, plus age-appropriate information and an assent pathway; a capable child under 16 is asked for assent; ages 16 and 17 assent in addition to representative consent; written assent when a participant reaches 16; own written informed consent when a participant reaches 18; refusal and dissent respected; representative authority verified. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.04 Adults unable to consent: legal-representative pathway and the EU protective conditions apply; information adapted to capacity; participant objection respected; direct written re-consent planned if capacity returns. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.05 Institutional or protective placement is addressed only under the Portuguese statutory exception (minors: treating-physician attestation; adults: coordination with the treating physician, investigator participation, CEIC approval). (applies when: population.institutional_placement)
- C4.06 Emergency enrolment: every Portuguese and EU condition stated; subsequent-consent forms for all likely pathways (participant, representative, regained capacity, minors); consent sought as soon as possible; later refusal stops prospective research. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.07 Impartial witness: the person has capacity and cannot write; reason documented without stigmatising language; alternative consent method recorded; separate witness, interviewer and participant or representative fields; interpreter identified separately. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.08 Pregnancy follow-up: pregnant participant and pregnant partner pathways separate; maternal and infant permissions distinguished; record access specific; contact does not reveal unnecessary participant information. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up)
