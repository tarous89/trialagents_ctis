Template supplied for Spain, in Spanish: **Hoja de información y consentimiento diferido en situación de urgencia** (Emergency Enrolment and Deferred Consent Form).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Use the approved Spanish adult-patient information and consent model as the anchor and preserve all ordinary, study-applicable information on purpose, research nature, treatment, procedures, risks, benefits, alternatives, costs, insurance, confidentiality, contacts, withdrawal, duration and participant numbers. Reframe the document solely for an adult already enrolled during a sudden serious or life-threatening emergency under the protocol-approved emergency procedure. State once, in concise lay language, why prior information and consent could not be obtained, that a legally designated representative could not be reached within the therapeutic window, that no prior objection was known, and that the trial was directly related to the emergency condition, offered a scientifically supported prospect of direct clinically relevant benefit, and imposed minimal additional risk and burden compared with standard care. Do not reproduce the full legal eligibility checklist or imply retrospective authorisation of the emergency intervention. Clearly distinguish what already occurred before deferred consent—including treatment, allocation, procedures, data and samples—from what will occur only if consent to continue is given. Seek information and consent without undue delay from the participant or the legally designated representative, whichever can be approached sooner; when the representative consents first, obtain the participant’s own consent as soon as capacity returns. State that refusal or withdrawal causes no disadvantage and leads to standard care; distinguish cessation of future trial activities from medically necessary safety follow-up and legally required processing. If continued consent is not provided, explain the right to object to use of data already obtained and the applicable limits without promising automatic deletion. Where circumstances permitted, record consultation with a person linked by family or factual relationship without presenting that consultation as legal consent. Apply only relevant optional modules and use separate dedicated documents for impartial-witness consent, pregnancy follow-up, or secondary use beyond the main trial. Comply with Article 35 of Regulation (EU) 536/2014, Article 7 of Real Decreto 1090/2015, the AEMPS/CEIm HIP/CI guidance, GDPR and LOPDPGDD requirements.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Study title, protocol code, sponsor and EU trial number are the same as in the application.
- C1.02 The document title clearly identifies the population or use case.
- C1.03 Site and investigator fields are correctly completed or intentionally left as controlled placeholders for site completion.
- C1.04 The introduction states that participation is offered in a research study that has received the required CEIm and AEMPS review, and invites the person to read, ask questions and consult others.
- C1.05 Study purpose, intended population, total planned participant number, treatments, treatment groups and the chance of receiving each option match the protocol; no full inclusion and exclusion list.
