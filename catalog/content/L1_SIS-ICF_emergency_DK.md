Template supplied for Denmark, in Danish: **Information om akut inklusion og efterfølgende samtykke** (Emergency Enrolment and Deferred Consent Form).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Skabelonen er til deltagere, der allerede er inkluderet i et klinisk lægemiddelforsøg i en akut situation uden forudgående samtykke efter den godkendte artikel 35-procedure. Den efterfølgende beslutning gælder fortsat deltagelse og må ikke formuleres som en retrospektiv godkendelse af den akutte intervention. Brug konsekvent ”forsøgsdeltageren” om personen i forsøget. I indledningen og signaturdelen vælges den relevante modtager: deltageren selv, værgen under relevant værgemål eller – for andre voksne uden handleevne – både nærmeste pårørende og uafhængig forsøgsværge. Samtykke skal søges hurtigst muligt og uden unødig forsinkelse fra deltageren eller den retligt udpegede repræsentant, alt efter hvem der kan tilgås først. Hvis stedfortrædende samtykke indhentes først, skal deltagerens eget samtykke indhentes, så snart handleevnen genvindes. Den endelige modtagerversion skal kun indeholde relevante signaturveje. Beskriv præcist hvad der allerede er sket og hvad fortsat deltagelse indebærer. Hvis samtykke ikke opnås, stopper nye forsøgsaktiviteter og ny dataindsamling; modtageren informeres om muligheden for at gøre indsigelse mod brug af allerede indsamlede data. Obligatoriske direkte forsøgsprøver og analyser er omfattet af samtykket, når de er fuldt beskrevet; reelt valgfrie fremadrettede aktiviteter kan have særskilte valg. Skabelonen omfatter kun hovedforsøgets direkte data- og prøveanvendelse, bortset fra den betingede danske information og fravalgsrubrik om opbevaring af overskydende biologisk materiale fra hovedforsøget til uspecificeret fremtidig forskning og kun reproduktionsrisici, forholdsregler og umiddelbare sikkerhedshandlinger. <i>[Redaktionsnote: Hvis personoplysninger eller biologisk materiale behandles i eller overføres til lande uden for EU/EØS, angiv de konkrete lande, formålet med hver behandling eller overførsel og de relevante garantier efter GDPR kapitel V. Tilføj ikke et særskilt Ja/Nej-valg alene for overførslen. Hvis overførslen kun sker som led i en reelt valgfri og uafhængig aktivitet, knyttes Ja/Nej-valget til selve aktiviteten og ikke til GDPR-overførselsmekanismen.]</i> Bevar redaktionsnoterne i denne skabelon. Alle instruktioner til skabelonbrugeren skal markeres som <i>[Redaktionsnote: ...]</i>. Redaktionsnoter, tomme alternativer og ikke-relevante moduler fjernes først fra det endelige deltagerdokument før udlevering til deltageren eller beslutningstageren.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Trial purpose and treatment groups are described accurately.
- C1.02 Investigational and comparator treatments are described in terms a participant can understand.
- C1.03 Visit schedule and participant duration are described accurately.
- C1.04 Randomisation and blinding are explained where relevant.
- C1.05 Invasive procedures, imaging, biopsies, blood draws and other burdens are described.
- C1.06 Optional procedures and substudies are presented as optional, not as part of the mandatory main trial.
- C1.07 Expected risks, common important adverse effects and material uncertainties are described.
- C1.08 Expected benefit is stated without implying benefit the protocol does not support; a clear statement when direct benefit is not expected.
- C1.09 Alternatives to participation or standard treatment are described where relevant.
- C1.10 Pregnancy prevention and follow-up procedures are described where relevant. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C1.11 Compensation, reimbursement and the insurance arrangement (Patienterstatningen or independent insurance) are described.
- C1.12 Contacts for the trial and participant rights, and how trial results will be available as required by the CTR, are given.
- C1.13 The information explains what the protocol means for a Danish participant and does not merely copy protocol language.
- C2.01 Visit number and timing match the protocol.
- C2.02 Treatment duration and follow-up period match the protocol.
- C2.03 Sample volumes and sample frequency match the protocol. (applies when: samples.collected)
- C2.04 Optional versus mandatory biopsies or samples match the protocol. (applies when: samples.collected)
- C2.05 Contraception periods match the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C2.06 Record-based follow-up after treatment or withdrawal matches the protocol. (applies when: data.record_follow_up_after_withdrawal)
- C2.07 Countries and laboratories receiving data or material match the protocol.
- C2.08 Retention periods match the protocol and are consistent across documents.
- C2.09 Endpoints or analyses that can generate potentially significant findings are identified consistently with the protocol. (applies when: samples.significant_health_findings_likely)
- C2.10 No participant-facing statement changes a scientific or operational fact rather than explaining it.
- C2.11 After a protocol change, every affected participant document has been reopened, not only the main adult form. (applies when: modification or revised protocol)
- C3.01 The consent procedure states that oral information is provided by a duly qualified physician with Danish authorisation who has appropriate clinical-trial experience, GCP knowledge and knowledge of the specific trial.
- C3.02 The procedure normally names the interviewing physician as the person receiving consent; where consent follows a reflection period, another qualified, delegated physician may receive it, answer questions and attest separately, with a separate attestation block in the ICF. (applies when: consent.delegated_consent_physician)
- C3.03 Where a physician other than the principal investigator performs the process, a written delegation agreement with the principal investigator is confirmed. (applies when: consent.delegated_consent_physician)
- C3.04 The blank ICF submitted in CTIS is not pre-signed.
- C3.05 At consent the participant or legally designated representative and the interviewing physician sign and date; the witness pathway applies where the person cannot write.
- C3.06 The participant or representative receives a copy of the document by which consent was given; adequate time to decide; understanding verified during the interview.
- C3.07 The person signing has legal authority for the decision (participant, holder or holders of parental authority, nearest relative plus trial guardian, or a guardian whose guardianship covers research consent).
- C3.08 The consent form matches the participant category; the competent-adult form is not used for a legal-representative pathway.
- C3.09 The ethics consent to participation is not presented as GDPR consent; the main ICF does not regulate ordinary data-law matters; any distinct GDPR consent is clearly separated.
- C3.10 Re-consent triggers are defined: reaching 18, turning 15, regained capacity, subsequent consent after emergency inclusion, new information.
- C3.11 The consent form does not introduce information absent from the information sheet.
- C4.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol maps to a population, populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage, and every population has its full Danish document set (SIS and ICF, representative, age-adapted, re-consent and other templates that apply). (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Healthy volunteers: absence of therapeutic benefit, burdens (fasting, confinement, repeated sampling and other restrictions), first-in-human or dose-escalation uncertainty and compensation are clear. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.03 Minors: age-adapted information, consent from the holder or holders of parental authority (both when joint), the dedicated pathway for 15-17-year-olds with own consent together with parental consent, dissent handling and re-consent at 18. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.04 Minors 12-14 and 15-17 are separate documents (route A and route B of the adolescent template); no combined 12-17 document. (applies when: population.minors_age_bands includes ages 12-17)
- C4.05 Adults without capacity: consent from the nearest relative plus the trial guardian (both written), or from a legal guardian whose mandate includes research; the two roles distinct; trial guardian independence; adapted participant information; objection respected; own consent when capacity returns. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.06 Emergency inclusion: Article 35 conditions and the subsequent consent process, representatives per participant category, what happens if consent is refused or cannot be obtained, and the right to object to use of already collected data. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.07 Unable to write: alternative documentation method and impartial-witness procedure; not confused with incapacity or emergency inclusion. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.08 Pregnancy follow-up: a pregnant participant is distinguished from a pregnant partner whose data are collected; data-only partner material is not uploaded as if VMK-approved, or carries the three VMK statements. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up)
- C4.09 Secondary or future use: optional substudy consent, Article 28(2) secondary data use and unspecified future sample use are separated correctly. (applies when: samples.optional_substudy or data.future_use_outside_protocol or samples.surplus_stored_for_future_research or samples.extra_collected_for_future_research)
- C5.01 The sponsor declaration (R1) states compliance with the GDPR and the Danish Data Protection Act.
- C5.02 Privacy text is understandable, presents data-subject rights, and does not present trial consent under the CTR as the legal basis for all personal-data processing.
- C5.03 Processing or transfers outside Denmark: the purpose is explained. (applies when: data.transfer_outside_denmark)
- C5.04 Transfers outside the EU/EEA: the countries are identified and the Chapter V GDPR safeguards explained at the level required by the current Danish guidance; no yes/no choice only for the transfer. (applies when: data.transfer_outside_eea)
- C5.05 A separate consent for secondary use of data outside the protocol states that it can be withdrawn and is kept separate from the main trial ICF. (applies when: data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C5.06 Controller and data-protection contact identified; complaint route to Datatilsynet.
- C5.07 No promise of anonymity where coded data are used, and no promise of deletion that conflicts with safety, reliability or legal retention duties.
- C6.01 For each sample type: type, number or volume, purpose, analysis site, storage site, storage period and destruction or other disposition.
- C6.02 If a sample leaves Denmark, the participant information identifies where it goes; for a third country, the data-protection implications are addressed.
- C6.03 Significant health findings reasonably likely: the PIS explains the possible findings and feedback process, and a participant who chooses not to receive qualifying findings uses the separate right-not-to-know form. (applies when: samples.significant_health_findings_likely)
- C6.04 Optional substudy integrated in the trial: mentioned in the main PIS and has its own PIS and ICF in Part II. (applies when: samples.optional_substudy)
- C6.05 Surplus trial material for unspecified future research: the main PIS explains storage, whether it is optional, duration and the Danish rules (ethics review and new consent as default in Denmark; foreign rules if abroad); only the VMK opt-out checkbox appears on the main ICF. (applies when: samples.surplus_stored_for_future_research)
- C6.06 Extra material collected specifically for unspecified future research: its information and consent are omitted from Part II, or if submitted state that they are not approved by VMK. (applies when: samples.extra_collected_for_future_research)
- C6.07 Archived material used as part of the trial: the participant is informed that it is used. (applies when: samples.archive_used)
- C6.08 Withdrawal effects on material are stated without promising destruction that conflicts with material already used, legal retention or a later lawful project.
- C6.09 Consistent with the S1 biological-samples form and R1.
- C7.01 The normal participant package is in Danish and written for a lay reader.
- C7.02 Participants or representatives who do not understand Danish: both the PIS and the ICF have a validated written translation, and the consent procedure explains how the oral conversation is supported in that language. (applies when: population.non_danish_speakers)
- C7.03 Patient-record screening before consent: the recruitment procedure identifies the concrete information, purpose, approximate number of records, time period, Danish legal basis, and the contact-permission rule (treating healthcare professional, fallback site management). (applies when: recruitment.patient_records_screening)
- C7.04 Post-consent record access: the PIS explains access by sponsor, sponsor representatives and investigator for trial conduct, quality control and monitoring, and Danish Medicines Agency inspection access (and foreign medicines authorities where relevant).
- C7.05 Record-based follow-up after withdrawal: the PIS and protocol explain scope, purpose and conditions consistently. (applies when: data.record_follow_up_after_withdrawal)
- C7.06 Insurance wording describes Patienterstatningen or the independent arrangement correctly and in participant-friendly terms, matching O1.
- C7.07 Recruitment material is the version actually used, submitted as presented in CTIS; audio or video as a scene-by-scene storyboard. (applies when: recruitment.material_used)
- C7.08 The current Danish information 'Dine rettigheder som forsøgsperson i forsøg med medicin' is enclosed or referred to as the templates instruct.
- C8.01 Document title matches the actual population and use case.
- C8.02 Version number and date are consistent across the PIS and the ICF.
- C8.03 No participant or representative signature appears on blank submission documents.
- C8.04 The correct VMK CTR standard form is used where the sponsor has chosen to follow the national standard, with its standard text fields unchanged.
- C8.05 No legacy non-CTR consent form has been substituted for the CTR form.
- C8.06 Optional checkboxes do not create consent logic that VMK does not accept (only the surplus-material opt-out on the main ICF; separate forms for substudies and secondary data use).
- C8.07 All internal references to protocol sections, appendices and contact details are correct.
- C8.08 The CTIS filename is clear and does not rely on date or version information that conflicts with the CTIS structured fields; correct CTIS section and naming convention.
- C8.09 Separate signature blocks for each signer; two parental-authority blocks where authority is joint; nearest relative and trial guardian blocks distinct; witness fields only where the witness route can apply.
- C8.10 Running footer with document name, population, version, date, protocol number and EU trial number.
- C8.11 No drafting instruction remains in the final version (<i>[Redaktionsnote: …]</i>, other [bracket] alternatives and gaps such as [indsæt], legacy FORMTEXT prompts, non-selected routes, yellow [INSERT/IF/CHOOSE/NOTE] labels); fields completed by hand at consent stay blank.
- C9.01 Headings, sentence length, terminology and risk descriptions are understandable without medical training.
- C9.02 Unavoidable clinical terms are defined when first used.
- C9.03 Optional choices look optional and stand alone.
- C9.04 Child, adolescent and limited-capacity documents are age- or capacity-adapted and clearly shorter than the parents' or representative's information. (applies when: population.minors or population.adults_without_capacity)
- C9.05 Translations reviewed by a qualified person and matching the approved source. (applies when: document is adapted from a reference document in another language)
- C10.01 A Danish reader can understand what will happen; the consent pathway matches Danish law; every protocol burden is represented; translations are controlled; record and privacy wording is complete; every optional or population-specific pathway has the correct document set.
- C10.02 The participant document list matches the protocol's populations and the Denmark Part II package checklist.
