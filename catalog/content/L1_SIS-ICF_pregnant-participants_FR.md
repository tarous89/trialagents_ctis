Template supplied for France, in French: **Document d’information et formulaire de consentement au suivi de grossesse - participante enceinte** (Pregnancy Follow-up and Consent - Participants).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Prepare a separate French pregnancy-follow-up information and consent form for a trial participant who becomes pregnant. Focus on pregnancy safety follow-up, relevant exposure, treatment consequences and the specific data collected. This consent does not authorise continued investigational exposure; any continuation during pregnancy must independently satisfy the protocol and the French protections for pregnant women, including Article L.1121-5. Explain required interruption, unblinding, medication changes or safety monitoring. Collect only necessary maternal, pregnancy-outcome and, where separately authorised, infant data. Integrate the applicable French data-protection information. Post-birth identifiable child follow-up requires separate or post-birth confirmed consent by the person or persons with parental authority.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Trial title, short title, protocol number, EU trial number, sponsor and investigational treatment are correct and consistent.
- C1.02 States why the person is invited and whether the cohort is a patient or healthy-volunteer cohort.
- C1.03 Randomisation, blinding, placebo and treatment allocation are explained in language that matches the protocol.
- C1.04 Visit schedule, treatment period, follow-up, tests, procedures, devices and remote contacts match the latest protocol.
- C1.05 Routine care is distinguished from research-only procedures.
- C1.06 Benefits are not promised or overstated.
- C1.07 Foreseeable treatment, control, procedural and reproductive risks are covered without copying a technical safety table into the participant document.
- C1.08 Realistic alternatives to participation and planned care after the trial are described.
- C1.09 Contact routes work for routine questions, urgent safety matters, participant rights and privacy issues.
- C2.01 Every eligibility-sensitive restriction participants must follow is stated as in the protocol, including medicines, food, activity, contraception and donation restrictions.
- C2.02 Number and timing of blood draws, biopsies, imaging procedures, questionnaires and home activities match the protocol.
- C2.03 Sample types, analyses, storage, transfers, retention, optional future use and destruction are reconciled across the protocol, laboratory manual, consent forms and the biological-sample compliance template (FR-S1). (applies when: samples.collected or samples.archive_used)
- C2.04 Data sources, recipients, transfers and retention are reconciled across the protocol, data-management material, MR-001 analysis and the participant notice.
- C2.05 Expense reimbursement and constraint indemnity match the EU compensation form (FR-P1) and the Additional France application document (FR-K-ADDITIONAL).
- C2.06 The insurance description identifies the correct France route and does not conflict with the certificate (FR-O1).
- C2.07 Pregnancy reporting and follow-up are described consistently in the protocol and participant documents. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements or pregnancy follow-up)
- C3.01 Main participation is separate from optional substudies, future research and any other choice that may be refused without losing trial access.
- C3.02 A clear yes and no choice is provided where an optional activity requires a recorded decision.
- C3.03 Genetic testing is not bundled into a general signature: separate information and express written prior consent for a genetic examination (FR-L1-GENETIC). (applies when: genetics.examination_planned)
- C3.04 The recruitment and informed consent procedure (FR-K1) identifies who informs, who consents, when consent occurs and how understanding is assessed.
- C3.05 The participant has adequate time to decide, except where a legally valid emergency route applies.
- C3.06 Withdrawal effects are explained separately for trial procedures, new data collection, existing data and stored samples.
- C3.07 The participant receives a signed copy of the consent record.
- C3.08 Electronic or remote consent methods meet the same information, identity, signature, copy and record requirements and are described in the submitted procedure. (applies when: consent.electronic)
- C4.01 Every cohort and consent pathway in the protocol maps to a population with an information version, consent form and recruitment route; populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage and each has its full document set. (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Healthy volunteers: non-therapeutic purpose, exclusion period, payment, restrictions and national-register information. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.03 Minors: age-adapted information, parent authorisation, personal adherence, refusal and re-consent at adulthood. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.04 Limited-capacity adults: the exact French protective status is verified before selecting a legal representative or assisted-consent (curatorship) route. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity or population.adults_under_curatorship)
- C4.05 Emergency enrolment: every Article 35 condition is addressed and later consent is sought without undue delay. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.06 Capable participant who cannot write: the correct witnessed process and attestation are included. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.07 Pregnancy follow-up: a pregnant participant is separated from a pregnant partner who is a new data subject. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up)
- C4.08 Optional future data or sample use: separate choices, transfers, genetics and withdrawal are checked. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C5.01 Controller, any EU representative and the data-protection officer or privacy contact are identified.
- C5.02 Processing purposes, data categories, sources, recipients, retention and participant rights are stated in clear French.
- C5.03 Direct collection under GDPR Article 13 is distinguished from collection through another person or record source under Article 14.
- C5.04 The selected legal bases are confirmed with the privacy team; trial consent is not equated with the GDPR legal basis by default.
- C5.05 The countries receiving data, the recipients or recipient categories, each transfer purpose and the adequacy status are listed as the France guidance expects. (applies when: data.transfer_outside_eea)
- C5.06 Rights to access, rectify, restrict or object and the right to complain to CNIL are stated, subject to lawful research limitations.
- C5.07 What happens to previously collected data after withdrawal is explained without promising deletion that law or scientific-integrity duties do not permit.
- C5.08 The notice matches the MR-001 declaration or CNIL authorisation route stated in the Additional France application document (FR-R1, FR-K-ADDITIONAL).
- C5.09 Privacy text is checked in recruitment websites, screeners, apps, diaries and pregnancy follow-up forms, not only in the main consent document.
- C6.01 Every sample, collection point, quantity or frequency and analysis is identified.
- C6.02 Storage location, access, coded status, retention, transfers and end-of-storage action are stated.
- C6.03 Protocol-defined analyses are separated from optional future research.
- C6.04 Any future purpose is specific enough to support an informed choice. (applies when: samples.future_use_outside_protocol or data.future_use_outside_protocol)
- C6.05 A changed sample purpose triggers a new-consent assessment before use.
- C6.06 The separate French genetic-testing information and consent form is prepared where a genetic examination is planned. (applies when: genetics.examination_planned)
- C6.07 The main form, optional form and EU biological-sample compliance template use the same choices and terminology.
- C7.01 All participant-facing documents for France are in French.
- C7.02 The main information is clear, concise, fair and not so long that it impairs understanding.
- C7.03 The document identifies the CPP opinion and ANSM authorisation as required by French law.
- C7.04 The information explains the right to personal health information and access to overall research results.
- C7.05 Any constraint indemnity aligns with French restrictions and the EUR 6,000 cap over twelve consecutive months. (applies when: payment.constraint_indemnity)
- C7.06 The data-transfer disclosure is exhaustive enough for the national Part II guidance.
- C7.07 The separate genetics route is used where applicable. (applies when: genetics.examination_planned)
- C7.08 The recruitment procedure uses the harmonised version required on the intended submission date (version 2.0 from 1 September 2026).
- C8.01 Each submitted file has a unique, consistent title, version and date.
- C8.02 The information-document version and date are on the consent page.
- C8.03 Signature and date fields are present for the participant and the person conducting the consent interview.
- C8.04 Parent, representative, curator, witness or separate data-subject fields are added only where the applicable route requires them.
- C8.05 Each role is labelled in French and it is clear whether a person signs for authorisation, assistance, attestation or consent.
- C8.06 Unsigned templates are uploaded at application; approved versions are signed during conduct.
- C8.07 Headers, footers, page numbers, blank pages, tracked changes, comments and hidden text are checked.
- C8.08 Filenames are intelligible in CTIS and PDFs contain searchable text.
- C8.09 Local contact placeholders are reconciled before site use through the approved document-control process.
- C8.10 Correct CTIS section and title per the naming guide and requirements.json (filename base without ( ) / , . ; | [ ]).
- C8.11 No drafting instruction remains in the final version (italic notes, [bracket] notes, ........... gaps, yellow [INSERT/IF/OTHERWISE/CHOOSE/NOTE] labels); fields completed by hand at consent stay blank.
- C9.01 Plain French appropriate to the intended age, condition and health literacy.
- C9.02 Medical and regulatory terms are defined at first use.
- C9.03 Sentences and paragraphs are short and headings help a participant find an answer.
- C9.04 Legible type, contrast, spacing and page layout.
- C9.05 Diagrams and icons are explained in words where needed for accessibility.
- C9.06 No coercive tone, sponsor marketing language or statements that could waive legal rights.
- C9.07 An independent French reviewer has checked meaning, not only grammar.
- C9.08 Digital screens are tested on the devices participants will use and keep the CPP-reviewed content and flow. (applies when: digital screens or electronic materials are used)
- C10.01 No protocol fact, consent giver, optional choice, data transfer, sample purpose, payment or signature block is unresolved; each resolution is recorded and the affected checks rerun.
