Template supplied for France, in French: **Document d’information et formulaire de consentement différé après inclusion en situation d’urgence** (Emergency Enrolment and Deferred Consent Form).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Adapt the document to the specific research, RIPH category, investigational product or device and participant population. Ensure consistency with the protocol without copying scientific protocol language. Use clear and understandable French, short sentences, active voice, non-technical terminology and consistent wording for each concept. Define abbreviations at first use and, where needed, in the glossary. Do not use the expression « tomber enceinte ». Clearly distinguish standard care from research-specific treatments, procedures, risks and constraints. Structure the information in three parts: information about the research; participant rights and management of collected data; and a glossary where useful. The version submitted to the Comité de protection des personnes must correspond to the version presented to participants; provide access links for electronic information or consent materials. Adapt and complete the participant-rights and data-protection wording with the controller and DPO details, legal basis, purposes, data categories, recipients, confidentiality measures, transfers, retention period, participant rights, CNIL complaint route, and secondary-use or SNDS information where applicable. Include specific prior written consent for genetic examinations and separate consent for optional studies. Include consent for sharing strictly necessary information with the treating physician where applicable. Prepare separate adapted documents for minors, protected adults and participants unable to consent where applicable. Reframe the adult-patient template for a participant already enrolled under the protocol-approved French emergency pathway. Under Article L.1122-1-3, if family members or the trusted person were present, their consent must have been sought; a derogation is possible only for an immediate life-threatening emergency assessed by the CPP. Inform the participant, family or trusted person as soon as possible and seek consent for possible continuation. Clearly distinguish procedures, data and samples already obtained from future activities. Do not imply retrospective authorisation of the emergency intervention. If continued consent is refused, stop future study-specific activities, provide appropriate care and explain the right to object to use of already obtained data subject to legal limits.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Trial title, short title, protocol number, EU trial number, sponsor and investigational treatment are correct and consistent.
- C1.02 States why the person is invited and whether the cohort is a patient or healthy-volunteer cohort.
- C1.03 Randomisation, blinding, placebo and treatment allocation are explained in language that matches the protocol.
- C1.04 Visit schedule, treatment period, follow-up, tests, procedures, devices and remote contacts match the latest protocol.
- C1.05 Routine care is distinguished from research-only procedures.
- C1.06 Benefits are not promised or overstated.
- C1.07 Foreseeable treatment, control, procedural and reproductive risks are covered without copying a technical safety table into the participant document.
- C1.08 Realistic alternatives to participation and planned care after the trial are described.
- C1.09 Contact routes work for routine questions, urgent safety matters, participant rights and privacy issues.
- C2.01 Every eligibility-sensitive restriction participants must follow is stated as in the protocol, including medicines, food, activity, contraception and donation restrictions.
- C2.02 Number and timing of blood draws, biopsies, imaging procedures, questionnaires and home activities match the protocol.
- C2.03 Sample types, analyses, storage, transfers, retention, optional future use and destruction are reconciled across the protocol, laboratory manual, consent forms and the biological-sample compliance template (FR-S1). (applies when: samples.collected or samples.archive_used)
- C2.04 Data sources, recipients, transfers and retention are reconciled across the protocol, data-management material, MR-001 analysis and the participant notice.
- C2.05 Expense reimbursement and constraint indemnity match the EU compensation form (FR-P1) and the Additional France application document (FR-K-ADDITIONAL).
