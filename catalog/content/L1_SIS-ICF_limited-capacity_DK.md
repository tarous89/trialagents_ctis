Template supplied for Denmark, in Danish: **Tilpasset deltagerinformation og erklæring om tilsagn eller modstand** (Subject Information and Assent Form - Limited Capacity).

Keep the model text and its order. Fill the [bracketed] notes and the ........... gaps from the study; follow the italic and bracketed instructions and remove them from the final document. Fields completed by hand at the consent visit (names, dates, signatures) stay blank.

**Content instructions:** Brug kun til en voksen forsøgsdeltager, som ikke kan afgive juridisk gyldigt informeret samtykke, men som kan forstå enkel information eller udtrykke en mening med ord, tegn, hjælpemidler, kropssprog eller adfærd. Dokumentet skal altid ledsage den separate danske information og samtykkeerklæring til den relevante stedfortræder. Den juridiske samtykkevej må ikke gentages her: ved værgemål kan værgen samtykke, hvis værgemålet omfatter forsøg; for øvrige voksne uden handleevne kræves samtykke fra nærmeste pårørende og en uafhængig forsøgsværge. Henvend informationen direkte til deltageren og tilpas ord, billeder og kommunikationsstøtte til personens forståelse. Deltagerens aktuelle tilkendegivelser skal tillægges betydning, og enhver tydelig mundtlig eller ikke-mundtlig protest mod deltagelse eller en konkret procedure skal respekteres, også under forsøget. Registrér deltagernes vilje, usikkerhed eller manglende mulighed for at udtrykke sig; præsenter ikke erklæringen som juridisk samtykke. Beskriv kun hovedforsøgets nødvendige information, obligatoriske og direkte forsøgsrelaterede valgfrie prøver/tests, risici, alternativer og data i enkel form. Medtag ikke sekundær/fremtidig brug, graviditetsopfølgning, stedfortræderens underskrift, upartisk vidne eller akut inklusion. Hvis deltageren genvinder handleevnen, skal eget skriftligt informeret samtykke indhentes med voksenskabelonen før fortsat deltagelse. <i>[Redaktionsnote: Hvis personoplysninger eller biologisk materiale behandles i eller overføres til lande uden for EU/EØS, angiv de konkrete lande, formålet med hver behandling eller overførsel og de relevante garantier efter GDPR kapitel V. Tilføj ikke et særskilt Ja/Nej-valg alene for overførslen. Hvis overførslen kun sker som led i en reelt valgfri og uafhængig aktivitet, knyttes Ja/Nej-valget til selve aktiviteten og ikke til GDPR-overførselsmekanismen.]</i> Bevar redaktionsnoterne i denne skabelon. Alle instruktioner til skabelonbrugeren skal markeres som <i>[Redaktionsnote: ...]</i>. Redaktionsnoter, tomme alternativer og ikke-relevante moduler fjernes først fra det endelige deltagerdokument før udlevering til deltageren eller beslutningstageren.

## Checklist
- C1.01 Trial purpose and treatment groups are described accurately.
- C1.02 Investigational and comparator treatments are described in terms a participant can understand.
- C1.03 Visit schedule and participant duration are described accurately.
- C1.04 Randomisation and blinding are explained where relevant.
- C1.05 Invasive procedures, imaging, biopsies, blood draws and other burdens are described.
- C1.06 Optional procedures and substudies are presented as optional, not as part of the mandatory main trial.
- C1.07 Expected risks, common important adverse effects and material uncertainties are described.
- C1.08 Expected benefit is stated without implying benefit the protocol does not support; a clear statement when direct benefit is not expected.
- C1.09 Alternatives to participation or standard treatment are described where relevant.
- C1.10 Pregnancy prevention and follow-up procedures are described where relevant. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C1.11 Compensation, reimbursement and the insurance arrangement (Patienterstatningen or independent insurance) are described.
- C1.12 Contacts for the trial and participant rights, and how trial results will be available as required by the CTR, are given.
- C1.13 The information explains what the protocol means for a Danish participant and does not merely copy protocol language.
- C2.01 Visit number and timing match the protocol.
- C2.02 Treatment duration and follow-up period match the protocol.
- C2.03 Sample volumes and sample frequency match the protocol. (applies when: samples.collected)
- C2.04 Optional versus mandatory biopsies or samples match the protocol. (applies when: samples.collected)
- C2.05 Contraception periods match the protocol. (applies when: protocol has reproductive requirements)
- C2.06 Record-based follow-up after treatment or withdrawal matches the protocol. (applies when: data.record_follow_up_after_withdrawal)
- C2.07 Countries and laboratories receiving data or material match the protocol.
- C2.08 Retention periods match the protocol and are consistent across documents.
- C2.09 Endpoints or analyses that can generate potentially significant findings are identified consistently with the protocol. (applies when: samples.significant_health_findings_likely)
- C2.10 No participant-facing statement changes a scientific or operational fact rather than explaining it.
- C2.11 After a protocol change, every affected participant document has been reopened, not only the main adult form. (applies when: modification or revised protocol)
- C3.01 The consent procedure states that oral information is provided by a duly qualified physician with Danish authorisation who has appropriate clinical-trial experience, GCP knowledge and knowledge of the specific trial.
- C3.02 The procedure normally names the interviewing physician as the person receiving consent; where consent follows a reflection period, another qualified, delegated physician may receive it, answer questions and attest separately, with a separate attestation block in the ICF. (applies when: consent.delegated_consent_physician)
- C3.03 Where a physician other than the principal investigator performs the process, a written delegation agreement with the principal investigator is confirmed. (applies when: consent.delegated_consent_physician)
- C3.04 The blank ICF submitted in CTIS is not pre-signed.
- C3.05 At consent the participant or legally designated representative and the interviewing physician sign and date; the witness pathway applies where the person cannot write.
- C3.06 The participant or representative receives a copy of the document by which consent was given; adequate time to decide; understanding verified during the interview.
- C3.07 The person signing has legal authority for the decision (participant, holder or holders of parental authority, nearest relative plus trial guardian, or a guardian whose guardianship covers research consent).
- C3.08 The consent form matches the participant category; the competent-adult form is not used for a legal-representative pathway.
- C3.09 The ethics consent to participation is not presented as GDPR consent; the main ICF does not regulate ordinary data-law matters; any distinct GDPR consent is clearly separated.
- C3.10 Re-consent triggers are defined: reaching 18, turning 15, regained capacity, subsequent consent after emergency inclusion, new information.
- C3.11 The consent form does not introduce information absent from the information sheet.
- C4.01 Every inclusion pathway in the protocol maps to a population, populations are mutually exclusive within each consent stage, and every population has its full Danish document set (SIS and ICF, representative, age-adapted, re-consent and other templates that apply). (applies when: always)
- C4.02 Healthy volunteers: absence of therapeutic benefit, burdens (fasting, confinement, repeated sampling and other restrictions), first-in-human or dose-escalation uncertainty and compensation are clear. (applies when: population.healthy_volunteers)
- C4.03 Minors: age-adapted information, consent from the holder or holders of parental authority (both when joint), the dedicated pathway for 15-17-year-olds with own consent together with parental consent, dissent handling and re-consent at 18. (applies when: population.minors)
- C4.04 Minors 12-14 and 15-17 are separate documents (route A and route B of the adolescent template); no combined 12-17 document. (applies when: population.minors_age_bands includes ages 12-17)
- C4.05 Adults without capacity: consent from the nearest relative plus the trial guardian (both written), or from a legal guardian whose mandate includes research; the two roles distinct; trial guardian independence; adapted participant information; objection respected; own consent when capacity returns. (applies when: population.adults_without_capacity)
- C4.06 Emergency inclusion: Article 35 conditions and the subsequent consent process, representatives per participant category, what happens if consent is refused or cannot be obtained, and the right to object to use of already collected data. (applies when: consent.emergency_enrolment)
- C4.07 Unable to write: alternative documentation method and impartial-witness procedure; not confused with incapacity or emergency inclusion. (applies when: consent.impartial_witness_possible)
- C4.08 Pregnancy follow-up: a pregnant participant is distinguished from a pregnant partner whose data are collected; data-only partner material is not uploaded as if VMK-approved, or carries the three VMK statements. (applies when: pregnancy.participant_follow_up_beyond_main_consent or pregnancy.partner_follow_up)
- C4.09 Secondary or future use: optional substudy consent, Article 28(2) secondary data use and unspecified future sample use are separated correctly. (applies when: samples.optional_substudy or data.future_use_outside_protocol or samples.surplus_stored_for_future_research or samples.extra_collected_for_future_research)
- C5.01 The sponsor declaration (R1) states compliance with the GDPR and the Danish Data Protection Act.
- C5.02 Privacy text is understandable, presents data-subject rights, and does not present trial consent under the CTR as the legal basis for all personal-data processing.

- C5.06 Controller and data-protection contact identified; complaint route to Datatilsynet.
- C5.07 No promise of anonymity where coded data are used, and no promise of deletion that conflicts with safety, reliability or legal retention duties.
- C6.01 For each sample type: type, number or volume, purpose, analysis site, storage site, storage period and destruction or other disposition.
